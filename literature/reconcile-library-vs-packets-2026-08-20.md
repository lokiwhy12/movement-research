## 1. What kind of roster this is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** The 23 are not her 1780–1860 utopian sample. Continuance is the closest of her three problems on most living shops, courses, gyms, fellowships, and churches; cohesion and control appear as room, class, Lodge, or weekend practices, not “specific ways of ordering and defining the existence of a group” (Kanter 1972, Ch.4 p.90). Named exceptions: occupy §1 names cohesion as the closest of the three on a 59-day encampment still short of her 25-year cut; military-boot-camp §1 is closer on all three inside a 13-week depot and still not her commune sample; freemasons §1 is closer on cohesion than a megachurch weekend; clubhouse §1 and supreme-or-harley §1 label continuance thin. Renunciation as she defined it is structurally unavailable on the modal case. A mismatch to her sample is a finding, not a pending fact.

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** The roster is a mix of pre-existing ties, stranger-member warmth, and media-first contact. It is not the 1965 millenarian convert series (full data on 15 of 21). Landmark §2 conversion Q1 and nxivm §2 conversion Q1 are the most network-weighted (official invitation / trusted-peer). Religious-seekership maps as official product language only at evangelical-megachurch §1 (“turn seekers into reproducing disciples”); elsewhere it is a mismatch. Overbalance stays a 1980-body hole on all 23.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** Packet §13 is observational, not a verdict, on every case. Harvey’s blurred boundaries — whole life entwined so leaving means leaving employer, housing, and social orbit — are not the default condition for the modal participant. Named thickenings already labeled: onetaste §1 (house/staff era, not 2026 digital-member); nxivm §1 (inner lanes, not modal ESP); scientology §1 (Sea Org exists; Melton’s sample is another paper); military-boot-camp §1 (total-institution container, time-bounded, DEP-release before shipping). Lifton’s milieu control remains a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22), asserted rather than tested. No cult verdict is licensed.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Closest roster matches: supreme-or-harley §1 (Supreme is a consumer brand under Muniz and O’Guinn 2001; partial-closer — no official club SKU; belonging is purchase-under-no-resale); apple §1 (the other brand-tribe control; Macintosh was their named sample); peloton §1 (closer than A.A. or LDS); crossfit §1 (closer than Landmark or NXIVM). Do not collapse Apple, Harley, or Peloton into Supreme. Harley 1995 full JCR stays a hole even as contrast. Consumer-brand mismatches, already labeled: aa §1, lds §1, qanon §1, occupy §1 (mismatch or very partial), scientology §1 (religion, not a branded good). 75-hard §1 and the-secret §1 sit closer to a brand public than an interacting room. Most of the rest are partial.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Twenty-three labeled Tarrow mismatches; zero matches. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022 p.195). Occupy §1 is closer than WeWork and still a mismatch unless the supplement itself showed a cycle — it did not; closed vs transformative was not run. A living shop, VIN-gated factory club, gym, or church is not promoted into that by analogy. Q5 is continuing-practice on most living orgs. Named non-default Q5 labels are kept as signed: nxivm (ended organization, limited); onetaste (ended org plus continuing successor); wework and clubhouse (both-as-layers); qanon (post-drop afterlife plus continuing-practice of the interpretation layer); crypto-tribe (protocol continuing-practice; overlay fracture-without-death); supreme-or-harley (continuing-practice for the living shop plus labeled late-stage/cooling file); occupy (ended-encampment / decayed-control for NYCGA / Zuccotti). Anderson’s five forces stay her Philadelphia rave/EDM claims.

## 2. Answers the 23 can support

Cite the filed supplement (case slug + section), not a reconstructed packet page. Keep the five lanes apart.

### Commitment

**Q1.** Continuance, cohesion, and control appear as three separable problems on the roster (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance is strongest on the commercial and church majority (§2 commitment Q1). Occupy §2 commitment Q1 names cohesion as the closest of the three. Military-boot-camp §2 commitment Q1 is the closest three-problem showing and still not her sample. Clubhouse and supreme-or-harley keep continuance thin.

**Q2.** Investment is the most widely codeable of the six: money or a date-specific seat puts a stake in the next meeting; successful groups in her sample generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96). Sacrifice is only partly codeable — hours, ordeal, tuition, tithe-frame, Crucible deprivation — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is partial: weekend, box hour, Lodge, camp, depot. Mortification is partial where confession-like practices sit in a ritual table. Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical services, pp.99–102) on the modal case. Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is not codeable as her pages. 75-hard §2 commitment Q2: investment only partly codeable (core protocol $0). Freemasons §2 and occupy §2: investment partial. Clubhouse, crypto-tribe, and supreme-or-harley: sacrifice mostly unavailable.

**Q3.** Something expensive is present on almost the whole roster. That is not yet Kanter’s psychic-expense path (sacrifice raises the expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief, p.91) and not Iannaccone’s price on outside activity (1992 body still closed). The-secret §2 commitment Q3 is the named inexpensive exception. Time 2011 at landmark §2 commitment Q3 (paid, reported benefit, declined to continue) is one path that does not show her sequence.

**Q4.** Substitution and screening stay a hole. Later Iannaccone-channel labels; 1992 body closed. Health advisories, DEP screens, and Lodge ballots are not that screening unless a supplement already said they were — they did not.

**Q5.** Club good with participatory crowding vs private good merely co-consumed: Iannaccone body closed. Official products are individual shifts, hardware, a class hour, a meeting, a ticket, a protocol. Co-consumption is visible (rooms, boxes, wards, camps); his crowding-quality claim is not licensed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices appear in ritual tables (mic shares; EM; frames; score declare; inventory; recommend interview; GA stack). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing arrangements on the modal case. Do not upgrade sharing to mortification. Freemasons §2 commitment Q6 and supreme-or-harley §2 commitment Q6 refuse the upgrade explicitly.

**Q7.** Official meaning claims are slogans and room or scripture authority, not institutionalized awe and insulated hierarchy (pp.128–138). Named examples stay the supplements’ official copy (possibility; GPP; Restoration; Ten Principles; creative-being). Slogan is the roster pattern.

**Q8.** What the roster used as an analogue of “25 years” is institutional or product span (est 1971 / Forum 1985; 1717/1813–2026; 1994–2026; 2009–2026; 2020–2026), not member-commitment duration. Occupy’s 59 days and military-boot-camp’s 13 weeks are cohort or encampment clocks, not her success cut. No unsuccessful coded case of hers exceeded 16 years (Appendix p.260). A different cut would change the reading; the supplements already said so.

**Q9.** Hall 1988 still closed. Prior-network invitation (landmark 90%; nxivm trusted-peer; freemasons proposer/seconder) could account for cohesion without the six-mechanism story. That remains an alternative, not a Hall finding.

**Q10.** Falsifiers already stated from the supplements, not invented here: three problems cannot be separated; renunciation remains unavailable and communion never leaves the weekend, box, Lodge, or shop; cost exists without her belief-justification path; a free or low-ordeal first contact plus leave-quietly is common and unpenalized beyond money already spent; 13 weeks or a Thursday ration is treated as her 25-year success.

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Mix is the roster pattern. Landmark §2 conversion Q1 and nxivm §2 conversion Q1 run mainly through pre-existing kin/friendship/elite-peer ties. The remaining majority are mixes (friend plus stranger-member plus media or locator). Scientology, aa, and lds §2 conversion Q1: mix, proportion not quantified. The-secret §2 conversion Q1 is media-weighted.

**Q2.** Overbalance cannot be operationalized. The word is secondary; 1980 AJS body closed; no member-tie / nonmember-tie ratio at joining is in any supplement.

**Q3.** Doctrinal or syllabus assent follows the first warmth on the public-copy layer (Stark 1996 belief-follows-attachment), as progressive revelation: intros sell outcomes; later days, rungs, or gates install vocabulary and duty (landmark through supreme-or-harley §2 conversion Q3). That is not a convert-by-convert timing study.

**Q4.** Of the seven 1965 conditions, affective bond is the most widely present (invitation, Specialist, first class, kitchen, recruiter, About crew). Intensive interaction is present as a weekend, class hour, Lodge night, depot day, or arena — not as daily contact that turns every verbal convert into a deployable agent. 75-hard §2 conversion Q4: intensive interaction is not present as that daily contact (self-administered loop). Religious problem-solving perspective and religious seekership mismatch on the secular majority; evangelical-megachurch §1 is closer as official seeker language and still not the 1965 series. Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted for modal participants (family-reunion framing; leave-quietly; miss the drop; DEP-at-home). Activation order vs funnel cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* remains closed.

**Q5.** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips’ partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three on almost every case that answered the Q. Necessity is not shown: no sample of entrants who lacked them. 75-hard §2 conversion Q5: affective bond more visible than intensive interaction (no room).

**Q6.** Open-network boundary, not inward closure, is the modal official design (Tuesday guests; box locator; ComeUntoChrist; App Store; 18 named Supreme stores). Crypto-tribe §2 conversion Q6 splits the objects: public protocol is open; interpreter purity tests are inward. Clubhouse §2 conversion Q6: object (A) closed then opened; friends left — not social implosion as the packet’s story.

**Q7.** Mix: organized-group invitation plus media. Weighted to invitation at landmark and nxivm; weighted to media/hashtag at 75-hard and the-secret; weighted to cultural presence plus Store or showroom at apple and peloton; Stark 1980-abstract media-occult boundary is not the claim here.

**Q8.** Entry is repeatedly conflated with retention or outcome in packet layers; the supplements already refused the collapse. Landmark 90% is entry; 94% survey is an outcome claim with methods not found; Guardian 80% is retention. Similar splits sit at aa, lds, peloton, occupy, clubhouse. Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s factors where members do not co-reside: the modal finding is that blurred boundaries are not the default Forum, box, Lodge, shop, or app condition (landmark through supreme-or-harley §2 NRM Q1). Co-residence *was* present in onetaste house/staff era and is not the 2026 digital-member default (onetaste §1). Military-boot-camp §1: total-institution container, still not Harvey default for a time-bounded pipeline with DEP-release. Occupy overnight sleepers are not Harvey default for the modal walk-in. Scientology Sea Org is Melton’s other sample, not this note’s score.

**Q2.** Lifton’s milieu control as base condition is not testable from these packets. Partial in-room or in-depot rules do not test his dependency claim; applying it would be asserted, not tested. Do not score.

**Q3.** Reintegration vs exit at roughly constant intensity: only if a supplement had both populations. Landmark Time 2011 left and graduates stay, but not two populations under roughly constant intensity from the same period (landmark §3). NXIVM leavers and retained are not a matched intensity design. The Q stays unanswered under the note’s own rule.

**Q4.** Retained and departed from the same period: not sampled as a design on this roster. Melton (current-member-weighted Sea Org) and Ransom (six British former Witnesses) remain other papers.

**Q5.** Mode of exit vs age of socialisation: neither evidenced as Ransom’s joint claim. Official slip, resignation, inactivity, refund spike, DEP release, and walk-away are exit *routes* in packet language, not her IPA design.

**Q6.** A self-measurement metric separable from confession and purity is generally not named. Ransom’s countable preaching-hours stick is her respondents’ metaphor, not a roster finding.

**Q7.** Harvey’s two senses of totalism — environment-creating and life-claiming — look separable where a weekend, depot, or shop creates a room without claiming housing and employer (landmark Time 2011; military-boot-camp recruiting web / DEP-at-home / Family Day vs in-depot isolation; clubhouse, crypto-tribe, supreme-or-harley §2 NRM Q7). Separable is not a score.

**Q8.** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost,” as opposed to illustrating it, is intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. That is the modal supplement finding for the modal participant (low-to-partial exit: non-refund after windows; unused bike; miss the drop; leave-quietly; guest status remains possible). It undercuts the strong link for that participant. Inner-lane thickenings already labeled stay labeled.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing consumer brand as a universal condition. Membership marks are graduate vs non-graduate; CrossFitter vs globo; Apple person vs other device; finisher vs modifier; wearer of the box vs reseller vs fake; Bitcoiner vs nocoiner. Muniz and O’Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement. Apple remains the Macintosh control, not Supreme.

**Q2.** Interacting brand community is the official design where a room, box, Store, Teams, camp, Lodge, depot, or arena structures relationships among buyers or members. 75-hard §2 brand Q2 and the-secret §2 brand Q2: closer to a non-interacting brand public. Supreme-or-harley §2 brand Q2: partial-closer. WeWork, Clubhouse, crypto-tribe: partial. AA and LDS interact as fellowship or church, not as admirers of a brand. Occupy: partial at most.

**Q3.** Moral responsibility as they defined it is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. It thickens as a ladder is climbed (assisting; sash; Dream Team; Crucible teammate-or-fail) and is not measured as “limited liability” for those who assist. Not a how-to.

**Q4.** Official talk is mostly desacralizing or secular (education, GPP, privacy, payment network, quality/style/authenticity). Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device; whether participant-side sacralizing talk exists is often undecidable from the packet. Scientology and LDS official talk *is* religion, not Kozinets’ fan-side move. Cult noun stays the packet’s paraphrase, not this note’s verdict.

**Q5.** Internal heterogeneity vs continuity: unanswered. Thomas, Price and Schau 2013 stays closed; Harley 1995 full JCR stays closed. Do not reconstruct elective affinities as a roster finding.

**Q6.** Self-marking is widely present (vocabulary, shirt, Leaderboard name, playa name, box-logo sticker as official FAQ). Recruiting sits on interaction-first (invite, box, Lodge, recruiter) or media/hashtag-first (75 HARD, The Secret) or a mix. Largely-online public is the flagship only where the supplement already said so (75-hard; the-secret). Do not treat Muniz and O’Guinn as a how-to.

## 6. Limits and overclaims

It does not collapse Supreme into Apple, Harley, or Peloton; does not collapse WeWork or Clubhouse into scene death of the 2026 remainder; does not treat a price crash, Lopp moniker, SEC impairment, or unused bike as field collapse.
