## 1. What this is

This is the record of **Loki's own words and decisions** on the movement-research project — not the research itself. Four separate sweeps were run over the live server: the raw conversation archive (his actual Telegram messages and voice notes to Gaia), Gaia's daily memory files, the mission-control task and decision records, and the shared docs plus the other ten agents' logs. Everything below is deduplicated across those four sweeps.

The point of the document is simple: the research output survives on disk, but the *intent* behind it — what he was actually after, what he rejected, and the lines he drew — lived almost entirely in chat, and chat rots. If this project is ever restarted on a new platform or handed to a new agent, this is the founder's brief.

**Honest note on thickness:** his direction is real and traceable, but a lot of it survives only as Gaia's paraphrase rather than his exact words. Every entry below is labeled. Where it says **(recorded paraphrase)**, those are not his words — they're the system's summary of what he said, and his original phrasing is gone.

## 2. The through-line

**What the project is for:** reverse-engineer why movements work. Not to write about them, not to design one yet — to build a *database* of how loyalty, charisma, zealotry, ritual, and belonging are actually manufactured, movement by movement, so that the mechanics can later be lifted and applied deliberately.

**What he kept pushing for:**

- **Coverage before cleverness.** The single most-repeated instruction across five months. Analyze every movement on the list first; the cross-movement synthesis, the matrices, the elegant frameworks come *after*. He said it in April ("doing deep analysis doesn't make sense if the entire data set hasn't even been analyzed yet") and again, harder, in June ("After every fucking movement has been analyzed that is your primary objective").
- **Pace.** He audited progress roughly monthly — March, April (twice), May, June — and nearly every audit ended in some version of "this is slower than I expected." His fix in April was to replace a vague sprint with a hard numeric quota and to explicitly disqualify busywork from counting as progress.
- **Full-spectrum, unflinching analysis.** Rituals and why they work. Rating the efficacy of mechanics including the ugly ones — sex, drugs, induced enlightenment. Scrape the hostile sources too, ignore their cynicism, take the mechanics.
- **Documentation that reads like a system.** He audited the folder itself, flagged stale and duplicate docs, and asked for a canonical map "so the folder itself reads like a real system instead of an archaeology site."

**What he kept rejecting:**

- **Branding it Apotheosis.** Corrected explicitly on 2026-03-15: "This is not going to be an apotheosis movement."
- **Designing the movement instead of studying movements.** Gaia drifted twice into blueprint/naming work and both times he pulled it back to research. Monolith is *downstream* of the database, not the same thing as it.
- **Self-censorship and amateur legal hedging.** He told Gaia to stop softening the research with its own compliance language — assume the lawyers and CPAs exist on the back end, analyze the mechanics.
- **Moralizing.** Standing rule from day one: no accusatory or preachy framing when the subject is religion, community, or tax structure.
- **Synthesis-first work.** Twice reversed.

**Where it stopped:** his last directive is 2026-06-25. After that there is no message from him on this topic anywhere in the corpus. The project kept running on nightly automation — a target packet closed 2026-07-07, another restored 2026-08-02 — and then went quiet. Nothing in the record kills or pauses it. It just stopped receiving him.

## 4. Standing rules and guardrails

These are the durable instructions. If the project restarts anywhere, these carry forward until he says otherwise.

1. **This is research, not movement design.** Build the database and decipher the mechanics. Do not design, name, or brand a movement inside this project. Monolith is a separate, later translation layer.
2. **Only Loki reopens the design lane.** Two separate docs tie that authority to him by name. Absent an explicit instruction from him, everything stays research.
3. **Coverage before depth. Individual packets before synthesis.** Stated April 27, restated June 25 in stronger terms. This was the governing rule when the project went quiet — a restart resumes here, not at the synthesis layer.
4. **Assume the lawyers and accountants exist; don't play one.** No self-censoring, no softening, no inserting amateur legal conclusions into the analysis. Flag counsel-review territory in a dedicated section instead of distorting the research.
5. **Nothing operationalized ships against finance/legal direction.** Every target packet ends with a risks-and-questions-for-review section.
6. **No moralizing.** Religion, community structures, tax incentives — respectful and practical, never accusatory or preachy.
7. **Full spectrum, including the ugly mechanics.** Rituals and why they work; rate efficacy across the whole range, sex and drug-induced enlightenment included. Ignore hostile sources' cynicism and take their mechanics.
8. **Keep the author's named terms.** A short cited definitional line when the wording is the claim. Paraphrase the rest. Analysis over transcription. I don't care about verbatim.
9. **Progress is measured in new substantive output, not maintenance.** README rewiring, path fixes, and doc re-syncing do not count as progress.
10. **The documentation is part of the deliverable.** Docs must have an actual purpose supporting the project, no duplicates, no stale files, and a canonical map so the folder "reads like a real system instead of an archaeology site."
11. **Keep the movement R&D lane separate from founder-brand marketing.** Formalized 2026-03-17.

No later file shows that ever happening. **If he remembers what he said, saying it again is the single highest-value thing he could add to this document.**

**Most of April and June survives only in paraphrase.** There is no raw inbound Telegram log on the server. The expected spool directory contains one synthetic test event; its database table is empty. That means his exact phrasing survives only where it happened to land in the conversation archive, or where Gaia transcribed a short reply into a structured record. The sprint-quota approval, the founder-brand split, the legal-filter correction — all real, all dated, none in his voice.

**Silence isn't a decision.** Nothing in the record pauses or cancels the project. His input simply stops after 2026-06-25, consistent with the broader pattern of the agent team going dormant. A restart should treat the June 25 rule as still standing, not assume the project was abandoned deliberately.

**One unattributed document worth a look.** `~/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/DOCTRINE_WITHOUT_DOCTRINE_WIP.md` reads in a first-person voice consistent with his own philosophy — a four-layer progressive-revelation doctrine sketch for Monolith (mechanics / meaning / mythic / spiritual) with an explicit non-negotiables list: opt-in only, no single authority, no shaming people who leave, behavioral proof only. Given the density of what was found and the clean start/stop pattern — dense March through June, absolute zero in July and August — this is very likely all of it.
