## Six primitives in plain English
1. **Compact doctrine** — a short public belief/behavior card lowers onboarding friction and speeds first-week retention.
2. **Pull-factor onboarding** — an immediate visible win on day 0 makes the value legible fast enough to improve activation.
3. **Ritual map** — a predictable micro-ritual cadence keeps people returning without increasing commitment pressure.
4. **Felt alignment** — warmth-first onboarding retains better than intensity-first; intensity should be earned later.
5. **Container + portability** — keep a strong branded culture while giving members portable assets they can keep.
6. **Transparency + room legibility** — visible rules and boundaries reduce perceived risk and widen mainstream trust.

## Recommended first product test
Start with **ritual map + pull-factor onboarding**: ship a 24-hour starter sequence that gives a fast win, a visible trace, and a repeatable daily micro-action.

## Fast review options
- **Approve** — keep the six as-is and move to downstream translation.
- **Tighten** — keep the set but sharpen wording or examples.
- **Reorder** — same content, different priority stack.

## Founder decision prompt
If only one primitive gets translated into an Apotheosis experiment first, the best starting bet is the onboarding/ritual combo because it changes activation and retention fastest.
