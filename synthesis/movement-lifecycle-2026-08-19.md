Code (same family as the primitives matrix and ritual taxonomy, adapted for stages):

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **S** | Strong / load-bearing stage in that packet. Named as a durable engine in exec summary, extracted-primitives list, and/or §7 / §6 / §8. |
| **P** | Present. Extracted and usable, but not the packet’s center of gravity, or present in a thinner / adjacent / container-bound form. |
| **W** | Weak / thin / official-none / contrast-only. Named as missing, proxy, unofficial, historical-only, or only as a foil. |
| **A** | Absent or not extracted. The packet looked and did not find it, or explicitly said it is not there. |
| **?** | Packet silent, unpublished interior, or contested. A legal conclusion would be required to score S/P. Needs counsel review if the cell is later used as more than a research flag. |

Rules used this pass:
- Every **S** or **P** in the tables is backed by a packet-section or extracted-primitive number in §5b (anchor key). If I could not point at a section I actually read, the cell is **W**, **A**, or **?**.
- Official voice vs reporting / scholarship stays labeled in the anchor when the score depends on that split.
- If scoring the cell as S/P would require deciding a legal question (forced labor, trafficking, disconnection-as-policy, etc.), the cell is **?** and the note says needs counsel review. The surrounding packet may still describe the mechanic as observed.
- Do not transcribe ceremony, passwords, or leaked interiors. Unpublished interiors stay unpublished.
- Harley cells are not scored. Mentions appear only as contrast notes under Supreme.
- Attract is scored from first-contact mechanics (§7 steps 1–3; exec front door). Screen is scored from a gate *before* the named crossing (ballot, NDA, MEPS, invite, ration, Survival/ticket, desire-to-stop, health PDF) — not from later ethics/worthiness that already sits on escalate. Threshold is the first real crossing. Belong is recurrence plus a social container. Escalate is the next-rung ladder (§7.7 + §8). Operate is member-to-operator / serve. Exit/decay/collapse is a named off-ramp, cooling, or organizational death — not “people sometimes leave.”

## 6. Stage-by-stage map

Coverage before cleverness. Each paragraph says what the stage is doing mechanically when it is **S**, names two to four strongest packet anchors, and names one or two leftovers that skip, invert, or break it. This is description of observed stages, not a recipe.

### 1. Attract / front door

When it is **S**, a first touch makes looking twice cheap: an invite, a free sample, a slogan, a window, a clip, a quiz, a test, a hashtag. The packet’s job at this stage is permission to approach, not yet a crossing. Strongest anchors: Landmark free Introduction + 90% invite (§7.1–2, ext#8); AA Find-a-meeting / self-diagnosis / attraction-not-promotion (ext#1, §7.1); LDS missionary + free Book of Mormon (ext#2); Occupy “we are the 99%” as a sentence anyone in debt could put on a sign (ext#1, §7.1); Megachurch weekend-for-people-who-didn’t-like-church (ext#1); Apple Think Different residue before a SKU (ext#1). Every one of the 23 packets has a named front door, so the row is **S** across the corpus. Leftovers that invert *what the door is*, not whether it exists: Clubhouse treated the invite itself as the object of attraction (ext#1) until Opening Day deleted it; QAnon and The Secret attract without a room (moral-emergency feed; Oprah-era kit). Attract without a later belong or operate is the leftover failure, not a missing front door.

### 2. Screen / filter

When it is **S**, someone or some rule decides who does *not* cross, before the named initiation. The filter can be a ballot, an NDA, a medical/ASVAB gate, a scarce ticket, an invite, or a rationed unit. Strongest anchors: Freemasons proposer/seconder + summons publicity + ballot (ext#4, §7); Boot camp ASVAB / MEPS / IST / DEP walk-away (funnel table, ext#13); NXIVM NDA + elite invite (ext#8, §7.4); Burning Man Survival + no-spectators + scarce ticket (ext#4–5, #14); Supreme first-come + one-style + refuse-any-reason + SMS chosen-few (ext#3, #17); Clubhouse invite-as-status (ext#1) — the leftover that then *broke* its own screen on Opening Day. Leftovers that skip it: QAnon has **A** (no central screen, no membership desk); The Secret has **A** (FAQ: no seminars, no accreditation). Occupy inverts it (walk-in; demandlessness as inclusion). 75 HARD is **W** (anyone can Day 1). Screen is the row the 4-beat taxonomy sketch does not name; the leftover corpus is why it is here.

### 3. Threshold / initiation

When it is **S**, a first real crossing installs a new noun: graduate, sash, OM practitioner, CrossFitter, Day 1, iPhone person, preclear, alcoholic, brother/sister, Member, burner, firewalker, occupier, Marine, Entered Apprentice, person-who-copped. Strongest anchors: Landmark three-day room (ext#5); NXIVM multi-day intensive (exec); OneTaste first timed dyad (“the ritual is the product”); LDS baptism + confirmation (ext#6); Tony Robbins Firewalk (ext#1); Boot camp receiving-strip then Crucible/EGA as forced threshold (ext#3, #9) — the leftover that makes the crossing non-optional once shipped. Leftovers that thin it: QAnon **P** (first drop/explainer; optional oath); The Secret **P** (first watch/read, solo); WeWork **P** (keycard, not a rite). Freemason First Degree is **S** on official public copy; interiors stay unpublished (holes). Distinct clocks (Forum weekend, 75 days, 13 weeks, stadium days, Gate night) sit in notes, not as an eighth stage.

### 4. Belong / recurrence

When it is **S**, a container repeats: a meeting, a ward, a box hour, a course room, a camp, a Lodge, a weekend plus a small group, a platoon, a device that is still in the hand tomorrow. Recurrence is what turns a crossing into a self. Strongest anchors: AA home group + cheap meetings (ext#5, #10); CrossFit local box + daily WOD (exec, §6); LDS geographic ward + sacrament (exec, §6); Freemasons Lodge + Festive Board (ext#6); Megachurch weekend + semester group (ext#7); Peloton Leaderboard/Teams as community-named-as-product (§6). Leftovers that skip or invert it: Clubhouse novelty spike without belong (**W** — drop-in / leave-quietly; FOMO dies when friends leave); 75 HARD **W** (no chapter; ambient feed); The Secret **W** (solo by design); Supreme **W** (no club, no dues; Harley HOG weekly ride is the contrast, not a 24th case). Occupy belong is **S** *inside* the park and is the object eviction removes — a container-bound **S**, not a durable parish.

### 5. Escalate / ladder

When it is **S**, each completed rung explains the last as incomplete: a next course, a next intensive, a next device, a next recommend, a next event, a next desk, a next degree. Strongest anchors: Scientology Bridge (ext#3, §8); NXIVM sash/stripe + 16-day + subgroups (ext#1, §8); Landmark Forum → Advanced → SELP → Wisdom (§8); LDS covenant path / recommend / mission (ext#5, §7.7); Tony Robbins UPW → DWD → Platinum (ext#8); WeWork On Demand → All Access → Dedicated → Private → Enterprise (ext#8) — the leftover that *then* collapsed. Leftovers that skip or break it: Occupy **W** (no durable ladder; the donation pile became an argument the process was not built to run); Clubhouse **W** (Plus / Payments after the spike); Supreme **W** (next Thursday is a clock, not a doctrine chart). AA escalate is **P** on purpose (sponsor/Steps/service, not a paid ladder).

### 6. Operate / serve

When it is **S**, a member becomes the delivery system: volunteer, coach, sponsor, missionary, Angel, Dream Team, assisting, node owner, Regional Contact, baker-turned-explainer, Lodge officer. Distribution is no longer a marketing department. Strongest anchors: AA 12th Step / sponsor (ext#11); LDS unpaid calling + missionary force (ext#7, #3); Landmark enrollment-as-pedagogy + assisting (ext#9–10); CrossFit L1 → affiliate owner (ext#16); Megachurch SHAPE → Dream Team → Extension (ext#8); Freemasons officer year + charity (ext#7, #9); QAnon audience-to-operator gradient (ext#18) even without a room. Leftovers that skip it: Occupy attract without a durable operate layer after eviction (**P** inside the park, then gone — the leftover break); WeWork **W** (Community team is staff); Supreme **A** (no member-to-operator); The Secret **A** (FAQ: no teacher accreditation, no franchise); 75 HARD **W** (self-administered; no certified leader).

### 7. Exit / decay / collapse

When it is **S**, the packet names a cooling or a death, not merely that people can leave: an eviction, an IPO that strips the myth, an open-invite that kills the status object, a house that closes, an entity that is later seized/defunct, a cachet file of impairments and search-drop. Strongest anchors: WeWork S-1 → ouster → Chapter 11 → de-missionizing (ext#16–18); Occupy eviction 15 Nov 2011 / “crushed in 2012” (ext#15, #18); Clubhouse Opening Day + copycats + 2023 half-staff (ext#12, #18); NXIVM 2017–18 mass exit / 2018 collapse (exec, §9); OneTaste 2018 close (exec, §12); Supreme VF-era cooling (ext#11, #18). Leftovers that invert it: Boot camp **W** (graduation is completion of a timebox, not decay of the Corps); AA **W** (exit is designed easy; no dues clawback); Clubhouse’s *own* screen-kill is the decay mechanic. Crypto winters are **P** (heat dies; protocol keeps producing blocks; HODL recodes the crash as an ordeal passed). Scientology leaving-cost is **?** (holes). This row is observation of named endings, not a guide to producing one.
