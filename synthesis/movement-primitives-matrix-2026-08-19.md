## 2. Method (how a cell was scored)

Read each packet’s executive summary, **Extracted primitives** block, §3 protocol, §4b pull factors, §5 ritual stack (including the 8-type table), and §6 social architecture. Then mapped those packet-local primitives onto the 16 library rows. Did not score from memory of the March 18 synthesis. Did not score from `cases/analysis/` files.

Code:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **S** | Strong / load-bearing in that packet. The mechanic is named as a durable engine in exec summary, extracted-primitives list, and/or the ritual/social tables. |
| **P** | Present. Extracted and usable, but not the packet’s center of gravity, or present in a thinner / adjacent form. |
| **W** | Weak / thin / contrast-only. Named, proxy, unofficial, or only as a foil. |
| **A** | Absent or not extracted. The packet looked and did not find it, or explicitly said it is not there. |
| **?** | Packet silent, layers conflict, or a legal conclusion would be required to score. Needs counsel review if the cell is later used as more than a research flag. |

Rules used this pass:
- Every **S** or **P** in the tables is backed by a packet-section or extracted-primitive number in §5 (anchor key). If I could not point at a section I actually read, the cell is **W**, **A**, or **?**.
- Official voice vs reporting / scholarship stays labeled in the anchor when the score depends on that split.
- If scoring the cell as S/P would require deciding a legal question (forced labor, trafficking, disconnection-as-policy, etc.), the cell is **?** and the note says needs counsel review. The surrounding packet may still describe the mechanic as observed.
- Compact doctrine (row 1) = a small portable worldview compression. Branded distinctions (row 5) = short phrases that change what members notice. They overlap; they were scored separately.
- Daily micro-rituals (row 6) = a recurring small act plus a trace, usually daily. Micro-ritual persistence (row 7) = whether that small act (or a close successor) survives the flagship / the first month / the eviction / the open-invite. A case can have a strong daily-on-playa and a weak year-round persist.
- Ritual signature — compact (row 8) = the recognizable “this is us” form (WOD hour, timed OM, Firewalk, human mic, Thursday 11AM, Crucible + EGA, tyled degree).
- Program over personality (row 12) = the packet shows a rulebook / syllabus / constitution that can travel without the founder’s body. Founder-fossil scripture (Hubbard) is P, not S, unless the packet also shows anti-charisma design (AA).
- Harley cells are not scored. Mentions appear only as contrast notes under Supreme.
