## Identity labels

- member — official 2019 and 2026 noun; not customer, not tenant (S-1; CNBC on S-1)
- WeWork member / All Access member / On Demand member — product-as-identity
- community team / Community Manager — local operator who issues the keycard and programs events (S-1; 2026 About/SF)
- enterprise member — S-1: organizations with 500-plus employees
- creator — S-1 origin language and Creator Awards caption
- pioneer of WE — WeGrow launch register via Wikipedia/S-1 naming, not a 2026 official tile
- WeLive resident — historical housing identity (S-1 / Wikipedia)
- WeGrow parent / student — historical school identity (Wikipedia; NY Mag)
- firewalker-equivalent is not a WeWork word. Rank is membership class and whether you have a door.

- On Demand / Day Pass — lowest commercial yes
