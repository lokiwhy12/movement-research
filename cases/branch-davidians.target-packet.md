## Executive summary

The Branch Davidians sold a portable **end-time literacy product** rooted in Adventist reform prophecy: a Mount Carmel community in which a living interpreter of the **Seven Seals** (under David Koresh / Vernon Howell) claimed to unlock Revelation for an elect remnant preparing for Babylon's assault and Christ's kingdom. The adhesion engine was not "stockpiled guns" alone. It was a stack: Shepherd's Rod prophetic succession grammar → Branch festival/Israel emphasis → charismatic Bible-study monopoly → **House of David** sexual reorganization → Mag-Bag arms-trade income + "Army for God" defensive myth → siege as lived prophecy.

**Movement-DNA read (this pass):** Branch Davidians fused **Adventist prophetic succession**, **sacred geography (Mount Carmel as Davidic kingdom)**, **marathon Bible literacy as belonging**, **founder rename as messianic claim (David + Cyrus/Koresh)**, and — under Koresh — a **House of David** rule that annulled prior marriages and concentrated sexual access, coupled to a **gun-show / Mag-Bag economy** and an **apocalyptic us-vs-Babylon enclosure**. The 1993 ATF raid and 51-day FBI siege converted the group's persecution theology into live theater; the Apr 19 fire deaths (public-record paraphrase only) froze the brand into American militia memory. Living residue splits: non-Koresh Branch claimants (e.g., Mitchell/Wilde lineage; Pace's "The Branch, The Lord Our Righteousness") vs Koresh-loyal remnant waiting narratives. That split — **practice lineage contested / siege myth shared** — is itself a durable mechanic.

**Public record (paraphrase, not our finding):** On Feb 28, 1993, ATF agents attempting to execute search/arrest warrants at Mount Carmel entered a firefight; four ATF agents and multiple Branch Davidians died that day (counts vary slightly by source on the Branch side). FBI Hostage Rescue then ran a 51-day siege. On Apr 19, 1993, after a CS-gas insertion using armored vehicles, the main building burned; roughly seventy-six people inside died that day, including children and Koresh; total deaths across raid+fire commonly summarized near eighty-six including agents. Fire origin remains contested in public debate (DOJ 1993 reports and Danforth Special Counsel conclude Branch Davidians started the fires; some survivors and independent critics dispute). Criminal trials followed; Danforth OSC (2000) largely cleared federal agents of starting the fire / directing gunfire that day while criticizing delayed disclosure of pyrotechnic CS rounds. **Needs counsel review. Not a legal conclusion of this packet. No how-to.**
