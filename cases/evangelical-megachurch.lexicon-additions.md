## Identity labels

- seeker — Purpose Driven product noun for the unchurched person being turned into a reproducing disciple (About Pastor Rick). Live Saddleback pages opened this pass prefer guest / newcomer / anyone.
- guest / newcomer — Give FAQ and Newcomers Connection nouns
- attender — weekend body before the signature
- member — trusted Jesus + immersion after salvation + Activate 1 + signed covenant / Membership Agreement (Extensions / Careers)
- Saddleback family — covenant and careers register
- belonger / home — Activate copy (“make Saddleback your home”)
- Dream Team / SHAPE-placed servant — volunteer identity
- group leader / Extension starter — reproduction identities (starter is member-only)
- calling — staff noun on the careers page
- teaching pastor / lead pastor — Andy and Stacie Wood on About; CT 2023 external layer on the female-pastor title
- founding pastor — Rick Warren on Purpose Driven / About origin
- clergy couple — Hartford sector noun (17%); official About presents Andy and Stacie together without using that term

## Rank / ladder labels

- Crowd → Congregation (CLASS 101) → Committed (201) → Core (301) → Commissioned (401) — Purpose Driven chapter-40 PDF
- guest → Newcomers Connection → Activate 1 → baptized → covenant member → small-group member → Talk It Over leader → Dream Team → cleared Kids/SSM leader → Extension starter → intern / staff
- Activate 1 Find Belonging / Activate Step 3 Make a Difference — live campus names (careers). Activate 2 **not named** on pages opened
- CLASS 101 / 201 / 301 / 401 — portable clone-kit names still published on rickwarren.org
- Beyond the Tithe — giving rank above 10%
- Time-in-role prestige (group leader, Dream Team veteran, Extension host) is cultural and **not** a purchased sash
- Willow Creek 1994 seeker prototype is a labeled variant rank in the external literature, not a Saddleback rung

The missing log line is that rank is belonging-gated and role-gated (signature, water, SHAPE placement), not a paid chart position. The portable product still numbers the rungs 101–401; the live campus re-skins the first rungs as Activate.

- self-help that starts with the self (Purpose Driven About)
