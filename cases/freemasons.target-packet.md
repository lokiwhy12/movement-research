## Executive summary

On UGLE homepage and What is Freemasonry (opened this pass), Freemasonry is one of the oldest secular social and charitable organisations in the world, with roots in medieval stonemasons who built castles and cathedrals. Official aim: empower members to be the best they can be -- building character, supporting members as individuals, helping them make a positive contribution to society. Four named values: Integrity, Friendship, Respect, Service. Official history page (opened): older formula was Brotherly Love, Relief and Truth, now expressed as those four. Membership (official become / about / homepage): open to men over 18 from all backgrounds, irrespective of race or religion; more than 7,000 Lodges; students may join one of 87 Universities Scheme Lodges. Women are invited to two female-only Grand Lodges (Order of Women Freemasons; Freemasonry for Women / HFAF). UGLE FAQ (opened): only men aged over 18 join UGLE; the two womens bodies and UGLE prefer single-sex practice; UGLE regularly hires its facilities to the womens Lodges.

The adhesion engine, from pages opened this pass, is not a ticketed seminar. It is a local Lodge the candidate chooses, entered after an interest form, a local Freemason contact, an application that prints the candidates name, age, occupation and addresses on the Lodge summons, a proposer and seconder, and a ballot at a regular meeting (Information for the Guidance of Members of the Craft, 2024, opened: Rules 159 and 160 -- proposals and ballots at Regular Meetings except real emergency). Then three named Craft ceremonies that official public copy describes as one-act plays / stages of life, then ongoing Lodge belonging, Festive Board dining, officer progression, and charity. Official FAQ cost line: on average annual dues and other fees amount to 200 pounds a year, although there are local variations. Lodge-level initiation-fee tables are not published on UGLE pages opened this pass. Grand Lodge Paper of Business June 2024 (opened): 2025 annual dues to Grand Lodge 51 pounds in a Metropolitan Area or Province (VAT included); registration of a Mason inclusive of Grand Lodge Certificate, Royal Arch exaltation and Grand Chapter Certificate 105 pounds exclusive of VAT in a Metro/Province Lodge; annual contribution to The Masonic Charitable Foundation 17 pounds per member in Metro/Province or unattached England and Wales. Those are Grand Lodge levies, not a lodge dining-and-subscription invoice.

Official What is Freemasonry (opened) on the three degrees, paraphrase only: First Degree -- initiated, becomes Entered Apprentice; reminds that all are equal; those that do well look after those less fortunate. Second Degree -- Fellowcraft; better themselves through education; self-development. Third Degree -- Master Mason; use life wisely and be remembered for the right reasons. Lodge-and-officers blog (opened) names the same three as Ceremony of Initiation / Passing / Raising and adds Installation of the Worshipful Master and Ceremony of Exaltation (Royal Arch). Official FAQ: details of the ceremonies can easily be accessed online but nothing beats experiencing it for yourself. That is official public copy. It is not a script. Interiors, passwords, grips, and oath wording are unpublished / not opened on official pages this pass and are not reconstructed from exposes.

Royal Arch (official royal-arch page, opened): fourth and final stage in Pure Antient Masonry as defined in the 1813 Act of Union; anyone a Master Mason for four weeks can join; about 40 percent of UGLE Freemasons are Royal Arch Masons. Strategy 2022 and Beyond (quoted on the lodge-officers page): one organisation; indissoluble link of Craft and Royal Arch. Do not treat Scottish Rite 33-degree ladders as this exemplar.

That is the official Grand Lodge voice. It is not the scholarship layer and not the caricature file.

Do not collapse Bogdan into ugle.org.uk. Academy of Management 2024/2025 abstracts (opened as abstracts, not full papers): 48-month ethnography of the Grand Lodge of Scotland -- secrecy as institutional maintenance.
