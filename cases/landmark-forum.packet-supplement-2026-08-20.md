## 1. What kind of case Landmark is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** For-profit, non-residential course: three long days plus a Tuesday evening, then an optional paid ladder (packet §0, §3, §8). Kanter’s opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Landmark is not that sample. Continuance (tuition, post-window non-refund, included seminar) is the closest fit. Cohesion and control are weekend-bounded room practices, not commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Official acquisition: “90% of the people who participate do so at the invitation of family and friends” (packet S7). Network entry on Stark’s 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian convert series. Lofland and Stark 1965 is a seven-condition funnel on one small group. Religious-seekership and extra-cult-detachment do not map as written.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet’s high-control scan is observational and contested, not a verdict (§13). No communal living requirement is found (§13 item 4). Harvey’s blurred boundaries — whole life entwined so leaving means leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default Forum condition. Lifton’s milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). Melton and Ransom are other samples.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Landmark sells a branded course and a graduate label. Brand community is “a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand” (Muniz and O’Guinn 2001). Room, dyads, seminar, and Tuesday guests are interacting, not an Arvidsson and Caliandro brand public. Official talk is secular education, not Kozinets’ religion/myth articulation.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). Landmark is a company selling a course — not early-risers against a state, not a polity-wide cycle, not an ended scene. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195). Fillieule’s individual disengagement can be asked of people who stepped back; collective field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance is strongest: deposit, post-window non-refund, included first seminar that is not transferable, reviewer rates, Wisdom-tier tickets (§8, §10, S3). Cohesion is weekend witness, dyads, graduate identity, later seminar (§4b factor 2; §5; §6), without communal property or pooled income. Control is “coachable,” inauthenticity, and integrity-policing as follow-through talk (§2; §7; §13 items 7, 9). Separable as problems; not her six-arrangement system.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: money and date-specific registration put a stake in the next meeting; successful groups in her sample generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); post-window non-refund is the nearest analogue (S3). Sacrifice is only partly codeable: hours, ordeal, tuition — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is partial: frequent contact during three days (§5), not homogeneous background or shared livelihood. Mortification is partial: mic shares, public coaching, inauthenticity/impact (§5). Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical services, pp.99–102). Official boundary is reunion with family, not isolation (§6; §13 item 3). Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is not codeable. No single living guru is on the 2026 homepage (§13 item 1).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet does not show that sequence. Time 2011 paid, reported benefit, and declined to continue (S13). Iannaccone’s price on outside activity: 1992 body still closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (mic shares; inauthenticity/impact; completion calls). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing arrangements. The packet never uses her word. Do not upgrade sharing to mortification.

**Q7.** “Possibility,” “transformation,” and abilities that “never leave you” are official meaning claims (§0, §1). Slogan and room authority, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Organizational analogues: est 1971, Forum 1985, employee purchase 1991 (§12) — corporate/product spans, not member-commitment duration. A participant-level cut (weekend vs next course vs assisting years) would change the reading.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable and communion never leaves the weekend; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if prior-network invitation alone produces the observed belonging; if taking the weekend and leaving is common and unpenalized beyond money already spent (Time 2011 is one such path).

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry runs mainly through pre-existing kin/friendship ties. Official proportion: 90% invitation of family and friends (S7). Employer-paid attendance is a secondary channel (Wikipedia via §7). Free introductions can attach a previously unknown guest.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: intros sell outcomes; Day 2–3 and assignments reveal completion calls and guest invites (§7). Typical 1→9 sequence is trusted inviter, then distinctions — consistent with Stark 1996’s belief-follows-attachment restatement, extended to a secular syllabus, not a convert-by-convert timing study.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions, as the conversion note recovered them: affective bond is present as invitation-and-intro warmth (their “cult” names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as 15-hour days for one weekend, not as daily contact that turns a verbal convert into a deployable agent. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured “acutely felt, enduring discrepancy.” Religious problem-solving perspective and religious seekership are mismatched (education, not church, not clinic; §0, §7). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Weak or neutralized extra-cult attachments are contradicted by family-reunion framing (§6). Activation order vs funnel cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* remains closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips’ partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown: no sample of entrants who lacked them.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Tuesday guests, the sharing-resource page, and the 90% channel require outward ties. Open-network boundary, not inward closure.

**Q7.** Mix, weighted to organized-group invitation. Official pages and journalism exist; the packet says Landmark does not lead with mass advertising on pages opened (§0b item 6). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. 90% is entry. The 94% survey line is an outcome claim with methods not found (§11). Guardian’s 80% second course is retention (2003). Time 2011 is entry plus benefit minus ladder. Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Landmark participants do not co-reside (§13 item 4). Completion calls steer relationships for a weekend; assisting “can become a life structure” (§13 item 7) without a housing requirement. What remains without co-residence: jargon, graduate identity, enrollment pressure on the same family the official story reunites (§6). Exit cost on evidence opened is “low-to-partial” — financial non-refund after windows, social disappointment of inviters, no sourced shunning doctrine (§13 item 10).

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show partial in-room rules (Time: note-taking / food / leaving discouraged; S10, S13) and an unusually open public syllabus (S2). That does not test his dependency claim; applying it here would be asserted, not tested.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey’s two senses, as the NRM note states them: environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet’s split is time-bounded ordeal vs lifestyle (§13 item 4). Time 2011 took the room and left. The two senses look separable here.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost,” as opposed to illustrating it, would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records weekend load, family contact, and leave-after-Forum paths, with exit penalties low-to-partial (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal participant.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing brand. Graduate vs non-graduate is the membership mark (§4b factor 2; §6). Named enemy: the unnoticed past/story/racket; being uncoachable (§12). Outsiders who use the cult label are a “reputation surface, not the official enemy” (§0b). Muniz and O’Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement.

**Q2.** Interacting brand community, not a non-interacting brand public. Room, dyads, public coaching, Tuesday guests, included seminar, assisting: structured relationships among buyers of the same branded service. Public-only (visibility without room ties) is not the flagship design.

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Landmark’s graduate duty to share, Tuesday invites, and assisting as unpaid ops (§4b factor 6; §5; §8) thicken as the ladder is climbed. Enrollment as proof of growth is the packet’s own risk note. Not measured; not obviously “limited liability” for those who assist.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official Landmark talk does the reverse: education company, not religion, not therapy (§7 table; S9). Stigma-management by desacralizing. Whether any participant-side sacralizing talk exists is not decidable from this packet.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: portable vocabulary and graduate label (§2). Recruiting: interaction-primary (friend/family invite, guest night) with mediated support (sharing-resource page, video intro, S7). Room-first; online Forum is a delivery format, not a largely-online public (§0, §9).

### Movement failure

**Q1.** Landmark is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow’s definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization and institutionalization as he defined them (pp.201–202) are contention terms. Differential decline of a periphery (pp.202–203) is a cycle-demobilization claim. Early-riser pattern with opportunity held constant (p.196) cannot be posed from a course packet.

**Q5.** No. This packet is not the still-unopened empirical study of one ended scene. Landmark has not ended. Korea and Hong Kong in Tarrow Ch.8 are illustrations inside a theory chapter, not that study. Anderson 2009 Sociological Forum was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley) as a different scene and is not imported.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule’s plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). The packet’s emotional arc ends “either graduate-advocate or step-back” (§4). Time 2011 is an individual step-back after attendance. Collective field collapse is not observed (decades of delivery, §9).

## 3. Questions left unanswered

**Commitment.** Q4: substitution and screening — later Iannaccone-channel labels; 1992 body closed. Health advisories (S9) are not that screening (enforcement “not found,” §13). Q5: club good with participatory crowding vs private good merely co-consumed — Iannaccone body closed; official product is individual “positive, permanent shifts” (S1) in a 75–250 person room. Co-consumption is visible; his crowding-quality claim is not licensed. Q9: Hall 1988 still closed. Prior-network invitation (90%) could account for cohesion without the six-mechanism story.

**Conversion / network.** Q2: overbalance cannot be operationalized — secondary wording, not an opened 1980 AJS body; no member-tie / nonmember-tie ratio at joining. Q4 remainder: religious three, turning point, extra-cult neutralization, funnel order. *Doomsday Cult* closed. Q5 necessity: no comparison sample.

**NRM interiors.** Q3: Time left and graduates stay, but not two populations under roughly constant intensity from the same period — unanswered under the note’s own rule. Q4: cannot sample retained and departed from the same period. Q5: mode of exit vs age of socialisation — neither evidenced. Q6: no countable member self-measurement metric separable from confession and purity. Lalich and Introvigne 2022 stay closed.

**Brand community / fandom.** Q5: internal heterogeneity vs continuity — unanswered. Thomas, Price and Schau 2013 stays closed; the packet does not show Kozinets’ elective affinities. Schouten and McAlexander 1995 stays closed.

**Movement failure.** Q1–Q5 as case tests: mismatch or default no, not “unanswered pending more Landmark facts.” Taylor 1989, Tarrow 2011 Part III, Tarrow 2022 Chs 9–11, and a required ended-scene study of Landmark itself remain unopened. Anderson 2009 is opened and not imported: Landmark is not that scene and has not ended.
