## 1. What kind of case The Secret / Rhonda Byrne / TheSecret.tv is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** For-profit media/product ecosystem, non-residential, solo kit: book + 91-min film + remastered rental/Blu-ray + apps + newsletter + story library + sequel books + cards (§0, §5, §8). Official FAQ: no seminars, no teacher accreditation, no franchise, no Secret University; thesecret.tv is the only official site (S4, S6). Kanter's opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Not that sample. Continuance (low-ticket spend; unused rental minutes kept; story license irrevocable) is the closest fit. Cohesion and control without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90). Sacrifice/communion/mortification/renunciation/transcendence mostly unavailable or partial as she defined them. Cheap versus Robbins; do not overclaim cost-as-sacrifice.

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Mix, media-weighted (§0, §7). Network entry on Stark's 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian convert series. Religious-seekership does not map for a modal first book/film. Official outward move is story and product, not a franchise (S4, S6, S11). Gift circulation plus retailer keep the network open.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** Packet §13 scan is observational, not a verdict. No communal living (§13 item 4). Harvey's blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default bedroom/phone condition. Lifton's milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). Official silence versus Hassan / Wikipedia / Shermer / Dunning stay apart (S29–S35). Do not score Lifton or Harvey.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Partial. Closer than QAnon or A.A. (a living named consumer brand sits at the center). Farther than Apple or Peloton (no Store/arena; official no-seminar; social interiors not opened; S4, S16). Brand community is "a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand" (Muniz and O'Guinn 2001). Stories plus named social rooms are more brand-public (Arvidsson and Caliandro) than interacting formation. Official talk is law-of-attraction / creative being, not Kozinets' religion/myth as a finding.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is "a phase of heightened conflict across the social system" (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A living book/film/app brand is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: Super App free; Money App **4.99**; Daily Teachings **4.99** plus **3.99** IAP; rental **9.99**; Blu-ray **29.95**; hardcover **26.99** / **29.95** (Beyond Words); cards **26.95**; unused rental minutes kept; Creste LLC story license irrevocable (20 Dec 2013); no membership fee found (§8, S11–S12, S16–S17, S23–S28). Sequel-book list prices unpublished — do not invent them. Cohesion: Story of the Week; Super App Stories; Facebook / Instagram / Twitter named, interiors not opened (§6, S16). Control: Ask-Believe-Receive as a named protocol; law of attraction treated as infallible; stall = wrong signal (Ask Rhonda); TOS educational, not for health/financial diagnosis or resolution, no results warranty — dual official voice (S2, S5, S9). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable at low ticket: spend, unused minutes kept, irrevocable story license; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); unused minutes and the 2013 license are the nearest analogues (S11–S12). Sacrifice is mostly unavailable: not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy (Table 1, p.95). Communion is partial at most: stories and named rooms, not communal property (pp.88, 110–116). Mortification is partial at most: story submit; Ask Rhonda recodes stall as a wrong signal (S9, S11) — not mutual criticism, surveillance, or probationary socialization (pp.122–125). Renunciation is structurally unavailable (pp.99–102). Transcendence as "institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday" (pp.128–138) is not codeable. Living founder (Ask Rhonda; §13 item 1), not tradition-as-yesterday.

**Q3.** Something inexpensive is present. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows low-ticket cost plus later defense (stall means the wrong signal; S9), not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices sit at story submit and Ask Rhonda. Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing arrangements. Do not upgrade a story form to her mortification.

**Q7.** Creative-being identity and law-of-attraction as an infallible law are official meaning claims (§1, §2, S1, S9). Slogan and founder authority, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: 2006 film/book; Thought Rules pre-order on sale 24 Nov 2026; still selling (§0, S18) — product/brand span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance: strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable and communion never leaves named rooms / stories; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if book/film/gift alone produces belonging; if stop-buying is common and unpenalized beyond unused minutes and an irrevocable story license.

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix, media-weighted: 2006 film/book; Oprah / Larry King / Ellen-era; gift circulation; social (@thesecret / @thesecret365); Secret Scrolls; retailer (Amazon, B&N, Target, Walmart, Costco) (§0, §7, S29–S32). Proportion not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: film/book sell the hidden-law packaging; apps and Scrolls install the daily kit; sequels deepen by domain (§3, §7, §8). Official outward move is story and product, not a franchise (S4, S6). Consistent with Stark 1996's belief-follows-attachment restatement, extended to a secular media kit.

**Q4 (partial).** Affective bond is present as founder-video and story warmth (their "cult" names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as solo daily kit — not daily deployable-agent contact. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured "acutely felt, enduring discrepancy." Religious problem-solving and seekership are mismatched for a modal first book/film (§0). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted (no live-in; no shunning doctrine; gift and retailer keep the network open; §13 items 3–4, 10). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips' partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Gift circulation, retailer, social, and Oprah-era residue require outward ties. No-seminar / no-franchise is a brand boundary, not network closure (S4, S6).

**Q7.** Mix, weighted to media presence. The closer is the book, the 91-min film, or the free Super App (§7, S1, S15). Not a franchise desk. Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** Film / book / gift / retailer is entry. Apps, sequels, cards, story submit are retention. Official biography 34 million copies / 50-plus languages versus Wikipedia book over 30 million / 50 languages: not reconciled (S7, S29). Forbes 15 Jan 2009: 300 million dollars book+film — a 2009 press figure (S32).

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey's opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost. No co-residence (§13 item 4). What remains: jargon; creative-being / manifester identity; story publication. Exit: no shunning doctrine, no damages fee, no 24-month forfeit. Contractual: irrevocable story license; unused rental minutes kept. Interpretive: leaving the law means re-owning chance, illness, and luck (§13 item 10; S11–S12). Blurred boundaries are separable from co-residence for the modal reader.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show repeat-the-canon, UGC and story license to Creste LLC, and a no-seminar IP boundary (S4, S6, S11) without a closed-media system. Critical press is abundant (S29, S33–S35). That does not test the dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey's two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet's split is solo kit versus interpretive totalizing — thought explains all outcomes, not an institutional lock (§13 items 2, 4, 7). The two senses look separable.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming "blurred boundaries → high exit cost" would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records put-down-the-book / stop-buying paths; exit penalties are contractual and interpretive, not institutional (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal first book/film.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing consumer brand. Creative being / knower of The Secret versus people on a lower frequency and unauthorized trainers is the membership mark (§0b, §6, §13 item 3). Named official other: negative thought; scarcity; unauthorized teachers / Secret University (S6, S9). Cult-adjacent outsiders are a reputation surface (S35). Muniz and O'Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement.

**Q2.** More brand-public than interacting formation. Story of the Week, Super App Stories, and Facebook / Instagram / Twitter are named; interiors not opened (S16, S11). No Store/arena. Official no-seminar (S4). Structured admirer-to-admirer relationships are not shown. Partial.

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Story submit, gift a rental or deck, "joy to billions" as official intention (§7; S1, S11). Official path is not teaching (S4, S6). Not measured.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official talk is law-of-attraction / creative being, not Kozinets' religion/myth as a finding (§1, S1). Cult noun: official silence versus Hassan / Wikipedia / Shermer / Dunning (S29, S33–S35). Not collapsed. Participant-side sacralizing talk is not decidable (social interiors not opened).

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: creative being; super manifestor / expert manifester; Blu-ray collector's rank (§2, §6, S12). Recruiting: mediation-weighted (film, book, Oprah-era, retailer, social) plus story and gift. Official no-seminar (S4). Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** The Secret / TheSecret.tv is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow's definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline of a periphery, and early-riser pattern (pp.196, 201–203) are contention terms and cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. Living product (Thought Rules pre-order on sale 24 Nov 2026; still selling; S18). The packet can stand as a continuing-practice study. Neither Anderson's scene nor Tarrow's cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley): Philadelphia rave/EDM. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule's plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Put down the book/app and stop-buying the next title are individual (§7, §13 item 10). Field collapse is not observed (still selling; §8, S18).
