## 1. What kind of case ALA is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** For-profit, non-residential intensive: Level 1 three days, Level 2 four days, Level 3 three live weekends, then named follow-ons (§0, §3, §8). Kanter’s opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). ALA is not that sample. Continuance (tuition; post-window non-refund; already-paid Level 3) is the closest fit. Cohesion and control run through a trainer-held weekend without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Acquisition is a mix: friend / colleague / loved one, quiz, FAQ, Angel (§0, §7, S4). Network entry on Stark’s 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian convert series. Religious-seekership does not map as written for modal first weekend.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet’s high-control scan is observational, not a verdict (§13). No communal living requirement is found (§13 item 4). Harvey’s blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default condition. Lifton’s milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). FAQ cult / MLM language stays the packet’s paraphrase, not this note’s verdict.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** ALA sells a branded intensive and graduate marks (EQC, Angel, Emotionally Intelligent Leader). Brand community is “a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand” (Muniz and O’Guinn 2001). Room, Buddy / Power Group / team, and Level 3 coaching are interacting, not a brand public. Official talk is EQ / leadership, not Kozinets’ religion/myth as a finding. Harley 1995 stays closed.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). ALA is a living Austin company selling an intensive — not early-risers against a state, not a polity-wide cycle. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule’s individual disengagement can be asked of people who stepped back; field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: $8,495 / $9,996; 7-day refund then none; 6-month expiration; $199 later transfer; Level 3 at no additional charge; I&I $18–22k (§8, S16). Cohesion: cap 80; Buddy / Power Group / team; ALA family; CSP (§5, §6). Control: Feedback as gift; Enrollment as taught skill; trainer-held room; “the drift” (§2, §13 items 7, 9). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: money and a date-specific ATX seat put a stake in the next meeting; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); post-window non-refund is the nearest analogue (S16). Sacrifice is partly codeable: hours, ordeal, Austin travel — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is partial: frequent weekend / Level 3 contact (§5), not communal property. Mortification is partial: life-experience shares; Feedback before the team (§5). Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical services, pp.99–102). Official boundary is nest-kick, not isolation (§13 item 4). Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is not codeable. Founders plus named trainers hold the room; not a single living guru on 2026 pages (§13 item 1; S6).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense under the expense FAQ (S4), not that sequence. Iannaccone’s price on outside activity: 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (shares; Feedback; nest-kick). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing arrangements. Do not upgrade sharing or Feedback to her mortification.

**Q7.** “Language of leadership,” “Emotionally Intelligent Leader,” and Level 2 freedom / possibility are official meaning claims (§1, §2). Slogan and trainer authority, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: first training May 2016; still selling on 2026 pages (§0, S5) — corporate/product span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable and communion never leaves the weekend; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if friend-plus-Angel invitation alone produces belonging; if Level 1-and-leave is common and unpenalized beyond money already spent.

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry runs through a mix: pre-existing friend / colleague / loved one (FAQ), previously unknown Angels, and quiz / homepage (§0, §7, S4). Proportion is not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: quiz and FAQ sell EQ outcomes; exercise contents are withheld until the room; Level 3 installs Enrollment, CSP, and PSP as pillars (§3, §7, S2). Angel-warmed attachment, then distinctions — consistent with Stark 1996’s belief-follows-attachment restatement, extended to a secular syllabus. Official EST / Erhard wording stays the packet’s FAQ paraphrase (S4), not a timing finding.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions, affective bond is present as invite-and-Angel warmth (their “cult” names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as multi-day rooms and a Level 3 block — not daily contact that makes a verbal convert a deployable agent. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured “acutely felt, enduring discrepancy.” Religious problem-solving perspective and religious seekership are mismatched (EQ / leadership, not church; §0, §1). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted by nest-kick and no live-in (§13 items 3–4). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips’ partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Quiz, Angel calls, and Enrollment as a taught skill (S4) require outward ties. Language and exercise secrecy can raise a boundary; they do not close the network.

**Q7.** Mix, weighted to organized-group invitation plus official pages. A friend is a closer, not the only path (§0, §7). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. Friend / quiz / Angel is entry. Flagship weekends, already-paid Level 3, Momentum, and operator roles are retention. Grad-count lines are throughput with owned-page conflicts (§11). Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Participants do not co-reside (§13 item 4). What remains: jargon, graduate / Angel identity, Enrollment pressure on outsiders the official story invites. Exit cost: 7-day-then-zero refund, sunk vulnerability, not an EQC graduate, “the drift”; no sourced shunning doctrine (§13 item 10).

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show exercise details withheld on purpose and remaining questions routed to Angels (S4; §13 item 5) without a closed-media system. That does not test his dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey’s two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet’s split is time-bounded intensive vs lifestyle / Momentum (§13 items 4, 7; S8). People can take a weekend and leave. The two senses look separable here.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost” would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records nest-kick and leave-after-weekend paths, with exit penalties low-to-partial (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal participant.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing brand. Graduate / Angel / EQC vs non-graduate is the membership mark (§4b, §6). Named enemy: limiting beliefs, comfort zone, the drift (§0b, §12). Outsiders who use the cult or MLM label are a “reputation surface, not the official enemy” (§0b). Muniz and O’Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement.

**Q2.** Interacting brand community, not a non-interacting brand public. Room, Buddy, Power Group, team share, Level 3 coaches: structured relationships among buyers of the same branded service. Public-only (testimonial wall without room ties) is top-of-funnel, not the flagship (§0, §6).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Angel duty, Enrollment-as-pillar, and graduate-coach / staff path (§4b; §7; §9) thicken as the ladder is climbed. Not measured; not obviously “limited liability” for those who Angel or staff.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official ALA talk does the reverse: EQ program, not therapy (Terms), and a FAQ that jokes then denies cult / MLM (S4, S16). Stigma-management by desacralizing. Participant-side sacralizing talk is not decidable here. Official EST / Erhard wording stays the packet’s paraphrase, not this note’s lineage finding.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: language of leadership, ATX codes, graduate / Angel titles (§2, §6). Recruiting: interaction-primary (friend / Angel) with mediated support (quiz, FAQ, S4). Room-first; Virtual EQ is stale on the opened page (§0, S14).

### Movement failure

**Q1.** ALA is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow’s definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. A living Austin intensive is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization and institutionalization (pp.201–202) are contention terms. Differential decline of a periphery (pp.202–203) is a cycle-demobilization claim. Early-riser pattern with opportunity held constant (p.196) cannot be posed from this packet.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. ALA is a living company (2026 pages still sell Live EQ and named follow-ons; S1–S16) and has not ended. Proof conflicts (§11) are not a death series. Not “one ended scene.” Not Anderson’s scene. Not Tarrow’s cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule’s plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Nest-kick and leave-after-Level-1 are individual (§4, §7). Field collapse is not observed (still delivering; §9).
