## Executive summary

The Rajneesh / Osho movement sold a portable awakening product: **cathartic “active” meditations**, a **neo-sannyas identity kit** (new name, mala, color clothing), and a **founder-as-presence** discourse culture that recoded ordinary restlessness, sexual repression, and institutional religion as the problem — and celebration, witnessing, and surrender to the master’s field as the exit. The adhesion engine was not “free love” alone. It was a stack: intense group catharsis → shared aesthetic (orange/maroon) → initiation as personal destiny → commune immersion → (in Oregon) political enclosure around a silent charismatic core managed by a lieutenant layer.

**Movement-DNA read (this pass):** Osho fused **ritual tech** (timed multi-stage Dynamic and siblings), **identity renaming**, **color synchrony**, **discourse as doctrine without a closed creed**, and — in the Oregon era — **totalizing commune logistics** that, under a small management circle, escalated into election interference and bioterror (public-record paraphrase only). The successor face (Osho International Meditation Resort / OIF IP) keeps the meditations, robes, Evening Meeting, and Multiversity ladder while officially denying “ashram,” “membership,” and “guru game.” That split — **practice continuity + institutional amnesia** — is itself a durable mechanic.

**Public record (paraphrase, not our finding):** In 1984, commune leadership contaminated salad bars in The Dalles, Oregon, sickening 751 people (45 hospitalized; none died) — still cited as the largest bioterror attack on U.S. soil. Ma Anand Sheela and others were later convicted on related and adjacent charges; Rajneesh entered an Alford plea on immigration counts, was fined, given a suspended sentence, and deported (1985). **Needs counsel review. Not a legal conclusion of this packet.**
