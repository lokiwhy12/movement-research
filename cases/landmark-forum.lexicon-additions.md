## Identity labels
- **graduate** — the primary post-ordeal identity; confers belonging and an implied duty to share
- **coachable** — virtue-label for dropping the observer stance; its inverse (“uncoachable” / “observer”) marks resistance
- **enrolling** — identity-as-verb: a person who is being extraordinary *is* someone who enrolls others
- **inauthentic** — moral-cognitive label for the pre-shift self
- **racket** used as a who-you-are diagnosis (“that’s your racket”), not only a behavior
- **possibility** used as a self-noun (“my possibility”) rather than a maybe
- **Forum leader / Introduction leader / SELP leader** — credentialed speaker identities
- **reviewer** — paid returner; repetition is normal, not remedial
- **Wisdom** — late-ladder identity community, not just a course name
- **assist / assisting** — unpaid operator identity that fuses service with status

## Rank / ladder labels
- curious guest → participant → **graduate** → seminar regular → **Advanced** graduate → **SELP** → assisting / intro leader → Communication / coaching / **Training Academy** → **Wisdom** → course leader
- numbered intensity is time-based (3 days, 4 months, year-long TMLP), not colored belts
- **first seminar included** functions as a rank lock-in more than a product
- **Master Class** signals seniority of the *leaders*, not the students
- **Elders** (65+) is an age-gated Wisdom lane, a rare explicit demographic rank

## Ritual / action verbs
- **distinguish** — pull a pattern from background to foreground
- **share** — public testimony; also the approved way to talk about the Forum
- **declare** — speech act that is treated as creating a future
- **complete / completion** — close a past account, usually by phone or letter
- **enroll** — invite so the other person is “touched, moved, and inspired”
- **invent** — transformation verb, opposed to “change”
- **pop** — journalist-reported room metaphor for the moment the reframe lands (Guardian 2003)
- **assist** — serve the delivery machine
- **review** — repeat a course at reviewer rate
- **be present / be coachable / be unreasonable** — imperative identity verbs (Guardian’s “secret seven” paraphrase of room talk; not copied as a slogan system for us)

## Enemy / problem words
- **already always listening** — the unnoticed filter
- **story** vs **what happened**
- **racket** — complaint that pays you and costs you
- **default future** / past-as-determinant
- **looking good / being right** — Time 2011 leader move: the hidden governor of behavior
- **making someone wrong** — the relationship toxin
- **breakdown** — officially recuperated as access, still named as the obstacle
- **uncoachable / observer** — the local heresy
- **more, better, different** — the official insult to ordinary self-help “change”

## Promise / destiny words
- **transformation** (invented) vs **change** (past-based)
- **possibility**
- **freedom and power**
- **effectiveness**
- **breakthrough**
- **redefine what’s possible** / **discover what’s possible**
- **make a difference**
- **extraordinary**
- **new realm of possibility** (Time’s paraphrase of poster copy)
- **yours forever** (official evening-session claim about the abilities)

## Phrase-structure patterns (why they travel)
- **X is decisive** (“context is decisive”) — short law-like sentence
- **X vs Y** pairs (what happened vs story; change vs transformation) — portable contrast
- **complaint + payoff + cost** — three-slot diagnostic
- **touched, moved, and inspired** — triplet used as the enrollment quality test
- **in just three days** — speed + completeness in one prepositional phrase
- **not therapy / transformative learning** — category jiu-jitsu (what it is by saying what it isn’t)
- trademarked everyday English (**Rackets™**, **Already Always Listening™**, **Vicious Circle™**) — ordinary words made proprietary handles
- virtue stack as “Be X” commandments (reported, not official homepage copy)

## Comparative note for the lexicon log
Landmark’s naming system wins on **speech portability** and loses on **backlash surface** when the same portable words get used to police integrity, recruiting, and staying in the room. Cross-case: closer to CrossFit (short insider verbs in normal conversation) than to NXIVM (rank fused to secret curriculum names). Do not mint Monolith equivalents here.
