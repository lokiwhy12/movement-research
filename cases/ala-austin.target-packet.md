## Executive summary

Ascension Leadership Academy (ALA) is an Austin-based, for-profit emotional-intelligence / leadership intensive. Official pages opened this pass sell a three-level live flagship (Level 1 Discovery, 3 days; Level 2 Breakthrough, 4 days; Level 3 Leadership Integration, three live weekends inside a longer application block), a 20/60/20 weekend pedagogy, a self-diagnosis quiz, an alumni/Angel conversion bridge, and a named follow-on stack (Momentum, Grace, Warrior's Council, Relationships, Abundance and Prosperity, Impact and Influence, Virtual EQ). The FAQ opened this pass roots the method in "the principles of EST and Wener Erhard" and answers "is it a cult / MLM" in the same document.

Movement-DNA read: ALA is a self-diagnosis to peer-warm to live-container to language-install to enrollment-as-curriculum to continuation-ladder system. The durable mechanics are not the EQ slogan. They are (1) a quiz that lets the prospect name their own gap, (2) a graduate who will talk to you before you pay, (3) a trainer-held room that spends most of its time in simulated trigger-exercises and witnessed sharing, (4) a compact "language of leadership" that participants are told to speak and live, (5) Level 3 that treats Enrollment, a Community Service Project, and a Personal Strategic Plan as the three pillars of becoming a leader beyond the training room, and (6) a product ladder that starts at a published about $8.5-10k flagship and continues into gender weekends, graduate Momentum, and an $18-22k speaker/facilitator immersion.

The prior packet was a public-source-constrained first pass because the official site was bot-blocked. That constraint is retired for the pages listed in SOURCES.md. Remaining retrieval misses (dedicated alumni HTML, Yelp/Trustpilot/BBB/Reddit/Quora bodies, Momentum dollar table, Virtual EQ price) are marked below. They are not a reason to invent.

Reaudit program names (Momentum, Grace, Warrior's Council, Relationships, Abundance and Prosperity) and founder-origin beats are in this packet because those pages were opened this pass, not because the reaudit remembered them.
