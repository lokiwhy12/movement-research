## Identity labels
- alcoholic — official self-applied identity; only you decide (P-2 / What-is-A.A.)
- member — official: you are a member if you say you are
- newcomer — official meeting-page noun
- home-group member — official survey-page belonging unit
- sponsor / sponsee — unofficial dyad; 81 percent of 2022 surveyed members have a sponsor
- service sponsor — official FAQ identity for service transmission
- trusted servant — official Tradition Two paraphrase: does not govern
- old-timer — press-release layer on the 2022 survey (members with long continuous sobriety)
- Loner / Internationalist — official history-page service nouns
- Class A trustee — official press: nonalcoholic friend of the Fellowship who may use a last name on camera
- Class B trustee — alcoholic trustee
- special worker — paid service-center role; not usual 12th Step work (Tradition 8 paraphrase)

## Rank / ladder labels
- passerby / family / professional observer -> first-meeting attendee -> member-if-you-say-so -> home-group member -> sponsee -> chair / treasurer / greeter -> sponsor -> service sponsor -> GSR -> district / area delegate -> Conference -> trustee / GSO staff
- Time-sober prestige (chips / anniversary) is cultural and **not** a Conference rank on official pages opened this pass
- There is no purchased rung. Literature ownership is not a rank.

The missing log line is that rank is moral-reputational (time sober, being asked to share, being useful) plus a rotating trusted-servant title, not a sash or a paid chart position.

## Enemy / problem words
- alcohol as unmanageable power / obsession to drink
- self-deception / unwillingness to face facts
- isolation
- slip / overconfidence after a dry stretch
- money, property, and prestige that divert a group
- personalities over principles
- outside contributions / public controversy
- 13th-stepping (external noun; official silence this pass)
- cult / religion charge (external; official one-line denial)

Not official enemies: society, unbelievers, moderate drinkers, temperance opponents.
