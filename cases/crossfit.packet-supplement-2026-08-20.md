## 1. What kind of case CrossFit is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** For-profit, distributed gym brand: local membership at independently owned affiliates, HQ credentials and Open/Games SKUs, no communal residence (packet §0, §6, §8). Kanter’s opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). CrossFit is not that sample. Continuance (monthly dues; L1 / Open / application no-refund) is the closest fit. Cohesion and control run through the class hour without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Acquisition is a mix: friend invite, gym locator, Open/Games spectacle, visible body-change (packet §0, §7, primitive 14). Network entry on Stark’s 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian convert series. Religious-seekership does not map as written for modal first-class.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet’s high-control scan is observational and contested, not a verdict (§13). No communal living requirement is found (§13 item 4). Harvey’s blurred boundaries — whole life entwined so leaving means leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default box condition. Lifton’s milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22).

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Closer here than Landmark or NXIVM. CrossFit sells a branded methodology and a membership mark (“CrossFitter,” box shirt, Rx, credentials). Brand community is “a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand” (Muniz and O’Guinn 2001). Box class, Open, and credential rooms are interacting, not a brand public. Official talk is GPP / health, not Kozinets’ religion/myth as a finding that the product is a religion. Harley 1995 stays closed.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). CrossFit is a company that licenses a branded class — not early-risers against a state, not a polity-wide cycle. Glassman exit, HQ sale, and affiliate churn are not automatically a Tarrow cycle ending. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule’s individual disengagement can be asked of box-leavers; field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: monthly dues; L1 $1,150 no-refund; Open $20 final; affiliate application $1,000 non-refundable; license $2,500–$4,500/year (§8, S3–S8). Cohesion: class hour, absence-noticing, box shirt (§4b, §5, §6). Control: coach as hour-gatekeeper; nutrition as doctrinal; Rx as local moral grade (§2, §13 items 2, 9). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: money puts a stake in the next class; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); L1 / Open / application no-refunds are the nearest analogues (§8, S7, S13). Sacrifice is partly codeable: hours, ordeal, dues, named Hero pieces (§5) — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is partial: frequent contact at a box (§5, §6), not communal property. Mortification is partial: score-declare; “I scaled” (§5). Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical services, pp.99–102). Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is not codeable. Post-2020 authority is IP + credentials + Rulebook, not a living guru on the 2026 homepage (§13 item 1; primitive 18).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense of the method (§7 step 8), not that sequence. Iannaccone’s price on outside activity: 1992 body still closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (score declare; “I scaled”). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing HQ arrangements. Do not upgrade scoring to mortification.

**Q7.** GPP, “unknown and unknowable,” and “It’s hard. That’s the point.” are official meaning claims (§1, §0b, §4b factor 5). Slogan plus Hero overlay, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: company idea 1996; Inc. 2000; still selling in 2026 (§0, §12) — corporate/product span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable and communion never leaves the box hour; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if friend-invite plus first-class win alone produces belonging; if trial-and-leave is common and unpenalized beyond money already spent (§7).

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry runs through a mix: friend ties, walk-in / locator strangers, and media-first Open/Games spectators (§0, §3, §7, primitive 14). Proportion is not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: intros sell scale and coach; class installs vocabulary and score; Open and L1 reveal operator identity (§3, §7). Trusted or spectacle-warmed attachment, then distinctions — consistent with Stark 1996’s belief-follows-attachment restatement, extended to a secular syllabus.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions, as the conversion note recovered them: affective bond is present as intro-and-coach-name warmth (their “cult” names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as 3–5×/week class — not daily contact that makes every verbal convert a deployable agent. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured “acutely felt, enduring discrepancy.” Religious problem-solving perspective and religious seekership are mismatched (fitness/GPP, not church; §0, §1). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted for modal members. Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips’ partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Bring-a-friend, locator, Open spectacle, and “more than one per town” (§6, S4) require outward ties. Language and Rx can raise a local boundary; they do not close the network.

**Q7.** Mix, weighted to local invitation plus a spectacle/media layer. The closer is the box (§7). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. Friend/locator is entry. Dues, Open, L1, and ownership are retention. Gym-count and Open-headcount lines are throughput / stock (§11). Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. CrossFit participants do not co-reside (§13 item 4). What remains without co-residence: jargon, “CrossFitter” identity, box graph. Exit cost is financial and social — “sunk money, lost friends, lost language, possible local stigma”; no sourced shunning doctrine (§13 item 10).

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show L1 test confidentiality, a Trainer License disparagement clause, and a 2019 social-media shutdown (Wikipedia via §13 item 5) without a closed-media system for members. That does not test his dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey’s two senses, as the NRM note states them: environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet’s split is time-bounded class vs lifestyle (§13 items 4, 7). People take a trial and leave. The two senses look separable here.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost” would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records leave-the-box paths, with exit penalties low-to-partial (§13 item 10; §7). That undercuts the strong link for the modal participant.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing gym brand. “CrossFitter” vs unstructured exerciser / “globo gym” is the membership mark (§0b, §2, §6). Named enemy: soft anonymous gym culture; specialization; inactivity / chronic disease (§0b, §12). Muniz and O’Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement.

**Q2.** Interacting brand community, not a non-interacting brand public. Box class, whiteboard, Open night, L1 room: structured relationships among buyers of the same branded service. Public-only (Games clip without box ties) is top-of-funnel, not the adhesion engine (§0, §6).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. CrossFit’s bring-a-friend, judge, coach, and owner path (§4b; §7; primitive 16) thicken as the ladder is climbed. Not measured; not obviously “limited liability” for coaches or owners.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official CrossFit talk does the reverse: methodology, GPP, health (§1, S1). Hero overlay is memorial framing, not a finding that the product is a religion. Do not reconstruct Harley 1995.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: compressed vocabulary, box shirt, Rx/scaled/foundations, credentials (§2, primitive 7). Recruiting: interaction-primary at the box with mediated support (locator, Open/Games). Room-first, not a largely-online public (§0, §5). Do not treat Muniz and O’Guinn as a how-to.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** CrossFit is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow’s definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Glassman exit, HQ sale, and affiliate churn are not that ending. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization and institutionalization (pp.201–202) are contention terms. Differential decline of a periphery (pp.202–203) is a cycle-demobilization claim. Early-riser pattern with opportunity held constant (p.196) cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default; limited partial-decline of network size only. CrossFit is a living distributed brand (2026 pages still sell class, Open, credentials, affiliation; S1–S11) and has not ended. The packet records shrinkage: Wikipedia peak ~15,500 affiliates (2018) vs ~9,900 early 2025 / “around 10,000” as of 2025; Open 415,000 (2018) vs 234,000 (2025); AP’s reported ~1,250 2020 disaffiliations (§11, §12, S14, S16). Official pages still say “more than 9,000” / “10,000+ gyms” the same week (§11). That licenses a continuing-practice study, and a partial-decline study of count stock only if those lines stay the packet’s paraphrase — not a finding of death. Not “one ended scene.” Not Anderson’s scene. Not Tarrow’s cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule’s plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Box-leaving is individual (lost friends, lost language; §7, §13 item 10). 2020 disaffiliations are clustered node-exits. Field collapse is not observed.

## 3. Questions left unanswered

**Commitment.** Q4: substitution and screening — later Iannaccone-channel labels; 1992 body closed. Q5: club good with participatory crowding vs private good merely co-consumed — Iannaccone body closed; official product is individual GPP (§1) in a coached room. Co-consumption is visible; his crowding-quality claim is not licensed. Q9: Hall 1988 still closed. Prior-network invitation could account for first belonging without the six-mechanism story.

**Conversion / network.** Q2: overbalance cannot be operationalized — secondary wording, not an opened 1980 AJS body; no member-tie / nonmember-tie ratio at joining. Q4 remainder: religious three, turning point, extra-cult neutralization, funnel order. *Doomsday Cult* closed. Q5 necessity: no comparison sample.

**NRM interiors.** Q3: stayers and leavers exist, but not two populations under roughly constant intensity from the same period. Q4: cannot sample retained and departed from the same period as a design. Q5: mode of exit vs age of socialisation — neither systematically evidenced. Q6: scores, PRs, and Open rank are organizational metrics (§11), not Ransom et al.’s self-measurement of the self. Lalich and Introvigne 2022 stay closed.

**Brand community / fandom.** Q5: internal heterogeneity vs continuity — unanswered. Thomas, Price and Schau 2013 stays closed; Rx / scaled / foundations and owner vs member are status/product lanes, not Kozinets’ elective affinities. Schouten and McAlexander 1995 stays closed.

**Movement failure.** Q1–Q4 as Tarrow case tests: mismatch, not “unanswered pending more CrossFit facts.” Taylor 1989, Tarrow 2011 Part III, and Tarrow 2022 Chs 9–11 remain unopened. Anderson 2009 is opened and not imported.
