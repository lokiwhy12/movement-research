## 1. What kind of case Evangelical megachurch / Saddleback / Purpose Driven is, for each lane

Two objects, labeled and not collapsed. (A) Saddleback Church — living church. Andy Wood lead (arrived summer 2022); Stacie Wood named. Founders Rick and Kay Warren; first service 6 April 1980 (S2). (B) Purpose Driven — living product / portable church OS. Five purposes; CLASS 101–401 still published as free downloads on rickwarren.org (S17); Purpose Driven Life 40/42-day campaign (S16, S18). Live Saddleback markets Activate, not CLASS, on pages opened (S4, S9). Willow Creek is a labeled 1994 CT variant (S22), not a second opened campus. Lakewood was not opened.

**Commitment (Kanter).** Living church + product OS, non-residential for the modal attender. Not her 1780–1860 utopian sample. Success cut 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Continuance (tithe framed 10%; covenant signature; staff membership within 30 days) is the closest fit. Cohesion (weekend, small group, Dream Team) and control (covenant, What We Believe, kids/students clearance) without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Mix: weekend invite; Newcomers; Activate; kids/students hook; Extensions; Purpose Driven book/campaigns in other churches (§0, §7). Network on Stark 1996 (open network). Religious-seekership maps as official product language — "turn seekers into reproducing disciples" (S16) — closer than WeWork or Occupy, still not the 1965 millenarian convert series.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** Packet §13 is observational, not a verdict. No communal living. Harvey blurred boundaries are not default for the modal weekend guest. Lifton milieu control is 1950s thought-reform (Ch.22). Critical press exists (CT, Guinness; S21–S22).

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Partial. Closer than Occupy or A.A. Farther than Apple/Peloton (covenant church, not a consumer-brand public only). Brand community is "a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand" (Muniz and O'Guinn 2001). Weekend + small group + Dream Team are interacting. Purpose Driven Life / CLASS / 40 Days are product-layer. Official talk is Jesus / five purposes / belonging, not Kozinets religion/myth as a finding.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Tarrow mismatch unless a cycle is shown. A cycle of contention is "a phase of heightened conflict across the social system" (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A living church and a portable church OS are not automatically that. Q5: continuing-practice default. SBC disfellowship is not scene death.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89). Continuance: weekend / Activate / Newcomers / baptism 0 USD; tithe framed 10% plus Beyond the Tithe; guest giving; Tithe.ly; staff membership within 30 days (§8, S9–S10). Cohesion: weekend 60–70 minutes; semester group + Talk It Over; Dream Team via SHAPE; Extensions (S4–S8). Control: signed covenant / Membership Agreement (body not published); What We Believe; kids/students clearance (S3, S8–S9, S12–S14). Closest of the three is continuance.

**Q2.** Investment is partial: 10% tithe-frame; $99 / $60 camp nonrefundable portions; unpaid Dream Team; recurring gift is editable/deletable (S10). Sacrifice is mostly unavailable: not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy (Table 1, p.95). Communion is partial: weekend + group + Dream Team — not communal property (pp.88, 110–116). Mortification is not codeable from a published covenant body; do not invent that text. Renunciation is mostly unavailable (pp.99–102): open weekend; no communal housing. Transcendence (pp.128–138) is mostly unavailable as institutionalized awe / insulated hierarchy.

**Q3.** Something expensive is present (time; 10% framing; unpaid hours; camp floors). Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost-shape plus later defense, not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices sit at Care Dream Team and at Celebrate Recovery as a named hub item whose program home was not opened (S7, S16). Do not invent the covenant body to code mortification.

**Q7.** Meaning claims stay labeled. About: yes to Jesus and his purposes; church for people who didn't like church (S2). Purpose Driven: five purposes; "turn seekers into reproducing disciples" (S16). Guinness 1992: seeker-friendly / felt-need as modernity irony (S22). Not a theology finding.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). First service 6 April 1980; 2026 pages still delivering (S1–S2). 1980–2026 is corporate/church span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance: strained if continuance is the only close fit; if sacrifice is tithe-frame / unpaid hours rather than Table 1; if the unpublished covenant is treated as her mortification; if 1980–2026 is treated as her duration.

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix: weekend invite; Newcomers; Activate; kids/students hook; Extensions; Purpose Driven book and 40 Days in other churches (§0, §7; S4–S8, S16). Proportion not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: weekend / Hub / Newcomers sell the room; Activate installs belonging-as-home; baptism, covenant, semester group, SHAPE / Dream Team deepen by role (§3, §7). Consistent with Stark 1996's belief-follows-attachment restatement.

**Q4 (partial).** Affective bond is present as Hub, Newcomers, Activate, group warmth. Intensive interaction is present for group / Dream Team regulars — not daily deployable-agent contact as 1965 defined it for the modal weekend guest. Religious-seekership maps as official product language (S16) — closer than Occupy / WeWork — still not the 1965 series; do not collapse the seeker noun into Lofland–Stark seekership as a complete finding. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted (open weekend; guest giving; no shunning doctrine; §13 items 3–4, 10). *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips' partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three, mainly inside group / Dream Team. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Weekend invite, Extensions, CLASS "thousands of churches," and 40 Days require outward ties (S4–S8, S16–S17). Membership / Extension-starter / staff 30-day clock are inward boundaries (S8–S9). Open-network at the free weekend door.

**Q7.** Mix as in Q1. The closer is the first weekend or first Activate hour (§7). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** Weekend / Newcomers / Activate / book are entry. Baptism, covenant, group, SHAPE / Dream Team, tithe-frame are retention rungs (§3, §7). About: baptized over 50,000 (S2). Wikipedia 2024: 15 campuses / 30,000 weekly (S19). About FAQ: 15 CA + five international + online (S2). Kids-page chips list a CA set plus Berlin (S13). Not reconciled. Hartford 2025 (Bird/Thumma, published 2026): ~1,850 U.S. megachurches; median in-person 2,805; $7.4M median income; 67% evangelical / 4.5% seeker (S20) — sector, not Saddleback's books.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey's opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost. No communal living. Weekend guests do not leave employer, housing, and social orbit as her default (§13 items 4, 10). What remains: signed covenant (body unpublished); staff clock; clearance; tithe-frame. Recurring gift is stoppable (S10). Blurred boundaries are separable from co-residence for the modal weekend guest.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show open weekend, walk-up Activate, guest giving, and critical press (S21–S22) without a closed-media system. Official About is silent on the SBC noun that CT 21 Feb 2023 reports (S2, S21). Do not score Lifton.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey's two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet's split is free weekend / Activate as environment-creating versus covenant + group + Dream Team + tithe-frame as life-claiming (§5, §13 items 2, 4, 7). The two senses look separable.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming "blurred boundaries → high exit cost" would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records a free door, guest giving, and a stoppable recurring gift (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal guest.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing consumer brand. Member / Saddleback family / Dream Team vs guest is the identity mark (§2, §6). Named official others: sin; self-help; a church people don't like (S2–S3, S16). External: CT 1994 Willow Creek variant; Guinness; SBC (S21–S22). Muniz and O'Guinn's definition is consumer-brand. Partial — closer than Occupy or A.A.; farther than Apple/Peloton.

**Q2.** Weekend + small group + Dream Team are interacting, not a brand public only (S4–S7). Purpose Driven Life / CLASS / 40 Days are product-layer (S16–S18). Willow Creek is not a second opened campus.

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Newcomers, Activate, Talk It Over, SHAPE, CLASS guides (S5–S7, S17). Covenant body unpublished.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official talk is Jesus / five purposes / belonging — this is a church, not his fan articulation, and not a theology finding (§1, S2, S16). Do not upgrade official worship language into Kozinets' device. Packet §13 does not decide the cult noun.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: member; Dream Team; SHAPE; Saddleback family (§2, §6). Recruiting: weekend invite; kids/students hook; Activate; Purpose Driven campaigns. Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** Not a cycle-of-contention case unless a cycle is shown. Tarrow's p.195 definition is not shown as a multi-sector phase. A living church and a portable church OS are not automatically that. Mismatch. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline of a periphery, and early-riser pattern (pp.196, 201–203) are contention-cycle terms and cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice default. (A) Saddleback Church is a living church: Andy Wood lead (arrived summer 2022); Stacie Wood named; 2026 pages still delivering (S1–S15). (B) Purpose Driven is a living product / portable church OS: CLASS downloads and Purpose Driven Life still sold/given (S16–S18). The packet can stand as a continuing-practice study of a living church plus a living product — not an ended scene, not Anderson's scene, not Tarrow's cycle. SBC disfellowship (CT 21 Feb 2023; S21) is not scene death. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley): Philadelphia rave/EDM. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case. Do not import.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule's plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Individual stop-attending / stop-tithing is askable: recurring gift is stoppable; guest status remains possible (S10; §13 item 10). Field collapse is not recorded. Hartford still counts a megachurch field (~1,850; S20).
