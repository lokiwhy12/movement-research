## Identity labels
- **devotee / bhakta / Vaishnava** — insider self; practitioner of bhakti
- **Hare Krishna** — outsider/public noun for the person and the movement
- **Prabhupada / Srila Prabhupada / Founder-Acharya** — founder reverence titles; Constitution locks exclusivity
- **initiated devotee** — post-diksha identity
- **aspiring disciple / taking shelter** — pre-initiation status
- **brahmacari / brahmacarini** — celibate resident student
- **grihastha** — householder congregant
- **sannyasi / Maharaja** — renunciate prestige address
- **Mataji** — respectful address for women devotees
- **Life Member / Life Patron** — paid patron identity (India-forward)
- **karmi** — non-devotee materialist (insider slang)
- **gurukuli** — second-generation boarding-school alumnus (crisis-era noun)
- **blooped** — historical slang for someone who fell away from full standards/temple life
- **Global Member** — Constitution affirmation-statement member (may be uninitiated)

## Rank / ladder labels
- feast guest → congregational regular → Global Member → aspiring disciple → harinam-initiated → brahman-initiated → departmental seva lead → temple president → sannyasi / diksa-guru → GBC / minister
- **zonal acharya (historical)** — post-1977 regional throne rank later reformed
- **diksa-guru vs siksa-guru** — initiating vs instructing authority split; Prabhupada permanent foundational siksa
- **temple president** — local operational rank
- **GBC zonal secretary** — geographic oversight rank
- **Life Patron card** — financial/prestige rank without requiring sannyasa
- **book distributor / sankirtan devotee** — historical status via collection/preaching score

The headline: **name + vows + seva visibility** do more ranking work than formal certificates; sannyasa and GBC are the hard prestige caps.

- **As It Is** — anti-interpretation claim for the Gita commentary brand
- **Founder-Acharya** — locked unique noun

- **Prasadam is transcendental** — food as doctrine

- **Books are the basis** — canon over living charisma (post-founder stabilizer)
- **No other way in Kali-yuga** — exclusivity couplet via harer nama verse citations
