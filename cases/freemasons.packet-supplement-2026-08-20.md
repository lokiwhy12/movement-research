## 1. What kind of case Freemasons / UGLE / Blue Lodge is, for each lane

One object, labeled. United Grand Lodge of England / regular Blue Lodge — living fraternal order. Duke of Kent Grand Master on the opened history page (installed 14 June 1967). Strategy 2022 and Beyond. 7,000-plus Lodges. Men 18+. Two women's Grand Lodges named and hosted (Order of Women Freemasons; Freemasonry for Women / HFAF), not collapsed into UGLE. Massachusetts is a labeled contrast, not a second opened UGLE. Prince Hall is named from Wikipedia only; official homepage was not opened (500).

**Commitment (Kanter).** Living fraternal order, non-residential. Not her 1780–1860 utopian sample. Success cut 25 years (Appendix p.260). Continuance (FAQ about 200 pounds; 2025 GL levies) is close. Cohesion (Lodge, Festive Board, officer year) is closer than megachurch weekend-only. Control (ballot; Tyler/closed door as official label; Information booklet) without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90). Sacrifice, renunciation, and transcendence mostly unavailable as she defined them. 1717/1813–2026 is institutional span, not member-commitment duration.

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Mix: web interest form written for the man who does not already know a member; proposer/seconder; Universities Scheme; special-interest Lodges. Network on Stark 1996. Proposer/seconder is network-weighted; official funnel also claims a no-prior-friend path. Religious-seekership does not map as the 1965 millenarian convert series. Supreme Being is an eligibility condition, not seekership.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** Packet §13 is observational, not a verdict. No communal living. Harvey blurred boundaries are not default. Lifton milieu control is 1950s thought-reform (Ch.22). Official privacy-not-secrecy and Bogdan secrecy-as-pedagogy stay apart. Critical press exists. Do not score Lifton/Harvey.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Partial. Closer than Occupy (durable Lodge + officer year + apron). Farther than Apple (fraternal order, not a consumer brand). Muniz and O'Guinn 2001 definition is consumer-brand. Lodge + Festive Board are interacting. Official talk is character / friendship / charity, not Kozinets religion/myth as a finding.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Tarrow mismatch unless a cycle is shown. A cycle of contention is "a phase of heightened conflict across the social system" (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A living fraternal order is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Q5: continuing-practice default. Decline, Lodge erasure, and a contested census are not an ended scene. ## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89). Continuance: FAQ about 200 pounds a year dues and other fees, local variations; 2025 GL dues 51 pounds Metro/Province VAT included; registration inclusive of Grand Lodge Certificate, Royal Arch exaltation and Grand Chapter Certificate 105 pounds exclusive of VAT; MCF 17 pounds per member (S6, S18). Lodge initiation-fee tables are not published on UGLE. Cohesion: Lodge, Festive Board, officer year. Control: proposer/seconder; summons print; ballot at a Regular Meeting (Rules 159–160); Tyler/closed door as official label. Closest is cohesion; continuance is close. Control without commune-ordering of existence.

**Q2.** Investment is partial: the 200-pound average; GL levies (S14). Lodge invoices unpublished. Sacrifice is mostly unavailable: not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy (Table 1, p.95). Communion is partial: Lodge + Festive Board — not communal property (pp.88, 110–116). Mortification is not codeable from unpublished interiors. Renunciation is mostly unavailable (pp.99–102): no communal housing. Transcendence (pp.128–138) is mostly unavailable as institutionalized awe / insulated hierarchy.

**Q3.** Something expensive is present (time; ~200 pounds; unpublished dining; officer year). Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost-shape plus later defense, not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Convictions disclosure and later reporting duty, plus Almoner welfare, are not her confession/criticism/probation (pp.122–125). Do not invent an oath or reconstruct a degree interior to code mortification.

**Q7.** Meaning claims stay labeled. Official: Integrity, Friendship, Respect, Service; older Brotherly Love, Relief and Truth. Bogdan: secrecy as ritual strategy / progressive-revelation pedagogy. Biella: intelligibility gap. AOM Scotland abstracts stay Scotland. Not a theology finding.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). 1717; 1813 Union; Duke of Kent installed 14 June 1967, still in office on the opened page. 1717/1813–2026 is institutional span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance: strained if continuance and cohesion are the only close fits; if sacrifice is dues/time rather than Table 1; if unpublished interiors are treated as her mortification; if 1717/1813–2026 is treated as her duration; if Lodge erasure is treated as an unsuccessful commune.
