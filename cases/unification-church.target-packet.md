## Executive summary

The Unification Church / Family Federation sold a portable **restoration product**: a systematic Christian-adjacent theology (*Divine Principle*) that reframes the Fall as a sexual/lineage catastrophe, casts Sun Myung Moon and Hak Ja Han as **True Parents** completing Jesus's unfinished family mission, and offers the **Holy Marriage Blessing** as the engrafting rite that moves a couple (and subsequent children) from "Satan's lineage" into God's. The adhesion engine was not only messianic charisma. It was a stack: Principle workshops → purity/celibacy until matching → founder-matched (later parent/self-guided) spouse → mass Blessing spectacle → indemnity labor (fundraising, mission, tithes) → Blessed Family identity → second-generation reproduction of the matching culture → political and media projects that convert religious destiny into public influence.

**Movement-DNA read (this pass):** FFWPU fused **systematic restoration theology**, **couple-as-messiah**, **arranged/matching marriage as salvation tech**, **indemnity as a portable sacrifice calculus**, **fundraising teams as devotion labor**, and **mass ceremony as social proof**. The successor face (Family Federation / Blessing & Family Ministry / MatchNet) keeps True Parents language, Divine Principle literacy, and Blessing as the core sacrament while shifting matching toward parent-supported / candidate-active processes and publishing readiness curricula with sticker prices. Japan-era **spiritual sales / donation-target** controversies and the 2025–2026 religious-corporation dissolution (Tokyo District → High Court → Supreme Court, June 2026) sit beside South Korea leadership legal cases — external public-record layer, not this packet's finding. Schisms (Sanctuary / GPF) show **succession fracture as competing True-Parent claims**. That split — **Blessing continuity + institutional crisis and amnesia contests** — is itself a durable mechanic.
