## Executive summary

The Oneida Community sold a portable **heaven-now product**: Christian Perfectionism (sinlessness possible in this life because the Second Coming already occurred in AD 70), **Bible communism** (property and family fused into one enlarged household), and a founder-centered apparatus of **mutual criticism**, regulated **complex marriage**, and later **stirpiculture** that recoded ordinary monogamy, private property, and exclusive parental love as selfishness — and communal belonging, industry, and self-improvement as the millennial exit. The adhesion engine was not “free love” alone. It was a stack: revivalist conversion → Putney family compact → flight and remake at Oneida → daily Big Hall meeting + criticism as government → industrial cashflow that proved the kingdom could pay its bills → (after founder flight and generational revolt) a joint-stock company that kept the factories and dropped the sexual theology.

**Movement-DNA read (this pass):** Oneida fused **realized eschatology** (kingdom already available), **total household communism**, **criticism as both therapy and government**, **sexual theology as anti-possessiveness tech**, **industrial proof-of-concept**, and — uniquely among famous 19th-c. utopias — a **successful corporate afterlife** (Oneida Ltd. silverware) that became the public brand while the commune’s radical nouns retreated into museum rooms. The successor face (Oneida Community Mansion House nonprofit; Oneida Ltd. as commercial cutlery brand) keeps the buildings, trap/silk/silverware myth, and educational framing while treating complex marriage / stirpiculture as historical exhibits. That split — **industrial continuity + selective sacral amnesia** — is itself a durable mechanic.

**Public record / public reporting (paraphrase, not our finding):** Putney-era adultery charges prompted relocation (Britannica / Wikipedia). 1851 Hubbard assault/battery case settled with payments (Wonderley via Wikipedia paraphrase). 1879: Noyes fled to Canada after warning of an imminent statutory-rape warrant (Wikipedia / VCU Social Welfare); complex marriage abandoned the same year; joint-stock reorganization followed (museum: amicable disband 1880; 1911 Britannica: stock transfer 1 Jan 1881 at $600,000 capital). Descendants burned many community records in 1947 (Wikipedia citing Wayland-Smith). **Needs counsel review. Not a legal conclusion of this packet. Sexual/reproductive/eugenic content remains observational paraphrase of public historical sources only.**
