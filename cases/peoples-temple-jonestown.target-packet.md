## Executive summary

Peoples Temple sold a portable **justice-and-belonging product**: a racially integrated Pentecostal-to-social-gospel church that became an **Apostolic Socialism** identity kit (communal property, “Father” as destiny center, Planning Commission discipline, bus-caravan recruitment), then a **political turnout machine** in San Francisco, then a remote **agricultural utopia** in Guyana framed as sanctuary from U.S. racism, fascism, and media “harassment.” The adhesion engine was not “Kool-Aid” alone. It was a stack: healing-revival spectacle → interracial belonging → communal asset surrender → exhaustion + loyalty tests → persecution narrative → White Night emergency drills → (Guyana) enclosed jungle logistics under armed perimeter.

**Movement-DNA read (this pass):** Jones fused **racial-integration charisma**, **Father Divine–style communalism**, **Latter Rain / Branham healing-revival recruitment tech**, **Marxist reframing of Christianity** (“Sky God” vs Divine Principle / socialism-as-God), **Planning Commission bureaucracy**, **political patronage**, and — in Guyana — **totalizing enclosure** (passports, loudspeaker saturation, White Nights, simulated poison loyalty tests) that terminated in the Nov 18 1978 mass death event Jones and inner circle framed as “revolutionary suicide.” The movement has **no living successor church**; the durable cultural residue is the idiom “drinking the Kool-Aid,” memorialization (Evergreen Cemetery), and the SDSU Alternative Considerations archive as the primary research commons. That split — **practice dead / phrase immortal / archive as afterlife** — is itself a durable mechanic.

**Public record (paraphrase, not our finding):** On Nov 18 1978, Temple security killed U.S. Rep. Leo Ryan and four others at the Port Kaituma airstrip; that evening, 909 people died at Jonestown (nearly all by cyanide-laced fruit drink; some injected; Jones by gunshot), with four more deaths at Georgetown headquarters, for a commonly cited total of **918**. Larry Layton was later convicted in U.S. federal court related to the airstrip attack (paroled 2002). Temple corporation dissolved 1979 after bankruptcy/receivership. **Needs counsel review. Not a legal conclusion of this packet.**
