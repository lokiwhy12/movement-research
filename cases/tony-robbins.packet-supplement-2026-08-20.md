## 1. What kind of case Tony Robbins / Robbins Research International is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** For-profit, non-residential live-event plus upsell-ladder: UPW, DWD, named Mastery rungs, Results Coaching, Platinum (§0, §3, §8). Kanter's opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Not that sample. Continuance (tuition; Day-2-only window; 24-month complete-or-forfeit; damages fee) is the closest fit. Cohesion and control run through an arena week without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Acquisition is a mix: books and clips; documentary; celebrity halo; SMS; free strategy session; friend testimony; four-referral ticket (§0, §7). Network entry on Stark's 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian convert series. Religious-seekership does not map for a modal first weekend.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet's high-control scan is observational, not a verdict (§13). No communal living requirement is found (§13 item 4). Harvey's blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default event condition. Lifton's milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). Cult language stays official silence versus Wikipedia / NBC / The Cut (§7 objection 10; S23, S26–S27), not this note's verdict.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Partial. Closer than QAnon or A.A.: a living seminar company with a founder-face branded service sits at the center. Brand community is "a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand" (Muniz and O'Guinn 2001). Arena, event community, Results Coach, and Platinum trips are interacting, not a brand public only. Official talk is secular transformation, not Kozinets' religion/myth as a finding.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is "a phase of heightened conflict across the social system" (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A living seminar company is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule's individual disengagement can be asked of Day-2 leave / stop-buying; field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: UPW GA **845** / virtual **495**; Day-2-only (or first-full-day BM) refund; 24-month complete-or-forfeit; **1995** damages on several contracts; Platinum **85k / 75k / 65k** (§8, S1–S2, S11–S14). DWD / Wealth / Fiji / Results Coaching 2026 lists unpublished — do not invent them. Cohesion: firewalker / DWD alum / Platinum; private event community (§6). Control: Day-2 clock after the ordeal; call-close; attribution loop if you stall (§4b; primitive 18). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: deposit and a date-specific seat put a stake; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); post-window non-refund is the nearest analogue (S12–S14). Sacrifice is partly codeable: hours, ordeal, tuition — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is partial: frequent four- or six-day contact (§5), not communal property. Mortification is partial: public coaching theater; DWD values/rules inventory; Day-2 desk (§5). Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical, pp.99–102). Official boundary includes couples at DWD and no live-in (§13 items 3–4). Transcendence as "institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday" (pp.128–138) is not codeable. Living operator (§13 item 1), not tradition-as-yesterday.

**Q3.** Something expensive is present. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense (stall means a deeper container; primitive 18), not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices sit in the ritual table (public coaching theater; values/rules inventory; Day-2 close). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing arrangements. Do not upgrade floor intervention to her mortification.

**Q7.** Identity-before-results, "decisions shape destiny," and *I Am Not Your Guru* as a positioning title are official meaning claims (§1, §2, §12). Slogan and operator authority, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: first led walk 1985, about 50 (S19); still selling on 2026 pages (§0, S7) — corporate/product span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance: strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable and communion never leaves the event week; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if book/clip plus first weekend alone produces belonging; if UPW-and-leave is common and unpenalized beyond money already spent.

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix: books and clips; documentary; celebrity halo; SMS; free strategy session; friend testimony; four referrals = a free UPW (Privacy 1095 vs 2026 sale 845 / retail 1195; §8, S17). Proportion not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: books and clips sell the myth; UPW installs ordeal and identity language; later rungs reveal rewrite and inner-circle rules (§3, §7). Consistent with Stark 1996's belief-follows-attachment restatement, extended to a secular syllabus.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions, affective bond is present as arena charisma and floor-coaching warmth (their "cult" names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as four- or six-day immersion — not daily deployable-agent contact. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured "acutely felt, enduring discrepancy." Religious problem-solving and seekership are mismatched (transformation, not church; §0). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted (couples work; no live-in; no sourced shunning doctrine; §13 items 3–4, 10). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips' partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Books, ads, SMS, celebrity, and the referral incentive require outward ties. Firewalker / Platinum marks raise a boundary; they do not close the network. Platinum no-solicitation (§6, S11) is an inner-circle rule, not funnel implosion.

**Q7.** Mix, weighted to media presence plus call-desk close. The closer above the flagship is a Personal Results Specialist (§7, S4). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. Book / clip / free session / virtual UPW is entry. Next-event, coach, Platinum are retention. Official 10 million / 100 million lines (S2) are marketing census, not a reconciled count.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey's opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Participants do not co-reside (§13 item 4). What remains: jargon; firewalker / alum / Platinum identity; public floor. Exit cost: after day 3 of purchase, cancel is generally closed except the in-event window; no-show and 24-month unused-tuition forfeit; **1995** damages on several contracts; complimentary/transferred dead; no official shunning doctrine on pages opened (§7; §13 item 10). Blurred boundaries are separable from co-residence for the modal first weekend.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show no-recording, personal-use materials, and UGC license to RRI (§5, S16) without a closed-media system. Critical press is abundant (S23–S27). That does not test the dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey's two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet's split is time-bounded event week vs coach / Platinum graph (§13 items 4, 7). The two senses look separable.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming "blurred boundaries → high exit cost" would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records leave-after-weekend paths, with exit penalties low-to-partial for the modal first-yes and higher once a 24-month contract or Platinum attaches (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal first weekend.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing seminar brand. Firewalker / DWD alum / Platinum Partner vs "settlers" is the membership mark (§4b, §6). Named official other: limiting beliefs; fear; autopilot; settler psychology (§0b, §12). Outsiders who use the cult label are a reputation surface, not the official enemy (§7 objection 10). Muniz and O'Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement. Askable, not forced as a complete finding.

**Q2.** Interacting formation, not a non-interacting brand public only. Arena, on-stage witness, private event community, Results Coach, Platinum trips: structured relationships among buyers of the same branded service. Public-only (clips, documentary) is top-of-funnel (§0, §6, S21).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Priming, next-event framed as care, four-referral incentive, and testimony (§4b; §7 step 9) thicken up the ladder. Not measured.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official talk does the reverse: experience over books; coaching is educational, not therapy (S11, S22b); *I Am Not Your Guru* as a positioning title (§12). Stigma-management by desacralizing. Cult is official silence versus Wikipedia / NBC / The Cut (S23, S26–S27). Not collapsed. Participant-side sacralizing talk is not decidable here.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: firewalker; DWD alum; Platinum Partner; portable priming / incantations (§2, §5). Recruiting: mediation plus interaction (books/clips plus referral / testimony). Room-first; virtual is a delivery format that excludes the in-person ordeal (§0, S2). Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** Robbins / RRI is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow's definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. A living seminar company is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline of a periphery, and early-riser pattern (pp.196, 201–203) are contention terms and cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. Robbins / RRI is a living company (2026–27 calendar still sells UPW, DWD, BM, Fiji, Wealth, Platinum; S7). The packet can stand as a continuing-practice study. Neither is Anderson's scene or Tarrow's cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case. Do not import them.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule's plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Day-2 desk, UPW-and-leave, and stop-buying the next container are individual (§4, §7). Field collapse is not observed (still delivering; §9).
