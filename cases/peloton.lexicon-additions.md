## Identity labels

- **Member / Membership Holder** — official paid-subscription nouns (Membership Terms)
- **Leaderboard name / username** — public screen identity; Privacy: always public
- **Peloton person / on the Bike** — vernacular self (Atlantic members)
- **Pelo-crush** — informal fandom noun for a beloved instructor (Atlantic; not official)
- **Favorite instructor** — official-adjacent loyalty anchor
- **Teammate / Team admin** — Official and member-made Teams
- **Century Club** — 100-class identity (member quote on official milestones article)
- **Road rider** — unofficial Facebook-tribe noun (Atlantic)
- **XXL Tribe / people of size** — unofficial inclusion tribe (Atlantic)
- **Instructor** — talent-caste, not a member rank; NY Mag: Hollywood-studio logic
- **All-Access household** — up to 20 profiles at one address
- **App Member** — single-user software identity (One or +)

## Rank / ladder labels

- browser / trial → App One (rationed cardio classes) → App+ → All-Access hardware Member → multi-hardware household → Team admin → Century / high milestone → studio rider / Homecoming pilgrim (studio and On Tour dollars not opened)
- **Rx-equivalent** is not a Peloton word. Rank is **output, Leaderboard place, badge count, streak, Team weekly stat**
- **Official Team** vs ordinary Team is a curation rank (instructor-led)
- **Club Peloton** — named in a related-article teaser; body not opened; do not invent tiers
- Instructor A-list vs everyone else is an external press rank (NY Mag: Rigsby / Arzon / Love vs departed mid-tier)

The missing log line is that **the Leaderboard name is both a handle and a rank surface**: Privacy keeps name, tags, badges, and in-class metrics public even when the profile is Private.

## Enemy / problem words

- friction / commute / parking / studio schedule (official implicit)
- lonely workouts / isolation
- inconsistency / motivation fade (milestones: habits beat motivation)
- generic gym anonymity
- “having to choose between convenience and community” (community-features, inverted)
- unused bike in the room (external / implied lock-in; not an official enemy)
- cult / religion charge (external Atlantic frame; official silence)
- pandemic hangover / overbuild (NBC; not official shop language)

Not official enemies: other gyms by name, unbelievers, people who scale.
