## 1. What kind of case QAnon is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** Distributed interpretation network: no HQ, no communal residence, no single membership desk (§0, stance). Kanter’s opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Not that sample. Continuance (time sunk in “research”; $0.99 app and $17 book; no HQ refund) is the closest public fit. Cohesion and control run through slogans, groups, and interpreter-clergy without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Acquisition is a mix: algorithmic discovery; influencer explainers; family/friend feeds; adjacent anti-elite / anti-trafficking / wellness / evangelical content; 2020 hashtag spike; Trump-rally visibility (§0, §7). Network entry on Stark’s 1996 restatement, not a millenarian series. Religious-seekership does not map for a modal first-decode (interpretation network, not church; evangelical mashup adjacent, S1 / S3).

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet’s high-control scan is observational, not a verdict (§13). No residential compound in HQ form (§13 item 4). Harvey’s blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default participant condition. Lifton’s milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). Cult language stays artifact silence versus Wikipedia / Brookings / Frontiers / ADL (S1–S3, S11; §7 objection 4), not this note’s verdict.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Mismatch as a consumer-brand community. Brand community is “a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand” (Muniz and O’Guinn 2001). The packet presents a distributed interpretation network, not a branded consumer good; no single priced product at the center (§0, §8). A distributed interpretation network is not automatically that. Artifact talk is sacred-war grammar, not Kozinets’ fan-side religion/myth.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A distributed interpretation network is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule’s individual disengagement can be asked of walk-away / platform-exit; field collapse of the interpretation grammar is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: drops **$0** to read; QDrops **$0.99**; book **$17** at NBC 2019 press time; other merch / donation dollars unpublished — do not invent (§8, S6–S7). Cohesion: WWG1WGA as belonging badge; baker / digital-soldier nouns; groups and livestreams (§2, §5–6). Control: no published code of conduct; “do your own research” as inverse of a QA standard; interpreter-clergy stabilize misses (§5, §9). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is partly codeable: time and opened small-dollar objects put a stake; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); no HQ refund is the nearest analogue (§8). Sacrifice is partly codeable: hours, date-waiting, family rupture — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is partial: channels and 2020 Saturday marches (§5), not communal property. Mortification is partial at most: “I was asleep” testimony (§5); do not upgrade every proof post. Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical, pp.99–102). No communal housing; family rupture is observed, not a housing rule (§13 items 4, 10). Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is not codeable. Oracle is anonymous; clergy distributed (§0, §13 item 1).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present (time, public decode, optional merch). Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense (misses as depth; leaving would waste years; §3, §7), not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (“I was asleep”; proof posts; r/ReQovery as inverse). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing Org arrangements. Frontiers comment-deletion (S3) is not her commune map.

**Q7.** Storm / Great Awakening / “trust the plan” are artifact meaning claims (§1–2; named, not dumped). Slogan and horizon authority, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: first attributed drop 28 Oct 2017 (S1, S11); 2024 PRRI still measuring a composite (S12) — network span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if a first decode alone produces belonging; if walking away is common and unpenalized beyond time and identity already spent (§13 item 10).

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix: feed, friend, rally, church, wellness account, Trump-rally merch (§0, §7). Proportion not quantified. Cold-door (aggregator / explainer / hashtag) is possible.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: first “this fits” decode; explainer attachment; baker / digital-soldier identity later (§3, §7). Consistent with Stark 1996’s belief-follows-attachment restatement. Artifact claims stay unverified.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions, affective bond is present as first-explainer / first-group warmth (their “cult” names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as recurring channel use — not daily contact that makes every verbal convert a deployable agent. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured “acutely felt, enduring discrepancy.” Religious problem-solving and seekership are mismatched for the modal first-decode (not church; mashup adjacent, S3). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is partial (family rupture / QAnonCasualties, S1; Frontiers walk-away line, S3). No live-in; no HQ shunning court (§13 items 4, 10). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips’ partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Algorithmic discovery, hashtag spike, rallies, and platform migration require outward ties. “Normies” / “sheeple” raise a boundary; they do not close the network.

**Q7.** Mix: cultural / media presence (explainers, aggregators), organized-group invitation (Facebook groups; rally orgs), and friend/family feed. Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary alone.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. First drop or explainer / $0.99 app / hashtag are entry. Baker status, admin work, merch, and advocacy after Q silence are retention (§7). PRRI 19% (S12) and NBC 3.5 million talking-about (S5) are stock / activity, not a reconciled census. Do not invent one. Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Participants do not co-reside (§13 item 4). What remains: lexicon; baker / digital-soldier / WWG1WGA; explainer attachment. Exit cost: no HQ dues; no sourced shunning court; social and identity cost (§13 item 10; S1). Blurred boundaries are separable from co-residence for the modal first-decode.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show counter-evidence routed into the cabal, and Frontiers comment-deletion in named cases, without a closed-media system (§13 item 5; S3). Critical press is abundant (S1–S12). That does not test the dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey’s two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet’s split is time-bounded first decode / lurker vs baker / admin / operator (§3, §13 items 4, 7). People can walk away. The two senses look separable.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost” would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records no compound and no HQ exit desk, with higher penalties once public decode, family rupture, and operator role attach (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal first-yes.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing brand. Baker / patriot / digital soldier vs sheeple / normies / cabal is the membership mark (§2, §6). Named artifact other: alleged cabal / deep state / captured media — not a competing consumer brand (§0b). External pages add cult rhetoric (S1, S2, S11). Brand-community as defined remains a mismatch: no branded good at the center.

**Q2.** Interacting community at groups / livestreams / rallies, not only a non-interacting brand public. Public-only (hashtag or aggregator without baker ties) is top-of-funnel (§0, §7). A brand-public reading of hashtag days does not make this a brand.

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Decoding for others and “digital soldier” mission (§5) thicken up the ladder. Not measured.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Artifact / interpreter talk is self-description of a claimed sacred war (Storm / Awakening — named, not dumped), not his fan articulation, and not this note’s religion finding. Cult is artifact silence (§7 objection 4) versus Wikipedia “has been described as a cult” (S1, S3). Not collapsed.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: WWG1WGA; rally merch; 17anon spelling (§2, §5). Recruiting: mediation plus interaction (groups, rallies). Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** QAnon is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow’s definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. A distributed interpretation network is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline of a periphery, and early-riser pattern (pp.196, 201–203) are contention terms and cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Post-drop afterlife of the oracle feed, with continuing-practice of the interpretation layer. Q posting stopped as a regular feed (ADL: 4,953 through 8 Dec 2020; 18-month silence; handful after 24 Jun 2022; S11). The packet records forks and continued talk: interpreter clergy absorbed silence (§5); devolution / Telegram / Jan 6 mythology (§7 objection 9; S1, S11). Default: not an ended scene. The packet can stand as a continuing-practice study of bakers / slogans / clergy after drops thinned, and as a post-drop afterlife of the tripcode feed. Neither is Anderson’s scene or Tarrow’s cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case. Do not import them.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule’s plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Watkins inauguration post (go back to your lives; S1), r/QAnonCasualties / r/ReQovery, and platform-exit after bans are individual (§7, §13 item 10). Field collapse of the interpretation grammar is not observed (PRRI 2024 still measuring; Telegram continuity; §9). Do not invent a census.
