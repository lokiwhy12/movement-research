Mechanics, causal chains, evidence, measurement, risk, mitigation

1) Precise ritualized container (protocolized intimacy)
- Causal chain: strict procedural script → reduces ambiguity in a taboo domain → makes practice teachable and repeatable → converts novelty into scalable product and social practice
- Evidence:
  - "The container is unusually explicit: nest setup, safeport statements, grounding pressure, glove/lube protocol, timer starts before genital contact, 15-minute stroke..." — /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md
  - "The ritual is the product. The precision itself is what allows the act to be repeated, taught, branded, and franchised." — /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md
- Measurement plan:
  - Signal 1: repeat practice frequency (sessions per month) among initial class attendees (expect moderate uptake if low friction).
  - Signal 2: coach training enrollment rate among repeat practitioners (expect conversion concentrated in high-engagement cohort).
- Risk severity: high (sexual domain + power asymmetries → exploitation risk).
- Mitigation: strict informed consent protocols, independent safeguarding, separate non-sexual pathways for credentialing, mandatory sexual-harassment training, and external complaint/advocacy channels.

2) Role asymmetry & dyadic scripting
- Causal chain: assigned roles + asymmetry → clear behavior expectations → lowers social ambiguity → creates strong dyadic bonding and reputational stratification (good partners vs bad partners)
- Evidence:
  - "Role asymmetry is structural, not incidental: clothed stroker, partially undressed strokee, fixed body positions, fixed script, fixed timing." — /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md
- Measurement plan:
  - Signal 1: partner recall and partner-request rates (how often people return to same partner; high rates indicate social stratification).
  - Signal 2: complaint incidence correlated to role assignment (monitor carefully).
- Risk severity: high (power asymmetry in intimate contexts is an abuse vector).
- Mitigation: make role opt-in, provide clear refusal pathways, enable rotating partners only with consent, mandatory reporting and third-party mediation.

3) Premium ladder & accreditation
- Causal chain: simple ritual + accreditation → professional certification market → significant revenue via high-ticket coach training and retreats → long-term operator layer
- Evidence:
  - "Public reporting described a very wide price ladder: lower-cost classes, higher-cost workshops, retreats, coach training..." — /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md
- Measurement plan:
  - Signal 1: revenue per cohort by tier; conversion dropoff by tier (expect steep dropoff but high ARPU at top tiers).
  - Signal 2: client satisfaction and safety-report incidence per tier.
- Risk severity: high (monetized intimacy increases exploitation surface).
- Mitigation: transparent curriculum, independent accreditation standards, refundable policies for safety issues, external oversight on coach behavior.

Ethically-usable takeaway (1 line)
- Convert somatic practice into teachable, low-risk rituals only if consent, rotating partners, and third-party safeguarding are baked into the design; never monetize access to intimate private data or use secrecy to gate progression.
