1) Compressed vocabulary
- Short description: Short branded phrases / distinctions that participants can repeat and use as cognitive handles.
- Why it works: Lowers cognitive friction to apply doctrine; creates portable badges of membership and simplifies social transmission.
- How to measure: % of participants using terms in social posts; share of follow-on sign-ups among users who use the vocabulary.
- Quick test: A/B test short-term course with branded distinctions vs neutral language; track mention rates and follow-on conversion over 30 days.
- Risk & mitigation: Low-medium risk; can become shaming language. Mitigation: teach nuance, non-pathologizing framing, opt-out language.
- Evidence examples:

  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md — "Compressed vocabulary... Memorable terms are portable doctrine."

2) Threshold ritual (initiation)
- Short description: First crossing event (intensive, weekend, Day 1) that creates a felt identity break.
- Why it works: Creates irreversibility, salience, and memory encoding; marks an identity boundary.
- How to measure: % of attendees who return after first ritual; follow-on purchases within 90 days.
- Quick test: Compare a timeboxed immersion vs spread-out sessions for new cohorts; measure retention/commit.
- Risk & mitigation: Medium-high (overwhelm). Mitigation: consent, opt-outs, mental-health referral.
- Evidence examples:
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/landmark-forum.target-packet.md — 3-day flagship description.
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-blueprint-v1.md — "the first threshold ritual".

3) Public witness / articulation
- Short description: Structured public sharing or declaration in front of others.
- Why it works: Social proof + reputation costs increase commitment; converts private change into public identity.
- How to measure: invites sent, social media posts, follow-on sign-ups from witness events.
- Quick test: Provide optional public-sharing slots vs private reflection; measure recruiting & retention.
- Risk & mitigation: Medium (shame on unmet expectations). Mitigation: explicit consent, debrief, aftercare.
- Evidence examples:
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/landmark-forum.glossary-and-protocol-v0.md — "Public articulation + witnessing (share, declare, be seen)."
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/alcoholics-anonymous.target-packet.md — "Testimony ritual: creates structured public witness."

4) Rehearsal loop / assignments that leave the room
- Short description: Homework or practice that forces participants to act publicly after the container.
- Why it works: Converts insight to behavior and distribution; early social proof accelerates spread.
- How to measure: assignment completion rate; secondary invitations generated by assignees.
- Quick test: Assign an outside-the-room task to a random subset; measure invites and follow-up actions.
- Risk & mitigation: Low-medium. Mitigation: make tasks optional, respectful, provide scripts for safe outreach.
- Evidence examples:
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/landmark-forum.essence.md — "Assignments convert insight into... recruitment/distribution."
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md — "Assignments that leave the room are decisive."

5) Progression ladder (certification / rank)
- Short description: Structured multi-step advancement (sashes, rungs, levels).
- Why it works: Creates aspirational goals, sunk-costs, and social hierarchy that encourages continued investment.
- How to measure: conversion rate between ladder steps; average spend and churn by step.
- Quick test: Introduce a visible intermediate credential vs no credential; measure enrollment to higher tiers.
- Risk & mitigation: High (caste dynamics, coercion). Mitigation: transparent criteria, competency-based assessment, appeal/complaint channels.
- Evidence examples:
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md — "The formal ladder... white sashes, yellow coach sashes..."
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/scientology.target-packet.md — "The Bridge turns belief into a visible progression system."

6) Visible status markers (badges, day counts, leaderboards)
- Short description: Publicly visible signs of status (sashes, PRs, Day counts, leaderboards).
- Why it works: Signals competence and belonging; creates comparison drivers that increase effort and content creation.
- How to measure: engagement of people who display markers vs those who don't; recruitment lift per visible marker.
- Quick test: Offer optional digital badges and track retention and referral behavior among badge holders.
- Risk & mitigation: Medium (shame, unhealthy competition). Mitigation: emphasize personal progress metrics, optional visibility, anti-shaming policies.
- Evidence examples:
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md — "Day counts act as status markers."

7) Confession / auditing (guided disclosure)
- Short description: Structured confession or counseling ritual used as a healing or accountability device.
- Why it works: Relieves secrecy, builds vulnerability-based trust, and creates institutional knowledge that can be integrated into identity change.
- How to measure: frequency of disclosure sessions, follow-on commitment after auditing, satisfaction/relief scores.
- Quick test: Offer optional one-on-one guided reflection vs group reflection; measure follow-on engagement and perceived helpfulness.
- Risk & mitigation: High (sensitive data, power asymmetry). Mitigation: avoid institutionalized confession as leverage, require independent oversight, legal review, counselling referrals.
- Evidence examples:

8) Secrecy / inner circles
- Short description: Level-gated secret groups that increase scarcity and perceived value.
- Why it works: Creates elite identity, scarcity, and loyalty; reduces outside scrutiny.
- How to measure: incidence of closed/secret nodes, attrition/complaint vs open paths.
- Quick test: (Historical analysis only) map complaints and opacity vs harm signals; do not implement secret tiers.
- Risk & mitigation: Very high (historically linked to abuse). Mitigation: DO NOT IMPLEMENT secrecy; legal review and transparent governance required.
- Evidence examples:

9) Payment gating / ascension fees
- Short description: Tiers gate progress behind increasing cost.
- Why it works: Sunk-cost and economic signaling increase commitment.
- How to measure: conversion at price points; complaint rates vs spend.
- Quick test: price elasticity experiments with cooling-off windows and transparent refund policies.
- Risk & mitigation: High (economic capture). Mitigation: transparent pricing, refund/transfer policies, consumer protection; independent complaint process.
- Evidence examples:

10) Daily micro-rituals / ordeal protocols
- Short description: Low-friction, high-frequency practices (daily checklist, workouts) that encode identity through repetition.
- Why it works: Habit formation, visible trace, low friction for social replication; creates cumulative identity change.
- How to measure: streak lengths, completion rates, correlation with retention.
- Quick test: implement a small daily habit vs control; measure 30/60/90-day persistence.
- Risk & mitigation: Medium-high for extreme ordeals (injury, disordered behavior). Mitigation: medical guidance, scaling, optional modes.
- Evidence examples:

11) Role asymmetry / scripted dyads
- Short description: Protocolized dyadic roles (one guides, one receives) with specific behaviors.
- Why it works: Reduces ambiguity, creates repeatable skill, and fosters intense dyadic bonding.
- How to measure: partner-return rates, partner-preference concentration, complaint incidence.
- Quick test: scripted dyad vs free-form pair practice; measure repeat attendance and satisfaction.
- Risk & mitigation: High (intimacy + power asymmetry). Mitigation: rotating partners, opt-in consent, third-party safeguarding.
- Evidence examples:

12) Self-diagnosis funnel (quiz → consult)
- Short description: Short self-assessment tools that move prospects into human conversations.
- Why it works: Lowers reactance; prospect self-identifies the problem; increases conversion quality.
- How to measure: quiz-to-consult conversion; cohort fit metrics.
- Quick test: embed a quiz vs static landing page; compare consult attendance and close rate.
- Risk & mitigation: Low-medium (overclaiming). Mitigation: clear limits, no medical claims, pathway to professional care.
- Evidence examples:

13) Operator / credentialing layer
- Short description: Formal training programs that create a reproducible operator layer (coaches, proctors, affiliates).
- Why it works: Institutionalizes culture and quality; enables scale via trained operators.
- How to measure: % of trained operators who remain active; incident reports per operator.
- Quick test: introduce a small credential and measure reproducibility of experience across nodes.
- Risk & mitigation: Medium (credential inflation, poor training). Mitigation: external standards, recertification, oversight.
- Evidence examples:
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/crossfit.target-packet.md — Level 1 certification.
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/landmark-forum.target-packet.md — certified leaders & volunteers.

14) Recruitment-as-mission / moralization
- Short description: Framing recruitment as moral duty rather than sales.
- Why it works: Lowers reluctance to recruit; creates zeal and social enforcement.
- How to measure: referral per member rate; message framing lift in referrals.
- Quick test: frame an invite as "helping others" vs neutral invite; measure conversion & long-term satisfaction.
- Risk & mitigation: High (moralization enables coercion). Mitigation: keep recruitment voluntary, transparent incentives, audit recruitment messaging.
- Evidence examples:
  - /home/ubuntu/.openclaw/families/loki/shared/docs/targets/movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md — "Recruitment is treated as mission and virtue" (NXIVM).
