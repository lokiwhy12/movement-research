## Executive summary

OneTaste sold a branded partnered practice, Orgasmic Meditation (OM), as attention-training / wellness / feminine-liberation rather than as a sex act. The adhesion engine was not the headline taboo alone. It was a stack: an unusually exacting container, compressed technical-sensory language, role-asymmetric dyads, communal witness, a steep paid ladder, and a founder-centric interpretive frame that recoded discomfort as growth.

Movement-DNA read (merged from the prior packet, the 2026-03-18 analysis, reaudit §3, and pages opened this pass): OneTaste fused **ritual tech**, **taboo-as-awakening**, **partner reputation as informal rank**, and **sexuality–status–money coupling**. The ritual *is* the product. Precision is what made a high-charge intimate act teachable, brandable, and franchiseable.

Self-named pass (reaudit §3 pulled into the body; “avoiding procedural detail” is retired as an extraction cap):
- The **container is an operating protocol**, not a vibe. Named non-negotiables (duration, clothing asymmetry, two safeports, no reciprocation, “nothing extra”) plus a standardized form. Extracted here at mechanic level. Not a reproducible script.
- **Role asymmetry is structural**, not incidental: clothed stroker / partially undressed strokee; fixed seats; fixed timer; fixed speech slots (offers/requests; one frame each).
- **Sexuality, status, and money are one engine.** Partner desirability, sales quota, communal housing, and course upsell sat on the same graph. Public reporting and later public-record charges describe the harder edge of that coupling.
- **Safeport / frames / nothing-extra** are doctrine, not etiquette garnish. They are how the system produces safety-feeling *and* how it recodes a request to alter the form as the practitioner’s problem.

Public record (paraphrase, not our finding): a 2025 EDNY jury convicted founder Nicole Daedone and former sales director Rachel Cherwitz of forced labor conspiracy; in March 2026 the court sentenced Daedone to 9 years and Cherwitz to 6.5 years, with ~$12 million forfeiture and about $890,000 restitution to seven victims (Guardian 2026-03-31; Wikipedia OneTaste, opened this pass). Defense has said it will appeal. The successor ecosystem (Institute of OM / Eros Platform) still sells OM, memberships, coaching, and an eight-month Connection Program. **Needs counsel review. Not a legal conclusion of this packet.**

## 5) Ritual stack + container

**Container (official, mechanic-level — paraphrased, not a script):**
- No reciprocation; both roles done for their own sake; no compensation.
- Fixed duration (15 minutes) with a late-session warning.
- Two named safeports at specified physical thresholds.
- Clothing asymmetry: stroker remains clothed; strokee undresses from the waist down.
- Nothing extra in the nest: no romantic gestures, added/subtracted steps, or time changes.
- Standardized nest as a prepared space; asking-form that forbids mixing the invitation with a date or a sex offer.
- Frames: one sensory sentence each, then exit.
- Official claim: these parameters *are* OM; genital contact without them is not OM.

**Form (official):** intention/behavior/technique inside that box. “Practice Forms” may vary technique under a trainer; they may not vary the container.

**Reaudit sentence kept:** the ritual is the product. Precision is what allows repetition, teaching, branding, and franchise.
