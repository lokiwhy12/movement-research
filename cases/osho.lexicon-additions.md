## Identity labels
- **sannyasin / neo-sannyasin** — initiated self; the portable convert noun
- **Rajneeshee** — press/Oregon-era outsider noun for the same people
- **Bhagwan / Osho** — founder titles across eras; first-name reverence culture
- **Ma / Swami** — gendered initiate honorifics (historical)
- **Zorba the Buddha** — ideal person: earthly pleasure + silence (official)
- **witness** — the observing self that survives catharsis
- **meditator / participant** — current resort base identity (no forced membership)
- **Living In** — residential immersant
- **Work as Meditation / WAM** — laborer-as-practitioner
- **facilitator** — Multiversity operator
- **Sheela** — lieutenant proper name that became a role-myth (“when Sheela spoke…”)
- **Peace Force** — commune security identity (Oregon)
- **New Man** — transcendent-of-convention destiny self (teachings summary)

## Rank / ladder labels
- day visitor → multi-day pass holder → Guesthouse guest → Living In (7/14/30) → WAM / Academy / Multiversity Plus → historical ashram coordinator → Sheela management / inner circle
- **maroon (day) / white (Evening Meeting)** — visual caste of the clock, not a belt system
- **sannyas name** is the real rank diploma; certificate culture is thinner than Scientology’s Bridge
- **drive-by proximity** was historical soft rank (who saw the master that day)
- **non-refundable Living In** is a financial rank lock
- **OIF / IP steward** is posthumous institutional rank, not charismatic succession

The headline: **color + name + daily spine** do more ranking work than formal degrees.

## Phrase-structure patterns (why they travel)
- **X is not Y** (“not an ashram,” “not a guru,” “not a membership”) — category jiu-jitsu
- **Catharsis then silence** — intensity justified by the quiet that follows
- **Remain a witness** — two-word law inside chaos
- **Zorba the Buddha** — compressed paradox as identity
- **The only thing to join is yourself** — anti-join joining
- **When Sheela spoke, she spoke for Bhagwan** — lieutenant ventriloquism (historical)
- **That was Oregon / that was Sheela** — era-scapegoat grammar in successor talk
- **Entry passes once sold can’t be returned** — sunk-cost as policy sentence
- **All payments for Living In are non-refundable** — same
