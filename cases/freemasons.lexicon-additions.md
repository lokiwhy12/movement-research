## Identity labels

- Freemason / Mason / Brother -- default in-group nouns (official).
- Entered Apprentice -- after First Degree / initiation (official What-is).
- Fellowcraft / Fellow Craft -- after Second Degree / passing (official).
- Master Mason -- after Third Degree / raising (official). Massachusetts contrast treats this as the highest degree.
- Companion -- Royal Arch after exaltation (official royal-arch). About 40 percent of UGLE take it.
- candidate -- pre-initiation; can cancel (Data Protection).
- Worshipful Master -- annual chair; East; rising sun (lodge-officers).
- Tyler -- outer guard (official + Bogdan + Wikipedia).
- Almoner -- internal welfare officer (official).
- Lewis -- named in Information booklet index; body of that entry not opened this pass. Do not invent a definition.

- enquirer / interest-form

- Deacon / Inner Guard / Secretary / Treasurer / Warden
- Worshipful Master (one year)
- Past Master
- Provincial / District / Metropolitan rank
- Grand Officer (Year Book; 2025 first-appointment fee 159 pounds ex VAT)

The missing log line is that the apron elaborates as the visible rank. Contested whether Royal Arch is a fourth degree or a completion of three (official Pure Antient Masonry includes it; Massachusetts says later bodies are supplemental).
