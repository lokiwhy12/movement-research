## 1. What kind of case 75 HARD is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** Free-core, self-administered program: a founder-defined rule set, optional paid kit, no communal residence, no local chapter (packet §0, §6, §8, §9). Kanter’s opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). 75 HARD is not that sample. Continuance (restart-to-Day-1; optional no-return digital SKUs) is the closest fit. Cohesion and control run through a private daily loop and founder text without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Acquisition is a mix: podcast, hashtag, friend before/after, official info page (packet §0, §7). Network entry on Stark’s 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian convert series. Religious-seekership does not map as written for modal Day 1.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet’s high-control scan is observational and contested, not a verdict (§13). No communal living requirement is found (§13 item 4). Harvey’s blurred boundaries — whole life entwined so leaving means leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default self-admin condition. Lifton’s milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22).

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Closer to a brand public than an interacting room. Brand community is “a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand” (Muniz and O’Guinn 2001). Official design is self-admin plus #75HARD continuity, not a structured in-person triad. Official talk is mental toughness / “not a fitness challenge,” not Kozinets’ religion/myth as a finding. Harley 1995 stays closed. Interaction mismatch is a finding.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). 75 HARD is a living self-administered program — not early-risers against a state, not a polity-wide cycle, not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule’s individual disengagement can be asked of quitters and restarters; field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: restart-to-Day-1; Phase 3 year-reset; downloadable software non-returnable (§3, §8, §10, S13). Cohesion: ambient hashtag and Day X speech, no official weekly congregation (§5, §6). Control: founder-as-doctrine, zero-compromise, intent-as-fail, FAQ as rule-oracle (§2, §13 items 1, 9). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is only partly codeable: core protocol $0 (§8, S1, S8); optional kit around it; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); downloadable-software non-return (S13) is the nearest money analogue; sunk days are not her financial stake. Sacrifice is partly codeable: hours, ordeal, weather, locked diet/alcohol — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is weak: no official frequent contact (§5 weekly cell); no communal property. Mortification is partial: restart event; “you cheated if you modified” (§5, §13 item 9). Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical services, pp.99–102). Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is not codeable. Founder voice is the method; no certified-leader path (§9, §13 item 1).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present (time, ordeal, late-program restart). Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense under critique (§7 step 9), not that sequence. Iannaccone’s price on outside activity: 1992 body still closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (restart event; Day 75 post). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing HQ arrangements. The participant is the judge (§9). Do not upgrade a private reset to her mortification.

**Q7.** “Mental toughness,” “Ironman for your brain,” and “war with yourself” are official meaning claims (§1, §2). Slogan plus founder-FAQ authority, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: introduced 2019; still selling in 2026 (§0, §12, S15) — corporate/product span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable and communion never leaves the hashtag; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if podcast-plus-hashtag invitation alone produces belonging.

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry runs through a mix: friend ties, previously unknown hashtag/podcast strangers, and the official info page (§0, §3, §7). Proportion is not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: intros sell toughness and a free start; later days install restart and LIVEHARD (§3, §7). Media- or friend-warmed attachment, then distinctions — consistent with Stark 1996’s belief-follows-attachment restatement, extended to a secular syllabus.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions, as the conversion note recovered them: affective bond is present as podcast/friend/hashtag warmth (their “cult” names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is not present as daily contact that turns a verbal convert into a deployable agent — the daily loop is self-administered (§6, §9). Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured “acutely felt, enduring discrepancy.” Religious problem-solving perspective and religious seekership are mismatched (mental toughness / program, not church; §0, §1). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted for modal starters (§13 items 3–4). *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond is more visible than intensive interaction (no room). The predisposing three are less visible. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Podcast, hashtag, and a $0 first-yes require outward ties. Language and unofficial contempt for modifiers can raise a boundary; they do not close the network (§6, §7).

**Q7.** Mix, weighted more to media/hashtag than a peer-only invite model. A friend before/after is a closer, not the only path (§0, §7). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. Podcast/hashtag is entry. Day X, paid kit, and LIVEHARD are retention. Completer-count lines are throughput with owned-page conflicts (§11). Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Participants do not co-reside (§13 item 4). What remains: jargon, Day-count identity, founder text. Exit cost is “sunk days, public reset, self-label as quitter”; no sourced shunning doctrine (§13 item 10).

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show founder text as canon and critics reframed as missing the point (S12) without a closed-media system (§13 item 5). That does not test his dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey’s two senses, as the NRM note states them: environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet’s split is a finite 75-day container vs LIVEHARD year / annual rerun (§3, §13 items 4, 6). People start and leave or reset. The two senses look separable here.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost” would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records leave-or-reset paths, with exit penalties low-to-partial (§13 item 10; §7). That undercuts the strong link for the modal starter.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing program brand. Finisher / “I don’t modify” vs modifier / unofficial 75 Soft is the membership mark (§4b, §6, §12). Named enemy: excuses, compromise, modification, waiting (§0b, §12). Muniz and O’Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement.

**Q2.** Official design is closer to a non-interacting brand public than an interacting brand community. Continuity is a hashtag and Day X posts, not structured in-person relationships among buyers (§5, §6). Public-only is the flagship. Unofficial peer threads exist; they are not a chapter.

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Finish-post and recruit/defend paths (§7 steps 8–9) thicken if someone evangelizes. No certified-coach ladder on pages opened (§8, §9). Not measured.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official 75 HARD talk does the reverse: program, tool, mental toughness, “not a fitness challenge,” a science-adjacent essay that says it is “not a peer-reviewed fitness protocol” (§1, S12). Whether any participant-side sacralizing talk exists is not decidable from this packet.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: Day X speech, zero-compromise vocabulary, optional patches/shirts (§2, §6). Recruiting: media/hashtag-primary with friend support. Largely-online public, not room-first (§0, §5). Do not treat Muniz and O’Guinn as a how-to.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** 75 HARD is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow’s definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. A living self-administered program is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization and institutionalization (pp.201–202) are contention terms. Differential decline of a periphery (pp.202–203) is a cycle-demobilization claim. Early-riser pattern with opportunity held constant (p.196) cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. Not high-fail/restart as a rates reading. 75 HARD is a living distributed program (2026 pages still sell the free protocol, app, book, LIVEHARD; S1–S14) and has not ended. The packet records restart as a mechanic, not as published rates: “What they do not publish: completion rate, median streak, injury rate, dropout” (§11). Origin show notes say “designed for failure” (S15) — a design claim, not a fail-rate. Completer-count conflicts are not a dropout series (§11). Not “one ended scene.” Not Anderson’s scene. Not Tarrow’s cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule’s plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Restart and quit are individual (Day 1 reset; unofficial 75 Soft as a leave-path, §12, S17). Field collapse is not observed. Restart is not field collapse.
