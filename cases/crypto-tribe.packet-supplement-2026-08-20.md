Do not extract Ethereum. Opened Wikipedia / CCAF stay weather, not either layer.

**Commitment (Kanter).** Not her 1780–1860 utopian commune. Non-residential; no owner. Continuance: self-custody irreversibility and HODL-as-ordeal are interpreter / official-adjacent costs, not her property stake — lost keys are not a how-to and not a financial finding. Sacrifice / renunciation / transcendence: HODL crash-as-test and hyperbitcoinization eschatology are interpreter meaning claims, not her Table 1. 2009–2026 is institutional span, not her 25-year member-commitment cut. Price is not a product price.

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Mix — official education; orange-pill as a label, not a script; meetups / conference; Wikipedia weather. Network-weighted (who showed you). Not the 1965 millenarian convert series even though hyperbitcoinization is eschatological interpreter copy. Extra-cult neutralization contradicted (you can sell; official high-risk / never expect to get rich). *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** Packet §13 observational, not a verdict. No communal living. Harvey blurred boundaries not default for a public protocol. Lifton milieu control is 1950s thought-reform (Ch.22). Critical press exists (Wikipedia economists; CCAF). Do not score. Do not dump the whitepaper body.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** PARTIAL. Closer than Occupy for (B) (HODL / orange-pill / not-your-keys as marks; dual enemy).
