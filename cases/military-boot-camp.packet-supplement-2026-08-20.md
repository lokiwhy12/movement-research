## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Continuance, cohesion, and control appear as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89). Continuance: official pay from arrival; meals and uniforms; DEP $0 until ship; service obligation (DD Form 4 body not opened). Cohesion: platoon; official Crucible as teammate-or-fail; EGA as name. Control: DI as local total authority; isolation (one arrival call, then letters; Family Day only); UCMJ named on Wikipedia receiving-bus, not adjudicated. Closest: cohesion and control in-depot; continuance close as employment-plus-obligation. Not her 1780–1860 commune-ordering of existence.

**Q2.** Sacrifice closer than Freemasons (official Crucible deprivation; Receiving haircut/kit) — still her unit (p.91), not a how-to. Investment: pay from arrival, not her property stake (p.96); no tuition on opened pages. Renunciation partial and time-bounded: 13-week isolation; DEP lives at home; Family Day as witness. Communion close (shared work, platoon, Crucible interdependence) — not communal property (pp.88, 110–116). Mortification closer than Freemasons as former-identity exchange (haircut; Wikipedia "this recruit") plus probationary socialization — her units (pp.122–125), not a score. Transcendence partial (DI; Commandant birthday message via Lawniczak) without upgrading Lawniczak fusion.

**Q3.** Expense is present (time; body; isolation; unpaid DEP up to 365 days; service obligation). Her path: sacrifice raises psychic expense so members justify staying by belief (p.91). Packet shows cost-shape plus official title-as-prize, not that sequence as measured. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Wikipedia moment of truth; official injury/recycle one-call; Wikipedia recycle / MRP / PCP / STC. Not her confession/criticism/probation (pp.122–125). Third-person and DI surveillance are labels, not her mutual-criticism sessions. Do not reconstruct interiors to code mortification.

**Q7.** Meaning claims labeled. Official: Honor / Courage / Commitment; Semper Fidelis; title earned, never given. Lawniczak: identity fusion; will to fight; transgenerational kinship — his claim. Wikipedia/Military.com transformation geography (Iwo Jima replica / The Reaper) is reporting, not official recruiting copy.

**Q8.** Success cut 25 years; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). 13 weeks is cohort duration, not her cut. 10 Nov 1775; PI 1915 / SD 1923 (Wikipedia); motto 1883 (official FAQ) is institutional span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Strained if 13 weeks = her 25-year success; if Corps span = member duration; if UCMJ is a legal finding; if Army ~90% BCT is borrowed as USMC; if Crucible census is reconciled; if Lawniczak fusion is upgraded; if DEP walk-away is ignored when coding renunciation.
