## Identity labels

- firewalker / someone who walks on fire — ordeal identity after UPW (official origin blog; go-UPW)
- UPW alum / Unleash graduate — first-rung belonging (official FAQ: graduates say the time was worth it)
- DWD participant — deeper identity rewrite (official DWD)
- Platinum Partner — paid inner-circle noun; terms say partner is generic, not a legal partnership
- Results Coach / Personal Results Specialist — operator vs closer (official coaching page; event FAQs)
- settler vs someone who will not settle — DWD FAQ out-group / in-group
- peak-state person — state as identity (what-is-UPW Triad)
- raving fan — relationship/business output noun (DWD FAQ; official relationship blogs)

## Rank / ladder labels

- virtual / General Admission / Executive (promo upgrade) / VIP / Diamond — UPW seating-as-status (go-UPW)
- DWD Diamond Upgrade (plus 2000 dollars, nonrefundable) — terms
- Mastery University session names: Life Mastery / Date With Destiny / Wealth Mastery — terms allocations 25/50/25
- Business Mastery / Advanced Business Mastery — calendar
- Leadership Academy / DWD Leadership / Wealth Mastery Leadership / Life Mastery Leadership — Platinum terms list
- Results Coach client → Business Results trainee → Platinum Partner
- Referral recruiter (four names → free UPW, Privacy 2020 values the ticket at 1095)

The missing log line is that seating class is both a price and a proximity rank: Diamond is front row plus private entrance; Platinum is a section of the main room and a trip graph. Rank is not a colored belt. It is how close you sit to the operator and whether you have a next-event noun.

Rx-equivalent is not a Robbins word. Rank is ticket class, event noun, and whether you have a coach or a Partner badge.
