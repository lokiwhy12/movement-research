## Executive summary

Tony Robbins (Anthony J. Mahavoric, b. 1960) operates a stadium-scale personal-and-business transformation business through Robbins Research International, Inc. (RRI; San Diego PO box on official terms). The adhesion engine is not a book. It is a long-day immersive room: music, movement, incantation, public coaching theater, a branded Firewalk ordeal at Unleash the Power Within, and a visible next-event ladder (Date With Destiny, Business Mastery, Life / Wealth / Life and Wealth Mastery Fiji, Leadership, Platinum Partnership). Official 2026 copy sells UPW as four days that change how you see yourself, with identity change treated as the precondition of behavior change. Date With Destiny is sold as six 12-plus-hour days that rewrite beliefs, values, and rules. Higher rungs convert the heat into business maps, money psychology, Fiji immersion, one-to-one coaching, and an 85000-dollar first-year inner circle (Platinum terms page, opened).

Movement-DNA read from pages opened this pass: the system manufactures a felt crossing (firewalk or board-break; public breakthrough; Day-2 I-am-different), then treats that feeling as proof that the next, more expensive container is the rational next step. List prices sit in the open at the entry event. From DWD upward, marketing pages often hide the dollar behind a Personal Results Specialist call, while contract pages publish payment-plan deposits, damages fees, and Diamond upgrades.

Layer split (do not collapse):

- Official self-description (opened): science-backed immersive events; Stanford Snyder Lab / Stanford School of Medicine percentages on UPW and DWD pages; Firewalk as a supervised fear-breakthrough; Day-2 afternoon satisfaction guarantee; virtual option that excludes the firewalk and substitutes a board-break; 100 Billion Meals Challenge attached to every ticket; celebrity and presidential coaching halo; Platinum as travel plus coaching plus waived event fees.
- External reporting (opened): Wikipedia (pointer-then-open) for origin, 1995 FTC franchise settlement, 2012/2016 firewalk injury cluster, 2016 I Am Not Your Guru, 2019 BuzzFeed allegation cycle and later SuperCamp claim, 2025 Irish-suit discontinuance. NBC 2016 Dallas firewalk (Dallas Fire-Rescue: 30-plus treated, five hospital; RRI: five of 7000 off-site, 911 call erroneous). NBC 2019 and The Cut 2019 covering BuzzFeed year-long investigation (misconduct and on-stage berating of abuse-survivors; Robbins denial via Medium, body 404 this pass). BuzzFeed original URLs 404 this pass — treated as not opened.

This is a charisma-centered commercial ascension system with a public ordeal, a short refund window inside the event, and a contract stack that converts non-completion into damages fees and forfeited tuition. Mechanics extract. Official voice and external documentation stay labeled.
