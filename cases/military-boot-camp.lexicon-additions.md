## Identity labels

- prospect — official become/FAQ: the person considering.
- poolee — official DEP: enlisted, not yet shipped; unpaid.
- recruit — official: the person in the 13 weeks before EGA. Wikipedia: this recruit as the required self-noun.
- Marine — official: the title after EGA; first time the noun is allowed as a self-name (official recruit-training page).
- The Few / The Proud — Terms of Use: registered mark. Official identity of scarcity.
- brotherhood — official benefits/FAQ. Scholarship: kinship / functional equivalence, not a club.
- Hollywood Marine — Wikipedia depot-rivalry insult for San Diego graduates. Secondary.
- Devil Dogs — official FAQ mascot origin after WWI nickname.
- Soldier — Army contrast noun (GoArmy). Do not collapse into Marine.

## Rank / ladder labels

Official enlisted ladder is not fully printed on pages opened; Wikipedia names recruit billets as temporary.

- prospect / lead-form filler
- poolee (DEP, up to 365 days)
- recruit (Receiving through Crucible)
- Wikipedia billets (DI-chosen, revocable): platoon guide, squad leader, scribe, house mouse, whiskey locker, Prac/Knowledge
- Marine (EGA onward)
- Private / E-1 (GoArmy money page names Private E1; Marines.com salary page names rank-based pay without the E-1 noun on that page)
- Private E-2 (GoArmy)
- drill instructor / hat (Wikipedia: campaign hat; three or more per platoon)
- series commander (Wikipedia: post-Ribbon Creek safety layer)
- company commander (Wikipedia: usually a captain)
- officer / OSO / OCS — parallel caste (official become). Not this packet's exemplar.

The missing log line is that the noun Marine is itself the rank claim. Contested whether hat is rank or a job.

## Ritual / action verbs

Phrase entries are labels, not scripts.

- earn the title — official. Binary completion verb.
- ship — official DEP: leave for recruit training.
- receive / Receiving — official parents FAQ: 3-5 day threshold.
- form on the yellow footprints — Wikipedia / Lawniczak. Threshold verb. Not a how-to.
- strip / issue (kit, haircut) — official receiving list, paraphrased.
- drill — official + Wikipedia close-order.
- recycle / drop — Wikipedia holding-platoon verbs. Not a how-to.
- crucible / go through the Crucible — official ordeal verb.
- award the emblem / earn the EGA — official + Military.com.
- call them Marine — Military.com: first use by the DI.
- graduate — official closure.
- Semper Fi — motto compression; official Always Faithful. Label, not a greeting script.
- orange-pill analog in this system: there is none. The conversion verb is earn, not convince.

Do not treat these as instructions. They are observed labels. Do not transcribe cadences, the DI Creed, or the Rifleman's Creed as scripts.

## Enemy / problem words

- excuses (official recruit-training: left behind)
- quit / surrender's pull (official; brochure pointer not fully opened)
- civilian self / unused potential (official benefits: corporate ladder without moral courage)
- other-branch basic as foil (official: stands apart in length and intensity)
- Army as insult-foil in historical DI speech (Wikipedia 1954 Popular Mechanics). Official Marines.com pages opened this pass do not print that insult.
- weak hands analog: the recruit who cannot carry a teammate (official Crucible: individual strength is not enough)
- maltreatment / abuse — Wikipedia incident file, not an official recruiting enemy-word
- cult / fad — official silence. Scholarship names fusion. This packet does not decide the nouns.

Not official enemies as a named caste: families. Official pauses them until Family Day rather than naming them as the problem.
