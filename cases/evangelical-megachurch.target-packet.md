## Executive summary

The evangelical megachurch model, as opened through Saddleback Church and the Purpose Driven product stack, is a seeker-sensitive adhesion system that converts a low-friction first weekend (casual dress, 60–70 minute service, kids check-in, a Hub greeter, no ticket) into a signed membership identity, a small-group semester, a SHAPE-placed volunteer role, and a tithe framed as 10 percent plus a “Beyond the Tithe” ask. The movement-DNA read is not a verdict on evangelical doctrine. It is a persuasion and retention system that makes belonging the product and the weekend the showroom.

Official 2026 Saddleback pages still run the same grammar the 1980 origin story named: a church for people who didn’t like church; urgently lead people to say yes to Jesus and his purposes; Activate as a one-hour first-step experience (booklet and pen provided; walk-ups welcome; kids childcare on campus); membership defined as trusted Jesus, baptized by immersion after salvation, completed Activate 1, and signed a membership covenant / Membership Agreement; small groups on a semester calendar with Talk It Over tied to the weekend message; Dream Teams placed by SHAPE (Spiritual Gifts, Heart, Abilities, Personality, Experiences); giving page that treats the tithe as 10 percent and anything above that as “Beyond the Tithe,” with guest giving allowed and Tithe.ly as processor; extensions as unfunded home-or-venue watch-parties that only a member may start. Lead pastors named on the About page: Andy Wood (lead, arrived summer 2022) and Stacie Wood (named on the same leadership block). Founders named: Rick and Kay Warren; first service 6 April 1980; “baptized over 50,000 people” (About). Campus count on the About FAQ: 15 locations in Southern California and five international campuses, plus online. Kids-page campus chips list a Southern California set plus Berlin. Wikipedia 2024 census: weekly attendance 30,000 and 15 campuses. Those figures are not collapsed.

Purpose Driven, opened on purposedriven.com and rickwarren.org, is the portable export of the same ladder. Official About: five purposes — planned for God’s pleasure (worship), formed for God’s family (fellowship), created to become like Christ (discipleship), shaped for serving God (ministry), made for a mission (evangelism). Official About Pastor Rick: Purpose Driven is “a simple path of discipleship to turn seekers into reproducing disciples” that trains churches to introduce attendees to membership and move members into progressively deeper commitment; “If you will concentrate on building people, God will build the church.” CLASS 101–401 remains a free downloadable church-in-a-box on rickwarren.org (101 church family / 201 maturity including tithing as one of four spiritual habits / 301 ministry / 401 life mission). Live Saddleback no longer markets CLASS on the pages opened this pass; it markets Activate, with careers naming Activate 1 “Find Belonging: How to Belong” and Activate Step 3 “Make a Difference.” Activate 2 is **not named** on pages opened. The current membership-covenant body is **not published** on saddleback.com pages opened this pass — only the fact of a signed covenant / Membership Agreement. Do not invent the live text. A 1990s Baptist Press first-person by Warren **timed out** this pass and is not used as an opened source.

External layer, opened: Hartford Institute *Megachurch Resurgence* (Bird/Thumma, 2025 survey published 2026) — about 1,850 U.S. megachurches, roughly one in six Protestant worshipers, 2025 median in-person attendance 2,805, median income $7.4 million, 67% multisite, 94% treat small groups as central, 67% self-label evangelical and only 4.5% self-label seeker; attractional invite culture still correlates with growth. Christianity Today 21 Feb 2023: SBC Executive Committee deemed Saddleback “no longer in friendly cooperation” after Stacie Wood as teaching pastor and the 2021 ordination of three women. Ligonier (Guinness, 1992): seeker-friendly / felt-need megachurch as a modernity irony. CT 18 Jul 1994: Willow Creek as the then-prototype of the seeker-sensitive megachurch (labeled variant; 1994 attendance and budget figures are that article’s, not 2026 Saddleback). Religion News Service 21 Feb 2023 **Cloudflare / not opened**. NPR 14 Jun 2023 SBC appeal **timed out**. Current Saddleback financial statements, a live Purpose Driven conference fee, and a 2026 CLASS paid SKU **not published** on pages opened.

Movement-DNA read: the model is a high-adhesion living-religion system whose flagship weekly ritual is the staged weekend service, whose midweek container is the small group, whose escalation ladder is contact → weekend → Newcomers Connection → Activate 1 → baptism → signed covenant → group → SHAPE / Dream Team → tithe / Beyond the Tithe → extension or mission, and whose portable product is a five-purpose operating system plus a 40-day book campaign. The durable mechanics are (1) a felt-need front door that removes churchy friction, (2) a one-hour belonging class that ends in a signature, (3) a semesterized small-group machine locked to the weekend talk, (4) unpaid Dream Teams that convert attenders into operators, (5) a percentage-of-income gift framed as worship plus an extravagance ask, and (6) a founder-authored curriculum that lets other churches clone the ladder without cloning the founder.

## 0b) Environmental conditions (why now / why it scaled)

3–7 conditions to demand to promise:

1. Post-1960s U.S. church boredom and a suburban population that would name “boring / always asking for money / not for my kids” as reasons they quit — demand for a service that feels like a showroom, not a courtroom — official About: a church for people who didn’t like church; FAQ: casual dress, 60–70 minutes, kids programming. Wikipedia layer (not official About): Warren’s 1979 neighborhood probe. External CT 1994 Willow Creek variant: same “why did you stop?” door survey, labeled as that church’s origin, not Saddleback’s.
2. Meaning collapse and a self-help shelf that tells people to look inward — demand for a purpose that is given, not invented — official Purpose Driven About: the basic question is why am I here; start with God, not desires and dreams; five purposes. Store: existence / significance / purpose as the three questions of a 42-day journey.
3. Isolation in postsuburban Orange County and later in every multisite zip code — demand for a weekly village that does not require a prior church identity — official: Newcomers Connection names parking / kids / who-do-I-sit-with as the first fears; small groups “life is truly better together”; Activate “make Saddleback your home.”
4. Volunteer-surplus professionals who want significance, not another committee — demand for a named gift-inventory that places you on a team this month — official SHAPE + Dream Team; CLASS 301 “pathway to real significance”; careers “more than a career, it’s a calling.”
5. Founder-scale charisma that other churches cannot hire — demand for a cloneable OS — official CLASS “thousands of churches”; Purpose Driven “trains churches how to introduce attendees to membership”; 40 Days of Purpose as a nationwide campaign. Hartford: megachurches as “laboratories of innovation” whose practices (multisite, residencies, digital, discipleship) spread.
6. Denominational distrust plus a wish for orthodox-enough branding — demand for Baptist-shaped doctrine without a Baptist gate in 2026 — official What We Believe (Trinity, inerrant Bible, salvation by faith not works, eternal security, marriage as one man / one woman from birth sex). ## 3) Transformation sequence (the protocol)

Numbered sequence from pages opened this pass. Goal / mechanism / expected emotional state.

1. **Finding / first weekend**
   - Goal: a person in the room who does not already like church.
   - Mechanism: invite culture (Hartford: attractional invite still correlates with growth); locations finder; online service; casual-dress FAQ; Hub greeter; kids check-in; 60–70 minute service.
   - Emotion: low threat, relief, evaluation.

2. **Newcomers Connection**
   - Goal: answer parking / kids / who-do-I-sit-with so the person returns.
   - Mechanism: after-service staff conversation; name small group, serving, and kids safety as the next menu.
