## Executive summary

Scientology is a new religious movement whose official pages present spiritual progress as an exact technology: auditing and training on a named chart (the Bridge to Total Freedom / Classification, Gradation and Awareness Chart), with Hubbard's written and recorded words as scripture, Religious Technology Center as final arbiter of orthodoxy, and Church of Scientology International as mother-church management. The movement-DNA read is not a theology debate. It is a persuasion system that converts self-diagnosis into a paid (donation-gated) ladder, then keeps that ladder alive with confession-under-measurement, a compressed lexicon, an ethics/suppressive-person frame, and an elite operator caste (Sea Org) whose public pledge is framed as a billion-year symbolic commitment.

Official pages opened this pass still run the same grammar in 2026: no variables in the technology; each process exact; one walks the chart and becomes free; donations attach to auditing and training (not a tithe); congregational services and a Free Scientology Center are the no-donation front door; the Oxford Capacity Analysis / personality test is the free on-ramp on almost every FAQ page; disconnection is last-resort / self-determined / not required for mere difference of belief; auditing files are sacrosanct; Sea Org members live communally, work long hours, receive an allowance, and sign the symbolic pledge. Exact current dollar prices for auditing intensives, training courses, OT levels, IAS membership, or Flag stays are **not published** on official pages opened this pass. Do not invent them.

External pages opened this pass (Wikipedia Scientology; Wikipedia Going Clear; LAT review of Wright's *Going Clear*; Urban's Princeton publisher page; New Yorker 2008 Château piece thin; New Yorker 2011 Apostate paywall-thin) document the other layer: fees that can run to tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars (LAT paraphrases Wright at as much as 300,000 USD to attain enlightenment); OT materials treated as confidential / leaked; disconnection and fair-game as control mechanics; Sea Org labor and discipline controversies; membership-size contest (official scale vs a 2014 estimate around 30,000 on Wikipedia). Those are externally documented or reported claims. The Church contests many of them. This packet does not collapse the layers and does not write a legal finding.

The prior dossier named ten portable primitives and left scores, the 8-type ritual table, the 1-to-9 map, objections-as-the-system-answers-them, section 13, and this-pass numbered sources missing or in the old shape. Those holes are filled below from opened pages, or marked honest remaining unknown. Founder / Bridge / Sea Org / RTC / disconnection / ethics beats are in this packet because those official pages were opened this pass.

Movement-DNA read: Scientology is a technical-religion identity system whose flagship ritual is the audited session, whose daily container is the course room / ethics / conditions language, whose temple is the org / mission / Flag / Freewinds, and whose escalation ladder is free test / intro course -> donated auditing or training -> Clear -> advanced / OT orgs -> staff or Sea Org. The durable mechanics are (1) a tiny lexicon that recodes suffering as charge / reactive mind / suppression, (2) a visible named ladder, (3) confession plus a measuring device, (4) donation as the doctrine-of-use (those who use most, pay most — official funding page), (5) orthodoxy enforcement via trademarks and RTC, and (6) a critic frame that pre-labels hostile information as suppressive.
