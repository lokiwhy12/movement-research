## 1. What kind of case Burning Man is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** Living high-cost pilgrimage culture: temporary city plus year-round regionals; ticket + Survival prep + camp labor + MOOP as membership tests (§0, §5, §8). Kanter's opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Not that sample. Continuance (non-refundable ticket; TAP $250; no BMP refund) is the closest public fit. Cohesion and control run through camp and terms without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Acquisition is a mix: word of mouth; JRS; Journal; regional; camp invite; Stewards; ticket sales; Ticket Aid (§0, §7). Official: little mass-media solicitation (S5). Network entry on Stark's 1996 restatement, not a millenarian series. Religious-seekership does not map for a modal first-timer (official: not a festival; cult noun silent; S7, §7 objection 10).

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet's high-control scan is observational, not a verdict (§13). No year-round communal living (§13 item 4). Harvey's blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default participant condition. Lifton's milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). Cult language stays official silence versus Vox / Wikipedia (S18–S19), not this note's verdict.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Partial. Brand community is "a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand" (Muniz and O'Guinn 2001). Official pages present a participatory city and a principle-set, not a branded consumer good (S1, S7). A festival plus year-round regionals plus a gift economy is not automatically that. Camp, burns, and regionals are interacting, not a brand public only. Official talk is anti-festival culture, not Kozinets' fan-side religion/myth.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is "a phase of heightened conflict across the social system" (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). An annual gathering plus year-round regionals is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule's individual disengagement can be asked of one-and-done / stop-going; field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: 2026 ticket $550–$3,000 + tax/fees; VP $165; TAP $250; all sales final (S3, S5). Camp dues and gear unpublished — do not invent. Cohesion: theme camp; Greeters; twin burns; regionals 106 / 36 / 34 (S14). Control: Ticket Terms + Survival as contract; LNT; revoke for scalp or commercial activity (S5). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: ticket and prep put a stake; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); no BMP refund is the nearest analogue (S5). Sacrifice is partly codeable: desert ordeal, unpaid labor, time — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is partial: camp and the city week (§5), not communal property. Mortification is partial at most: Temple offerings in the confession/closure row (§5); do not upgrade Temple to her mortification. Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical, pp.99–102). Official: no year-round communal housing; leave when you want (S3; §13 items 4, 10). Transcendence as "institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday" (pp.128–138) is not codeable. Harvey is dead; Principles are guideposts, not rules; Goodell is CEO, not a living founder-guru (S1, S15; §13 item 1).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense (burner noun; I survived BRC [year]; §3, §4b), not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (Temple offerings; Ranger / Green Dot if harm). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing Org arrangements. Authenticity ranking (sparkle-pony / plug-and-play) is external (S18).

**Q7.** The Ten Principles (named, not dumped) and participation-only transformation are official meaning claims (S1, paraphrased). Harvey: communities from shared confrontation with survival (S15). Slogan and principle authority, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: Baker Beach 1986; first desert 1990; 2026 pages still listing BRC week (S2, S15–S16) — event span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if a ticket click alone produces belonging; if skipping a year is common and unpenalized beyond money already spent and unused gear / identity (§13 item 10).

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix: friend story; Journal / JRS; regional; camp invite; Stewards; ticket sales; TAP (§0, §7, S3–S4, S7, S14). Proportion not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: first-timer sells not-a-festival; ticket / Gate / camp / burns reveal inner-circle role (§3, §7). Consistent with Stark 1996's belief-follows-attachment restatement.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions, affective bond is present as first-playa / Greeters / camp warmth (their "cult" names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as the city week and camp labor — not daily contact that makes every verbal convert a deployable agent. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured "acutely felt, enduring discrepancy." Religious problem-solving and seekership are mismatched (participatory city, not church). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted (no live-in; no published shunning; leave when you want; §13 items 3–4, 10). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips' partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Word of mouth, first-timer, TAP, STEP, and regionals require outward ties. Camp and Stewards raise a boundary; they do not close the network.

**Q7.** Mix, weighted to cultural / media presence (mythology; official: little mass-media solicitation) plus organized-group invitation (camp, regional, Stewards). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. Journal / first-timer / ticket are entry. Camp, Steward, volunteer, regional, and donor are retention. Wikipedia population series (S18) and official regional 100,000+ in 2024 (S14) are stock, not a 2026 BRC attendance figure. Do not invent one. Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey's opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Participants do not co-reside year-round (§13 item 4). What remains: lexicon; burner noun; camp; year-count; Principles as guideposts. Exit cost: official leave; no BMP refund; unused gear; lost camp role; no sourced shunning doctrine (§13 item 10; S5). Blurred boundaries are separable from co-residence for the modal first-timer.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show tight image control — joint copyright, personal-use only, take-down — without a closed-media system (§13 item 5; S12–S13). Critical press is abundant (S18–S20). That does not test his dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey's two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet's split is time-bounded ticket / first week vs camp / Steward / regional (§13 items 4, 7). Official leave-when-you-want and STEP before ship keep the two senses separable.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming "blurred boundaries → high exit cost" would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records an opt-in ticket and official leave, with higher practical penalties once camp and Steward attach (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal first-yes.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing brand. Participant / burner vs spectator / default-world is the membership mark (§0b, §2). Named official other: sponsorship, spectators, MOOP, failing self-reliance — not a competing festival as the enemy noun (§0b). Vox / Wikipedia / BI supply tech-bro and plug-and-play frames (S18–S20). Muniz and O'Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement. Brand-community as defined remains partial: no branded good at the center.

**Q2.** Interacting community at camp / burns / regional, not a non-interacting brand public. Public-only (Journal or photo without camp ties) is top-of-funnel (§0, §6, S7, S14).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Gifting, two-hour city MOOP, and camp labor (§4b, §5) thicken as the ladder is climbed. Not measured.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official talk does the reverse: not a festival; Principles as guideposts; participation as the path (S1, S7) — not his fan articulation, and not this note's religion finding. Cult is official silence (§7 objection 10) versus Vox pilgrimage and Wikipedia religious-studies cites (S18–S19). Not collapsed.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: playa name; burner; camp; year-count (§2, §6). Recruiting: word of mouth, regional, camp invite, Stewards — interaction-primary at camp, not a largely-online public only (§0, §7). Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** Burning Man is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow's definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. An annual gathering plus year-round regionals is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline of a periphery, and early-riser pattern (pp.196, 201–203) are contention terms and cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. Official 2026 pages still publish BRC week, tickets, Survival, TAP, and regionals (S1–S17). A living event culture; it has not ended. Not "one ended scene." Not Anderson's scene. Not Tarrow's cycle. Do not collapse the three. 2024 non-sellout and the 2024 990 loss (S18, S20–S21) are stress tests, not field death. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case. Do not import those forces onto Black Rock City even if both look like festival culture.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule's plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). One-and-done, unused ticket, and stop-going are individual (§7, §13 item 10; S5). Field collapse is not observed (2026 OMG still selling; regionals 2024 100,000+; still delivering; §9; S2–S3, S14). BI 2024 loss / $20M ask (S20) stays external finance reporting, not field collapse.
