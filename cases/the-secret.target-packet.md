## Executive summary

The Secret is a mass-market manifestation franchise built around a single portable claim: thoughts and feelings attract matching outcomes, so a person who masters visualization, gratitude, intention, and emotional state can have, do, or be anything. The adhesion engine is not a classroom and not a congregation. Official FAQ (opened this pass): The Secret does not conduct or endorse seminars or workshops; any event claiming to represent The Secret is treated as trademark misuse. The product is a solo-practice kit that a person can start from a book, a 91-minute film, a free phone app, or a printable Bank of the Universe check, then stay inside through daily teachings, a money-practice app, sequel books, a 4-week remastered-film challenge, a story library, and a free email drip (Secret Scrolls).

Movement-DNA read from pages opened this pass: the system manufactures a felt law (like attracts like; the Universe wants what you want), then treats every coincidence, mood lift, discount, or gift as confirmation and every stall as a wrong signal. Official Ask-Rhonda copy (opened): the law of attraction is infallible; if a desire does not arrive, the person is sending the wrong message. That attribution loop is the retention engine. The commercial ladder is low-ticket and open-ended rather than a hidden-price call-close: a free Super App and free Scrolls sit next to a 4.99-dollar money app, a 4.99-dollar daily-teachings app, a 9.99-dollar film rental, a 29.95-dollar Blu-ray, a 26.99/29.95-dollar hardcover, and a 26.95-dollar card deck (dollars from pages actually opened; sequel-book list prices not published on official product pages).

Layer split (do not collapse):

- Official self-description (opened): you can have, do, or be anything you want; Ask, Believe, Receive; joy to billions; 34 million copies / 50-plus languages (official biography); Super App free worldwide; no official seminars, no teacher accreditation, no franchise, no affiliation with The Secret University; TOS educational disclaimer that books/films/products are not for diagnosis or resolution of health disorders or financial matters; Contact page: no personal advice, seek a professional for financial/medical/mental-health problems.
- External reporting / scholarship (opened): Wikipedia book/film/Byrne (pointer-then-open) for New Thought lineage, Oprah-era scale, 30 million copies (conflicts with official 34 million), 300 million dollars gross by 2009 (Forbes opened), film budget/box-office, Hicks removal, Australian legal disputes. Scientific American (Shermer, June 2007) rejects the science halo and names the victim-blame implication. Skeptoid (Dunning, 15 Apr 2008) same cluster: victim-blame, medical substitution, quantum-physics packaging. Psychology Today (Hassan, 21 Feb 2023) maps law-of-attraction belief to shame, inaction, and exploitable magical thinking. Manifestation claims are not treated as true in this packet.

This is a branded belief-and-practice ecosystem with a thin doctrine, a daily ritual kit, a testimony loop, and a low-friction product ladder. Mechanics extract. Official voice and external documentation stay labeled.
