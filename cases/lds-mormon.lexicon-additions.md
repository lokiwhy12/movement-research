## Identity labels
- Latter-day Saint — preferred official member label (Newsroom style guide)
- member / new member / returning member — Handbook 23 retention nouns
- friend / person being taught — current official nouns for the prospect (investigator is older usage still in the first-pass packet)
- brother / sister — ward address form (Ministering FAQ)
- ministering brother / ministering sister — assigned watch-care identity
- missionary / teaching missionary / service missionary / senior missionary — Handbook 24 types
- returned missionary / RM — cultural short form; official pages say recently released
- full-tithe payer — recommend-interview identity
- recommend holder / endowed / sealed — ordinance-gated identities
- child of record — membership without personal baptism (under 8)
- bishop / Relief Society president / ward mission leader — unpaid role identities
- prophet, seer, and revelator — apex title for the President (and, in recommend questions, the First Presidency and Twelve)

## Rank / ladder labels
- friend → baptized member → ministered-to new member on My Covenant Path → Aaronic Priesthood office or Young Women class → calling / ministering companion → Melchizedek Priesthood / Relief Society → proxy recommend → endowed → sealed → young missionary → returned missionary → ward/stake calling → senior missionary → (males) bishop / stake presidency / high council → general authority
- Time-in-covenant prestige (returned missionary, temple marriage, years of unpaid leadership) is cultural and **not** a purchased rung
- BYU member/nonmember tuition is a priced badge of membership, not a spiritual rank
- Women are not ordained to the priesthood on pages opened this pass; they hold auxiliary presidencies and may witness baptisms (Wikipedia 2019)

The missing log line is that rank is ordinance-gated and calling-gated (recommend, endowment, set-apart title), not a sash or a paid chart position. Local clergy are unpaid.

## Enemy / problem words
- apostasy / falling away after the original apostles
- sin and death
- changing values / contention
- teachings, practices, or doctrine contrary to the Church (recommend question)
- apostate groups / apostate sects (Handbook 32)
- the occult (Handbook 38)
- serious sin requiring confession to a bishop
- cult / high-control charge (external; official silence on the noun this pass)
- financial-opacity / reserve charge (external SEC/AP; official 2019 reserve-as-parable-of-the-talents frame)

Not official enemies on pages opened: investigators who decline; other living religions as persons; inactivity by itself (Handbook 32: not apostasy).
