## Executive summary

Heaven's Gate sold a portable **Next-Level graduation product**: a **classroom** (not a church) in which "students" under teachers **Ti** (Bonnie Nettles) and **Do** (Marshall Applewhite) learned to shed mammalian human traits — family, sexuality, individuality, possessions — so their consciousness could board a spacecraft crew from **The Evolutionary Level Above Human (TELAH / "Kingdom of Heaven")**. The adhesion engine was not "comet suicide" alone. It was a stack: Revelation Two-Witnesses identity → UFO-rapture reframed as scientific metamorphosis → monastic "flexibility" obedience → vehicle/container dualism after Ti's cancer death → cybersect web design (Higher Source) + internet manifesto → Hale–Bopp as "marker" for willful exit framed as boarding, not suicide.

**Movement-DNA read (this pass):** Do & Ti fused **Christian millenarian Two Witnesses myth**, **ancient-astronaut / New Age walk-in language**, **ascetic monastic boot-camp logistics**, **"illusion of choice" open-door filtering**, and — after 1985 — a **body-as-vehicle** doctrine that converted failed physical-pickup prophecy into dignified evacuation. The 1990s face added **Higher Source professional web work** as camouflage income and **heavensgate.com** as SEO'd eternal classroom. The living residue is not a restartable membership church but **archive-as-afterlife**: TELAH Services custodians freeze 1997 disk contents online and mail book/tapes. That split — **practice terminated / message permanently hosted** — is itself a durable mechanic.

**Public record (paraphrase, not our finding):** On Mar 26 1997, San Diego County deputies found 39 bodies (including Applewhite) in a rented Rancho Santa Fe mansion after a tip from former member Rio DiAngelo; authorities and reporting describe a coordinated multi-day ritual exit using sedatives mixed into food, alcohol, and asphyxiation aids, timed to Comet Hale–Bopp's approach, with identical black clothing, Nike Decades, purple shrouds, and "Heaven's Gate Away Team" patches. Subsequent months saw additional deaths among some former members. **Needs counsel review. Not a legal conclusion of this packet. No how-to.**
