## Identity labels
- **student / client** — entry noun; you are in a school, not a church
- **Nxian** — insider self-noun (Grigoriadis)
- **integrated** — post-EM self; the disintegration has been “upgraded”
- **unified** — theoretical end-state Raniere would not claim in the 2018 interview
- **potent** — rich + disciplined + slim + ethical; the desired body-status
- **badass** — preferred self-word, especially on the women’s track / DOS
- **ethical** — not a compliment; a caste claim (ethical people should control wealth)
- **coach / proctor** — operator identities that convert a buyer into staff
- **Vanguard** — required name for the founder; daily speech discipline
- **Prefect** — required name for Nancy Salzman
- **master / slave / first-line** — DOS identity nouns; “master of your own life” as the recruitment gloss
- **sister** — DOS peer noun that makes exit feel like betraying women, not a man
- **suppressives / Luciferians / The Fall** — identity assigned to critics and irrevocable defectors

## Rank / ladder labels
- white sash (student) → yellow (coach) → orange (proctor) → green (senior proctor, later reporting)
- **stripe path** — four silk stripes ironed on before a color change; some colors “never attained”
- numbered intensity is *time* at the front (3 / 5 / 16-day) and *caste* after (sash)
- **Ethos** — weekly rank-maintenance, not a one-off
- **Jness / SOP** — gendered mid-ranks
- **DOS slave → DOS master** — hidden caste that still requires recruiting a line below you
- **Grandmaster** — DOJ/court caption for Raniere inside DOS
- commission (Forbes: 20% at high rank) is a rank privilege, not a side job

The existing log line (“rank/status labels fused with curriculum identity”) is the headline. The addition is that **recruiting and “issues cleared by EM” are the hidden exams**; the sash is the diploma.

## Ritual / action verbs
- **enroll** — recruit; mission-statement verb, not a sales verb
- **integrate / upgrade** — rewrite a memory’s meaning
- **explore the meaning** — run an EM
- **tribute** — thank Vanguard/Prefect at the close
- **bow / stand / clap** — body verbs that install rank without argument
- **collateralize (your word)** — SOP: put a group consequence behind a promise
- **give collateral** — DOS: hand over ruinous material to hear the pitch
- **take a vow** — DOS lifetime obedience
- **brand** — cauterize as initiation
- **do an act of care / act of self-denial** — labor and penance as devotion
- **purge** — mission verb for parasite/envy habits
- **source** — as in Relationship Sourcing: extract value even from a death

## Phrase-structure patterns (why they travel)
- **There are no X, therefore I will not choose to be X** — law-like moral sentence (victims)
- **X is a science based on the belief that…** — claim-as-method
- **You are choosing / you chose to stay dependent** — agency assigned as blame
- **Replacing data with data** — computer metaphor for identity overwrite
- **Collateralize your word** — finance metaphor for conscience
- **Master of your own life** — gloss that hides master-of-you
- **We teach everything nobody else teaches** — totality claim
- **Ethical people must control the money** — humanitarian wrapping on accumulation
- sash color as a sentence you wear (“I am yellow”) — identity as textile
- trademark/patent theater on ordinary English (Rational Inquiry, module titles)

## Comparative note for the lexicon log
NXIVM’s naming system wins on **caste visibility** and **moral compression** and loses on **backlash surface** the moment the same words (victim, slave, Vanguard, collateral) meet daylight. Cross-case: opposite of Landmark on speech portability (Landmark nouns can be said at dinner; NXIVM nouns mark you). Closer to a martial-arts school plus a classified program.
