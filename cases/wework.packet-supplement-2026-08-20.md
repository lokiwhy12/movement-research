## 1. What kind of case WeWork / The We Company / WeWork Inc. is, for each lane

Two objects, labeled and not collapsed. (A) Neumann-era commercial quasi-movement: community-as-product, founder charisma, 2019 S-1 consciousness register. (B) 2026 hospitality / flex-office product still selling memberships (John Santora CEO; Made for work. Built for business).

**Commitment (Kanter).** For-profit commercial company, non-residential for the modal member. Not her 1780–1860 utopian sample. Success cut 25 years community duration (1972, Appendix p.260). Continuance (membership fee; Commitment Term; five-day cancel; non-refundable fees/set-up) is the closest fit. Cohesion and control run through Community team / events / House Rules / keycard without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90). WeLive was a housing product, closed — do not upgrade the modal desk to her communion. Sacrifice/renunciation/transcendence mostly unavailable as she defined them. Object (A)'s 2019 consciousness register is slogan, not her transcendence pages. Object (B) is hospitality remainder.

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Mix: location pages, day-pass trial, app, brokers, enterprise; historically founder/VC story (§0, §7). Network entry on Stark's 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian convert series. Religious-seekership does not map for a modal first desk.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** Packet §13 scan is observational, not a verdict. No communal living as default (§13 item 4). Harvey's blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default desk condition; WeLive/WeGrow were product extensions, closed. Lifton's milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). Do not score Lifton or Harvey.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Partial. Closer than The Secret or QAnon (physical rooms, Community team, events, membership identity). Brand community is "a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand" (Muniz and O'Guinn 2001). Object (A) sold community-as-product with programmed events and Community managers — interacting, not a brand public only. Object (B) is hospitality remainder; Community is still a word.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is "a phase of heightened conflict across the social system" (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A failed IPO and a Chapter 11 are not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings; do not run it.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: membership fee; Commitment Term month-to-month or 3/6/12; five-day cancel; fees non-refundable; set-up fee named non-refundable, dollar unpublished. Opened 2026 dollars: On Demand from $25/day; All Access Help 2026 US Plus $339 / Basic $220; marketing tiles from $187/mo; SF coworking $339 / $289; Dedicated from $181/desk/mo vs SF $494–$665. Private office inquire; list not published (§8, S5–S7, S9, S12). Cohesion: Community team; events (S-1 2,500-plus/week; 2026 regularly hosts). Control: House Rules; keycard non-transferable; guest limits (S10–S12).

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: spend, Commitment Term, non-refundable fees/set-up, credits that expire; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); leftover month is the nearest analogue (S9, S12). Sacrifice is mostly unavailable: not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy (Table 1, p.95). Communion is partial at most: events, Community team, visible peers — not communal property (pp.88, 110–116). WeLive terminated Jul 2021; WeGrow closed end of 2019 academic year (S18). Mortification is partial at most: in-app report, not mutual criticism, surveillance, or probationary socialization (pp.122–125). Renunciation is structurally unavailable (pp.99–102). Transcendence as "institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday" (pp.128–138) is not codeable.

**Q3.** Something expensive is present at dedicated and inquire-price rungs. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense, not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices sit at the in-app report and, historically, all-hands (S10, S21). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing desk arrangements.

**Q7.** Elevate the world's consciousness and make a life, not just a living are official 2019 meaning claims (S15, §1). Official 2026: a smarter way to work; Made for work. Built for business (S1–S2).

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: founded 2010 Neumann + McKelvey after GreenDesk; S-1 first community 154 Grand Street, early 2010; Wikipedia first SoHo April 2011 — not reconciled (§0, S15, S18). Still selling on 2026 pages (S1–S9) — product/company span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance: strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable and communion never leaves Community team / events; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if day-pass plus designed lobby alone produces belonging; if five-day cancel / On Demand leave is common and unpenalized.

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix: location pages, day-pass trial, app, brokers, enterprise; historically founder/VC story (§0, §7, S1–S6). Proportion not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: day-pass / lobby sells the room; All Access / Dedicated install recurring identity; historically WeLive / WeGrow deepen by life-domain, then close (§3, §7, §8, S18). Consistent with Stark 1996's belief-follows-attachment restatement.

**Q4 (partial).** Affective bond is present as Community-team and event warmth. Intensive interaction is present as daily desk among members — not daily deployable-agent contact. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured "acutely felt, enduring discrepancy." Religious problem-solving and seekership are mismatched for a modal first desk (§0). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted (no live-in as default; WeLive closed; no shunning doctrine; gift/referral and city presence keep the network open; §13 items 3–4, 10). *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips' partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Location pages, day-pass, brokers, enterprise, and city presence require outward ties. Keycard and House Rules are a brand boundary, not network closure (S10).

**Q7.** Mix as in Q1. The closer is the first room or first keycard (§7, S5–S6). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** Location / day-pass / broker is entry. All Access Commitment Term, Dedicated, Private, Enterprise are retention. Official 2026 half a million members versus S-1 527,000 memberships; 600 / 600-plus versus Wikipedia 2024 154 versus S-1 528: not reconciled (S1–S2, S15, S18).

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey's opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost. No communal living as default (§13 item 4). WeLive/WeGrow were product extensions, closed (S18). Exit: no shunning doctrine; contractual five-day clock, Commitment Term, non-refundable fees/set-up, expired credits (§13 item 10; S9, S12). Separable from co-residence.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show app-centric booking, House Rules, and keycard (S10–S13) without a closed-media system. Critical press is abundant in 2019 (S20–S22). That does not test the dependency claim. Do not score Lifton.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey's two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet's split is 2019 consciousness / WeLive / WeGrow as life-claiming product extensions (closed) versus 2026 hospitality desk (§13 items 2, 4, 7). The two senses look separable.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming "blurred boundaries → high exit cost" would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records five-day / On Demand leave paths; exit penalties are contractual, not institutional (§13 item 10).

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing consumer brand. Member versus traditional office is the membership mark (§0b, §6, §13 item 3). Named official other: 2019 static long-term leases / stuffy corporate culture; 2026 one-size-fits-all real estate / hidden costs (§0b). IWG / other flex is the packet's §1 note, not an official enemy.

**Q2.** Partial. Object (A): programmed events and 2,500-plus community managers (S15) — interacting, not a brand public only. Object (B): hospitality remainder; Community is still a word; 2026 member-to-member interiors thin (S1–S2, S6).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Community-team introductions, events, case-study quotes, Dialpad All Access passes (§7, S3).

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official 2019 talk is consciousness / We; official 2026 is flex real estate — not Kozinets' religion/myth as a finding (§1, S1–S2, S15). Cult noun: official 2026 silence. *The Cult of We* is a book title. HBO *Cult of WeWork* episode (Wikipedia Neumann, S19). Brown / Wiedeman stay reporting (S21–S22).

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: member; keycard; dedicated / private / enterprise rank (§2, §6). Recruiting: location pages, day-pass, brokers, enterprise; historically founder/VC. Physical rooms first (§0, §7). Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** WeWork / The We Company / WeWork Inc. is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow's definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. A failed IPO and a Chapter 11 are not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline of a periphery, and early-riser pattern (pp.196, 201–203) are contention terms and cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Both as labeled layers. Continuing-practice: object (B), the 2026 desk product, still sells memberships (S1–S9; John Santora CEO; Made for work. Built for business). Ended-operator: object (A), the Neumann-era quasi-movement, did not survive its operator. S-1: community company; elevate the world's consciousness; 527,000 memberships; 528 locations; $3.3B run-rate; $4.0B backlog; $47.2B future undiscounted minimum lease-cost payments; net losses $1.9B 2018 / $0.9B H1 2019; 2,500-plus community managers; 2,500 events/week; WeLive / WeGrow named; 20-vote high-vote stock; related-party risk named (S15). IPO pulled ~six weeks later; Neumann ousted; SoftBank recap; 2021 SPAC; Chapter 11 D.N.J. 6 Nov 2023; emerged 11 Jun 2024; preexisting equity cancelled (S16–S17). Consciousness register gone from 2026 pages (S1–S2). Neither Anderson's scene nor Tarrow's cycle. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley): Philadelphia rave/EDM. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule's plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Five-day cancel and On Demand leave are individual (§7, §13 item 10). Field collapse is not recorded: Chapter 11 of one operator is not automatically field collapse; 2026 WeWork still sells (S1–S9); other flex operators exist in the packet's own distinctiveness note (IWG; §1).
