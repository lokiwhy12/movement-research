## Executive summary

On Marines.com recruit-training (opened this pass), recruit training is where Marines are made and excuses are left behind. Winning means earning the title of Marine. The site names four phases — Foundation, Immersion, Transformation, Transition — over 13 weeks at one of two depots: Marine Corps Recruit Depot Parris Island, South Carolina, or Marine Corps Recruit Depot San Diego, California, generally assigned by where the person enlisted. Official FAQ-for-parents (opened): Receiving is the first week (3-5 days of processing); actual training is 12 weeks after that. Official become page (opened): enlisted path is recruiter, then Delayed Entry Program, then 13-week recruit training, then graduation as part of something greater than the self, then School of Infantry (every Marine a rifleman), then MOS school, then a unit.

The adhesion engine, from pages opened this pass, is not a ticketed seminar. It is paid employment that begins on arrival (official recruit-training FAQ: recruits begin earning pay the day they arrive, plus meals and uniforms; pay is modest and rises with rank and time). Delayed Entry Program FAQ (opened): no pay and no benefits until official entry onto active duty at boot camp; a poolee can request release before shipping. Enlistment is not a product SKU. The cost that binds is time, body, isolation, and a title that the official voice says is earned, never given (Terms of Use, opened: EARNED, NEVER GIVEN and MARINES. THE FEW. THE PROUD. are registered trademarks).

Official receiving (FAQ-for-parents): paperwork, haircuts, issued uniforms and gear, medical evaluations, Initial Strength Test; at week's end the recruit meets the drill-instructor team. Official communication rule: one required phone call the night of arrival to next-of-kin or recruiter; after that, letters and postcards only until named liberty windows after the Crucible. Families may not visit until Family Day, the day before graduation.

The Crucible, on the official recruit-training page (opened): a 54-hour culminating event; food and sleep deprivation; 45 miles of marching; physical and mental challenges; designed to simulate combat hardship; camaraderie is solidified; individuals become a unit; those who complete it earn the title United States Marine. Official FAQ-for-parents: those who complete the final challenge earn the Eagle, Globe, and Anchor, symbolizing their transformation from recruits to Marines. Official recruit-training page: the emblem ceremony is the first time a recruit is called United States Marine; the emblem is not something that is given. Graduation (become page): those who make it through possess discipline, grit, and determination; graduation marks becoming part of something greater than themselves.

That is the official recruiting voice. It is not the scholarship layer and not the incident record.

Scholarship (Brent Lawniczak, Journal of Advanced Military Studies vol. 17 no. 1, Marine Corps University Press, opened): identity fusion — personal and group identities merge into a visceral oneness; fusion, not mere social identification, is what theorists tie to extreme pro-group action. The article names Marine Corps Recruit Training as the primary rite of passage; yellow footprints as the start; the Crucible as a 54-hour dysphoric-plus-euphoric ordeal; EGA as the signal that fusion with the Corps and all those who have gone before is complete; the November 10 birthday as transgenerational ritual; every Marine a rifleman as functional equivalence. The article also names rites of terror, humiliation, and joining-ordeals as fusion pathways in the general theory (Whitehouse/Lanman via Lawniczak). That is a military-university reading of the same pipeline. It is not Marines.com copy. Do not collapse them.

External reporting (Wikipedia United States Marine Corps Recruit Training, pointer-then-open): 13 weeks including in- and out-processing; yellow footprints; this-recruit third-person speech; Black Friday meeting of permanent DIs; Crucible designed 1996; Wikipedia Crucible numbers are 48 miles, two MREs, six hours of sleep — these conflict with Marines.com 45 miles and with a Military.com DI quote of eight hours sleep, two-and-a-half MREs, and about 40 miles (opened). Wikipedia also records a public incident stack: Ribbon Creek 1956 (six recruit deaths); 1976 Hiscock shooting and McClure pugil-stick death; 2006-07 San Diego recruit-abuse courts-martial; 2016 Parris Island suicide investigation and 2017 Felix conviction; 2025 Washington Post/Military.com reporting on elevated drill-instructor suicide. Those pages are not a finding that the current pipeline is lawful or unlawful. They are the public failure-mode record.

Labeled Army BCT contrast (GoArmy basic-training, opened; one line only): Army BCT is 10 weeks in four named phases (Yellow / Red / White / Blue) ending in a multiple-day field exercise called The Forge; the same page says about 90 percent of candidates complete BCT and that Drill Sergeants are not there to break you. Do not treat The Forge as the Crucible.

Movement-DNA read from pages opened this pass: strip (receiving / haircut / issued kit / isolation / third-person) then rebuild (drill, values, marksmanship, MCMAP, DI as local total authority) then bind (Crucible deprivation plus teammate dependence) then name (EGA / first use of Marine) then witness (Family Day / graduation / birthday thereafter). Official voice sells transformation and title. Scholarship names identity fusion. Reporting names the same rites plus the abuse-and-death file. This packet extracts the mechanics. It does not teach anyone how to enlist or how to get through.
