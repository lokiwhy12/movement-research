## Executive summary

The Children of God / Family International sold a portable **endtime-missionary identity**: drop out of "the System," pool possessions in a colony/home, witness full-time (literature + music + personal approach), and take David Berg's **Mo Letters** as living prophecy equal in practice to scripture. The adhesion engine was not only apocalyptic urgency. It was a stack: countercultural belonging → forsake-all sacrifice → colony immersion → prophetic literature flood → (in one era) Law-of-Love sexual theology that sacralized adult sexual sharing and, for a bounded period, sexualized evangelism branded Flirty Fishing → later constitutional Charter rules → post-2010 vaporization into an online tithe-and-report network of autonomous missionaries.

**Movement-DNA read (this pass):** TFI fused **charismatic remote prophecy** (nearly 3,000 Mo Letters from a secluded founder), **Acts-style communism as logistics**, **litnessing quotas**, **us-vs-System framing**, and — in contested eras — **sexual theology as recruitment and cohesion tech**. Reform eras show a durable mechanic of their own: **revolution / reboot as self-absolution** (RNR 1978; child-protection declarations mid-1980s; Love Charter 1995; Reboot 2010) that keeps missionary nouns while discarding or quarantining earlier literature. The successor face (TFI Online / Charter / Child Protection Policy) presents as a mainstreaming online Christian network; external NRM scholarship and survivor literature treat the same arc as high-control continuity with episodic rupture. That split — **mission continuity + selective amnesia** — is itself a portable mechanic.

**Public record / public reporting (paraphrase, not our finding):** A 1995 UK High Court judgment (Lord Justice Ward) found that the group and some top leaders had in the past engaged in abusive sexual practices involving minors and severe corporal punishment, required denunciation of certain Berg writings, and (with conditions) found the then-current environment safer for the ward. Multiple national raids/investigations in the early 1990s are reported; outcomes and findings are contested across sources and jurisdictions. In January 2005, Ricky Rodriguez (raised in the founder's household circle; subject of the internally distributed *Story of Davidito* childrearing materials) killed former caregiver Angela Smith and then himself — an event that revived public allegations (NYT / CNN paraphrase). **Needs counsel review. Not a legal conclusion of this packet. Child-harm topics remain observational paraphrase of public sources only.**
