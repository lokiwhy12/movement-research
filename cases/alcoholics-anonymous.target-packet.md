## Executive summary

Alcoholics Anonymous is a decentralized peer fellowship whose official pages present recovery as one alcoholic helping another: no dues or fees for membership; the only requirement is a desire to stop drinking; groups are autonomous except where other groups or A.A. as a whole are affected; leaders are trusted servants who do not govern; public relations run on attraction rather than promotion; anonymity is named as the spiritual foundation that puts principles before personalities. The movement-DNA read is not a treatment-efficacy paper. It is a persuasion system that converts self-diagnosis into repeated low-cost attendance, then keeps that attendance alive with testimony ritual, a sponsor dyad, a compressed spiritual-recovery lexicon, and a service loop that turns the helped person into the helper.

Official pages opened this pass still run the same grammar in 2026: meetings cost nothing to attend; membership is open to anyone who wants to do something about a drinking problem; A.A. welcomes newcomers and does not recruit them; it is not allied with any religious denomination and the one-line religion FAQ answers "No"; the Twelve Steps are spiritual principles that, practiced as a way of life, are said to expel the obsession to drink; the Twelve Traditions keep groups unified and related to the world; the Seventh Tradition keeps money inside the membership (individual gifts to the General Service Board capped at 7,500 USD per year; one-time member bequests capped at 12,500 USD; no outside contributions; no group-contribution cap). Exact suggested basket amounts at local meetings are **not published** on official pages opened this pass. Do not invent them. Literature is sold: 2025 A.A.W.S. catalog unit prices recovered this pass include the jacketless hardcover Big Book at 12.00 USD, portable Fourth Edition at 11.00 USD, pocket abridged at 6.00 USD, hardcover Twelve and Twelve at 11.00 USD. Grapevine (opened store): US print 36.00 USD / year; digital 29.99 USD / year or 2.99 USD / month; Complete 56.00 USD / year. Official: Grapevine is supported by sales, not by Seventh Tradition contributions to A.A.

External pages opened this pass (Stanford Medicine 2020 summary of a Cochrane review; Kelly / Hoeppner / Stout / Pagano 2012 multiple-mediator analysis on PMC; Wikipedia Alcoholics Anonymous as pointer-then-open) document the other layer: a 2020 Cochrane-style review, as summarized by Stanford, found AA / Twelve-Step Facilitation more effective than psychotherapy for abstinence in the studies that passed quality filters, and often cheaper for health systems; the Kelly mediator paper finds the largest paths are social-network change and social abstinence self-efficacy, with spirituality / negative-affect paths more visible in the more-impaired aftercare subsample. Wikipedia records critiques (Dodes low-success claim; Glaser Atlantic piece; court rulings on coerced attendance; 13th-stepping studies; secular-meeting delisting). Those are externally documented or reported claims. Official pages opened this pass do not argue the cult charge and do not publish a success-rate table. This packet does not collapse the layers and does not write a medical or legal finding.

The prior dossier named ten portable primitives at the end and left scores, the 8-type ritual table, the 1-to-9 map, objections-as-the-system-answers-them, section 13, and this-pass numbered sources missing or in the old shape. Those holes are filled below from opened pages, or marked honest remaining unknown. Steps / Traditions / sponsorship / anonymity / GSO beats are in this packet because those official pages were opened this pass. Official P-15 pamphlet body (Questions and Answers on Sponsorship) was a catalog teaser only; the official PDF URL tried this pass 404'd. Sponsorship mechanics below come from the opened service-sponsor FAQ, the 2022 membership-survey page (81 percent of surveyed members say they have a sponsor), What-is-A.A. / homepage / P-2, and Wikipedia as an external layer — not from a reconstructed P-15.

Movement-DNA read: A.A. is a high-adhesion recovery identity system whose flagship ritual is the meeting share, whose daily container is one-day-at-a-time plus optional literature / prayer / sponsor contact, whose temple is the rented room or the online/phone meeting, and whose escalation ladder is first meeting -> return -> home group -> sponsor / Steps -> service / sponsorship of others. There is no paid spiritual ladder. The durable mechanics are (1) a tiny lexicon that recodes drinking as powerlessness / unmanageability / illness-or-malady, (2) self-diagnosis instead of a hard sell, (3) testimony as portable proof, (4) a same-problem guide dyad, (5) anonymity as both safety and anti-glamour, and (6) service as the retention engine that converts gratitude into operator labor.

## 0b) Environmental conditions (why now / why it scaled)

3-7 conditions to demand to promise:

1. Mid-century (and continuing) public alcoholism as moral failure plus inaccessible or unaffordable professional care -> demand for a free, immediate, local room that will take you tonight -> official: no dues, no application, desire-only membership, meetings in ordinary rented space.
2. Shame, stigma, and a drinking identity that cannot be worn at work -> demand for a container that will not put your face on the news -> official anonymity at press / radio / films / internet; personal-level protection especially for newcomers.
3. Repeated failed solo attempts and a social world organized around drinking -> demand for a replacement network that is also a practice -> official one-alcoholic-helping-another; external Kelly paper: drop pro-drinking ties, gain pro-abstinence ties.
4. Distrust of paid gurus, churches-as-owners, and temperance politics -> demand for a fellowship that will not bill you, endorse a candidate, or reform the world -> official: not allied; no opinion on outside issues; not a temperance movement; no recruiting; self-support only.
5. Meaning collapse after loss of control -> demand for a spiritual (not sectarian) handle that a Catholic, an atheist, and a person who uses the group as the higher power can all speak -> official religion FAQ + P-2 Higher Power latitude; Steps use God as understood (paraphrase, not a transcript).
6. Institutions that need a free aftercare and court-compliance slot -> demand for a ubiquitous meeting list and, sometimes, a signature on an attendance slip -> official: referrals welcomed; slips are not A.A. procedure; group autonomy. External: US courts have limited coerced attendance (Wikipedia; opinions not opened).
7. Founder death as the usual kill-point -> demand for a structure that outlives Bill W. and Dr. Bob -> official history: 1951 Conference; 1955 came of age; inverted pyramid; Class A nonalcoholic trustees who can use last names on camera.

Meaning crisis named (official language, paraphrase): cannot control alcohol; life unmanageable; obsession to drink; isolation; the last to know; guilt, loneliness, hopelessness (opened 2021 press item on the press page); a wish to live a normal, happy life without alcohol.

Enemy / chaos named: official — alcohol as the unmanageable power; self-deception / unwillingness to face facts; isolation; overconfidence after a dry stretch (slip causes in P-2); money, property, and prestige that divert a group from its primary purpose (Tradition 6/7 paraphrase); personalities over principles; outside contributions and public controversy. Not named as enemies: society, unbelievers, other religions, moderate drinkers (official: not anti-alcohol; not a temperance movement). External opened pages add: coerced spirituality, 13th-stepping, cult rhetoric, low-success claims.

Certainty / belonging / status offered: I am an alcoholic as a speakable identity; home group; sponsor / sponsee; length of sobriety (chips common in culture; official pages opened this pass do not make chips universal); trusted-servant roles (chair, treasurer, GSR); being asked to share; being useful to the next newcomer. Status is moral-reputational, not purchased.

## 1) Promise + positioning (the offer)

Explicit promise (1 sentence): come together with other people who cannot control alcohol, practice a set of spiritual principles, and stay sober while helping the next alcoholic (What-is-A.A. + homepage + Preamble-on-P-2, paraphrased).

Implicit promise: you are not uniquely broken; others have been where you are; a satisfying life without alcohol is possible; the group can hold you until you can hold yourself; helping someone else is how you keep what you were given.

Pain named: loss of control; drinking more than intended; wrecked family / work / civic life; periodic binges that shorten; fear of the word alcoholic; the morning-after remorse; the conviction that willpower should have been enough (P-2, paraphrased).

Time-to-win: official — some get and stay sober from the first meeting; for others the first meetings plant a seed (2022 survey page). P-2: the current 24 hours is the only period you can do something about. No official month-count to a completion state. First meeting is same-day if a room or an online meeting is listed.

Before / after identity: from a person who cannot control alcohol and may still be deciding whether they are alcoholic -> self-identified alcoholic / member if they say they are -> home-group member -> sponsee working Steps -> sponsor / trusted servant / person who carries the message. Official: you are a member if you say you are (group page + Tradition Three paraphrase).

Positioning markers: fellowship not a treatment center; spiritual not a religion; nonprofessional usual 12th Step work; attraction not promotion; no dues; not allied; not anti-alcohol; one primary purpose.

### Working score
- Clarity: 5/5 — the official sentence is stable across homepage, What-is-A.A., Preamble, and P-2 opened this pass.
- Believability: 5/5 insiders / 3/5 outsiders who hear religion, cult, or it-does-not-work (opened Wikipedia / Stanford-as-counter). Official pages do not publish a success rate.
- Urgency: 4/5 once drinking has become unmanageable or a referral has landed (homepage: if drinking is out of control, A.A. can help) / 2/5 as a cold visitor with no crisis (the CTA is a meeting, not a countdown).
- Distinctiveness: 5/5 as a globally scaled no-dues, anti-promotion, anti-paid-ladder fellowship; 3/5 if scored only as another support group.

## 3) Transformation sequence (the protocol)

Numbered arc from pages opened this pass:

1. Pain / referral / self-doubt. Goal: get a body into a room. Mechanism: crisis, family, treatment, court, PSA, or Is A.A. for You? twelve questions (press names the tool; pamphlet body not fully used beyond P-2's only-you-can-decide). Expected state: shame, reactance, curiosity.
2. First meeting. Goal: survive the room. Mechanism: open or closed; sit and listen is officially enough (homepage); optional newcomer intro, not mandatory (meeting page). Expected state: recognition or alienation.
3. Recognition. Goal: these people are like me. Mechanism: testimony triad; beginners meeting; literature reading. Expected state: relief or I-am-different.
4. Return behavior. Goal: the second yes. Mechanism: keep coming back (survey page quote); no fee; many meetings. Expected state: habit forming or dropout.
5. Home-group claim. Goal: placement. Mechanism: you are a member of a group if you say you are (group page). Expected state: belonging.
6. Sponsor ask. Goal: dyadic accountability. Mechanism: unofficial, for the asking (survey 81 percent; P-15 body not opened). Expected state: attachment, sometimes dependency risk (external).
7. Step work. Goal: identity break deeper than attendance. Mechanism: inventory, telling another human being, amends (Steps page paraphrased). Expected state: vulnerability, repair, or refusal.
8. Service. Goal: convert the gift into labor that keeps the giver sober. Mechanism: making coffee, chairing, GSR, sponsorship, institutions. Expected state: usefulness, status-without-glamour.
9. Carry / stabilize. Goal: stay sober today and keep the door open. Mechanism: meetings + sponsor + one-day-at-a-time + Twelfth Step. Expected state: identity maintenance.

First threshold: attending despite stigma; optionally identifying. Deeper threshold: Step Five-style confession to another human being and/or amends (paraphrase). Sacrifice signals: time, honesty, vulnerability, service — not a tuition.

### Working score
- Repeatability: 5/5 — any town with two alcoholics and a rented room can run the sequence; GSO will list a new group and send a starter literature pack.
- Speed: 3/5 — first meeting can be tonight; the identity break is weeks-to-years and officially never done.
- Durability: 5/5 as a lifelong practice design; 3/5 when the person never attaches to a sponsor or home group (official survey implies attachment is the majority pattern, not a guarantee).

## 4b) Psychological pull factors

Top drivers (mechanism to moment to reinforcement):

1. Belonging — walk into a room that already knows the shame; home group; after-meeting social air. Reinforcement: keep coming back; 81 percent sponsor attachment in the survey sample.
2. Meaning / spiritual handle — Steps as a life practice; Higher Power latitude so a believer and an atheist can sit in the same row (official). Reinforcement: daily reflections, inventory, prayer/meditation as Step Eleven paraphrase.
3. Catharsis — inventory + telling another human being + amends. Reinforcement: the room treats disclosure as normal.
4. Agency-through-surrender — official paradox: admit powerlessness over alcohol to regain a livable life. Reinforcement: one-day-at-a-time wins.
5. Competence / usefulness — service and sponsorship as the advanced skill. Reinforcement: my sobriety depends on connecting with other alcoholics (What-is-A.A.).

7. I do not have time / I will miss my friends / work drinks. — Official P-2: most already spent the time drinking; take a soft drink at the business cocktail party; bar acquaintances are not the same as friends; sobriety has not been a hindrance to work in the stories they print.

## Section G — keep / modify / avoid (analytical, not a build)

### Keep (mechanics worth understanding)
- Self-diagnosis instead of a hard sell
- A testimony triad short enough to reuse
- A free, local, frequent room
- A same-problem dyad that is not a paid coach
- Anonymity as both safety and anti-glamour
- Rotation + trusted servants do not govern
- A service loop that makes the helped person the helper
- Published contribution caps and a written no-outside-money rule

- Publish the local basket custom in the same type size as it-does-not-cost-anything
- Put the sponsorship how-to (P-15) on the same HTML depth as the service-sponsor one-pager
- Keep official not-a-religion and the actual meeting-close prayer on the same table when the audience is a court or a referrer
- Label Cochrane / mediator findings as external, not as A.A. claims

### Avoid
- Designing a movement from this packet (out of scope)
- A paid ladder that copies the adhesion without the no-dues constitution
- Treating court attendance as conversion
- Using newcomer vulnerability as a dating or status pool
- Transcribing Steps / Traditions as decorative scripture in derivative work without the credit line the Terms require — and, for this research file, do not transcribe them at all
- Collapsing official voice with Stanford or with Dodes
