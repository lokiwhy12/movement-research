## 1. What kind of case the Church is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** Living restorationist church: geographic ward, unpaid callings, covenant-path ladder, temple recommend, missionary labor for a subset (§0, §5, §8). Kanter’s opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Not that sample. Continuance (tithing-as-recommend-gate; non-refundable missionary contributions; unpaid years) is the closest public fit. Cohesion and control run through the ward without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Official acquisition: member love / share / invite; full-time missionaries; ComeUntoChrist; find-a-church; free Book of Mormon (§0, §7; S2, S11). Network entry on Stark’s 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian series. The 1980 AJS abstract names “Mormons (LDS)” as a sample — historical/external label. Body closed; do not upgrade secondary kin / cold-call figures.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet’s high-control scan is observational and contested, not a verdict (§13). No communal lifetime housing (§13 item 4). Harvey’s blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default member condition; closer as official missionary housing away from home (S3). Cult language stays official silence (no cult-denial FAQ; visits free; agency; §7 objection 4) versus Wikipedia / external charge (S21). Not collapsed.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Mismatch as a consumer-brand community. Brand community is “a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand” (Muniz and O’Guinn 2001). Official pages present a religion, not a branded good (S1, S15). Ward and mission companionships are interacting, not a brand public. Official talk is Restoration Christianity, not Kozinets’ fan-side religion/myth.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A living church is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule’s individual disengagement can be asked of resignation or inactivity; field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: Sunday and visits 0 USD; tithing framed as 10 percent (S8); full-tithe as a recommend question (S4); missionary contributions cannot be refunded (S9); current equalized dollar unpublished (S3, S9). Church News 2019: 400 USD then / 500 USD planned 1 July 2020 — historical, not 2026 (S20). Cohesion: geographic ward; sacrament; ministering; callings (S1–S2, S14). Control: recommend interviews; membership councils; apostasy definition; living prophet / all priesthood keys (S4–S5). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: tithing and missionary-fund contributions put a stake; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); official no-refund (S9) is the nearest analogue. Sacrifice is partly codeable: unpaid callings; 18–24 month mission labor; Word of Wisdom / chastity / Sabbath — closer to abstinence as Table 1 coded it (p.95). Communion is partial: weekly ward (S2, S13), not communal property. Mortification is partial: recommend interview; confession to bishop (S4–S5); do not upgrade every interview to her mortification. Renunciation is thin as she defined it (pp.99–102). Official: families are forever; inactivity is not apostasy; no communal lifetime housing (S1, S5). Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is partly codeable: living prophet / keys; handbook as operating system (S4, S15).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present (time; 10-percent framing; unpaid years). Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost-shape plus later defense (S2, S4), not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (recommend interview; repentance confession; membership council). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are only partly standing (S2, S14). Do not upgrade every interview to her mortification.

**Q7.** Restoration, living prophet, eternal family, and covenant path are official meaning claims (S1, S15). Scripture and living-prophet authority, not her transcendence pages. Official homepage: members are Christian; some beliefs are distinct (S1). Not a theology finding.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: organized 1830; 2026 pages still delivering (S1, S17) — organizational span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if a free first visit alone produces belonging; if Sunday-and-leave is common and unpenalized (S5; §13 item 10).

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix, proportion not quantified. Official: member invite; full-time missionaries; ComeUntoChrist; find-a-church; free Book of Mormon; ward activities (S2, S11, S15). Cold-door and member invitation both named. 1980 AJS body closed; do not upgrade secondary kin / in-home figures.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: first lesson / sacrament meeting sell Restoration and belonging; baptism, calling, recommend, endowment reveal the deeper identity (§3, §7). Official: pray about the Book of Mormon with real intent (S15) — consistent with Stark 1996’s belief-follows-attachment restatement.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions: affective bond is present as first-Sunday warmth / ministering / missionary companionship (their “cult” names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as weekly ward plus, for missionaries, daily companionship. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured “acutely felt, enduring discrepancy.” Religious problem-solving sits next to official Christian self-description (S1, S12). Religious seekership, turning point, and extra-cult neutralization are not systematically evidenced for the modal visitor; extra-cult is contradicted by open meetings and inactivity-is-not-apostasy (S5). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips’ partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Love / share / invite, ComeUntoChrist, and missionaries require outward ties (S2, S11). Recommend / contrary-doctrine / apostasy definition are inward boundary (S4–S5). Open-network at the free Sunday door; 2026 pages still recruit (S17: 385,490 convert baptisms — official count, not a retention finding).

**Q7.** Mix: cultural / media presence (Conference; ComeUntoChrist; Book of Mormon as object) plus organized-group invitation (member invite; missionary force). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. ComeUntoChrist / first lesson / sacrament meeting are entry. My Covenant Path, calling, recommend, endowment, mission are retention (S2, S4). Official 17,887,212 membership (S17) vs Pew ~2 percent U.S. identity (S23) vs Wikipedia activity estimates (S21) stay labeled, not collapsed. Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost. Members at large do not co-reside (§13 item 4). What remains: lexicon; Latter-day Saint identity; geographic ward; recommend; garment (endowed). Missionary housing away from home is the closer Harvey condition (S3). Exit cost: official resignation by signed request; inactivity is not apostasy; ordinances revoked; no sourced civil shunning statute (§13 item 10; S5). External: social cost of leaving a dense ward / family (S21). Blurred boundaries are separable from co-residence for the modal member.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show official scripture to be read, public Conference, public handbook, a recommend question on contrary doctrine, and apostasy as persisting after correction (§13 item 5; S4–S5). Temple interiors unpublished; not dumped. That does not test his dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey’s two senses: environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet’s split is time-bounded public (Sunday / first lesson) vs missionary total-life and endowed recommend-gated life (§5, §13 items 4, 7). The two senses look separable for modal Sunday.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost” would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records a free congregational door and official resignation-by-letter, with higher practical penalties once calling, recommend, endowment, and family sealing attach (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal visitor, not necessarily for a long-attached member.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing church brand. Latter-day Saint / brother-sister / recommend-holder vs outsider is the membership mark; official name, not Mormon, is the style-guide mark (S16). Named official other: apostasy after the original apostles; contrary doctrine; apostate sects; the occult (S4–S5, §0b). External pages add historicity, finance, and LGBTQ-policy critics (S21). Muniz and O’Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement. Brand-community remains a mismatch.

**Q2.** Interacting community at ward / Sunday / ministering / mission, not a non-interacting brand public. Public-only (homepage or Conference clip without ward ties) is top-of-funnel (§0, §6).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Love / share / invite, ministering, and missionary purpose (S2, S14–S15) thicken up the ladder. Not measured; not obviously “limited liability” for bishops or missionaries.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official Church talk is institutional self-description — Restoration, Christian, sacrament, temple (S1, S12, S15) — not his fan articulation, and not a theology finding. Cult is official silence (§7 objection 4) versus Wikipedia’s controversy cluster (S21). Not collapsed.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: compact lexicon; brother / sister; missionary badge; garment (endowed, not displayed) (§2, §6). Recruiting: mediated (ComeUntoChrist, Conference) plus interaction-primary at ward / missionary visit. Ward-first. Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow’s definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. A living church is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline of a periphery, and early-riser pattern (pp.196, 201–203) are contention terms and cannot be posed from this packet.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. Official 2026 pages still publish handbook, ComeUntoChrist, statistical report, temples, and missionary CTA (S1–S19). The packet can stand as a continuing-practice study of a living church — not an ended scene, not Anderson’s scene, not Tarrow’s cycle. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule’s plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Official resignation and inactivity (not apostasy by itself) are individual (S5). Wikipedia millennial-disaffiliation estimates are a retention-leak layer (S21). Field collapse is not observed (still delivering; §9; S17).

Co-consumption is visible; his crowding-quality claim is not licensed. Q9: Hall 1988 closed.

**NRM interiors.** Q3–Q4: stayers and leavers exist, but not two populations sampled from the same period under roughly constant intensity. Q5: mode of exit vs age of socialisation — neither systematically evidenced. Q6: Covenant Path Progress and ordinance dates are organizational metrics (§11), not Ransom et al.’s self-measurement. Lalich and Introvigne 2022 stay closed.

**Brand community / fandom.** Q5: internal heterogeneity vs continuity — unanswered. Thomas et al. 2013 and Schouten and McAlexander 1995 stay closed. Brand-community as defined remains a mismatch even where Q1–Q4, Q6 can be asked of church marks.

**Movement failure.** Q1–Q4 as Tarrow case tests: mismatch, not pending more Church facts. Taylor 1989, Tarrow 2011 Part III, and Tarrow 2022 Chs 9–11 remain unopened. Anderson 2009 is opened and not imported.
