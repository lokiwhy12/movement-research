## Identity labels

- the 99% / we are the 99% — official identity noun (Principles; Declaration; occupywallst.org About). Tumblr origin of the "we are" variation is Wikipedia-mediated, not a live Tumblr open this pass.
- occupier / Occupier — body-in-the-park identity
- the 1% / 1%-ers — enemy-identity pair (Declaration layer; May Day solidarity post)
- autonomous political being — Principles register
- facilitator / co-facilitator — process role, not a named leader (NYCGA.cc)
- stack-keeper — speaker-queue operator (Wikipedia)
- pace keeper / pacer — march cohesion role empowered by the GA (NYCGA.cc)
- spoke — momentary, recallable working-group mouthpiece (occupywallst.org Spokes Council; commenter aaronb)
- working-group member / operations group / caucus — the actual unit of work
- Comfort / Medical / Legal / Accounting / Direct Action / Arts and Culture / Safer Spaces — named work identities
- founder (contested) — occupywallst.org About: ~200 Tompkins Square / september17-list people as "founders"; Adbusters (Lasn / White) as call authors. The movement later denied founders. Layer split.
- later: Occupy Democrat — brand-use afterlife (OpenSecrets PAC). Statement of Autonomy said the movement was not a brand.

## Rank / ladder labels

Occupy published no certification ladder. Rank was informal and process-shaped.

- walk-in / first-night sleeper — lowest yes
- working-group joiner — first durable yes
- facilitator / stack-keeper / pace keeper — process elevation
- spoke (recallable) — operational elevation after Nov. 2011
- Accounting / Finance / Legal / Media — high-leverage informal rank because they held money, cameras, or the NLG number
- progressive stack — a speaking-order rank that inverted ordinary dominance (Wikipedia)
- later: Occupy Democrats Election Fund treasurer / hybrid PAC — a later brand-use rank the 2011 Autonomy statement would have classed as independent of OWS

The missing log line is that "leaderless" was itself the rank claim. Informal hierarchy (facilitators, finance, livestream, media) sat next to that claim. Contested.

## Ritual / action verbs

- occupy / occupy space / occupy Wall Street — threshold verb (Declaration; Adbusters paraphrase)
- assemble / general assembly
- consensus / block / stand aside / twinkle (comment culture on occupywallst.org; Wikipedia modified consensus)
- mic check / human microphone / people's mic — two generations required (NYCGA.cc)
- step forward, or step back — progressive-stack verb (Wikipedia)
- report back
- join a working group
- speak with us, not for us — Autonomy
- donate / pass the bucket / lock-box
- freeze spending (Jan. 2012 CNN / Marketplace)
- bail (Village Voice: occupier or supporter, nonviolent, OWS-connected)
- evict / raid / clear the park — the city's verb; the movement's rupture

Do not treat these as instructions. They are observed 2011–12 labels.

## Enemy / problem words

- Wall Street / financial Gomorrah (Adbusters July 13, 2011)
- the 1% / corporate forces / corporatocracy (Adbusters candidate demand; Declaration "they")
- money in politics / Citizens United (Wikipedia background; Adbusters presidential-commission candidate)
- illegal foreclosure / taxpayer bailouts / student debt as hostage (Declaration grievances, paraphrased)
- profit over people, self-interest over justice, oppression over equality (Declaration)
- those seeking to capitalize on this movement or appropriate its message or symbols (Autonomy)
- later official caution: social-media accounts still posting as Occupy after 2012 (occupywallst.org About)
- cult / unwashed / no demands — external frame (Gitlin-via-Rumpus stereotypes; Wikipedia criticism). Official silence on the cult noun.

Not official enemies as a named caste: people who leave. Exit was walking out of the park. No published shun list on pages opened.
