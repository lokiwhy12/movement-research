## Identity labels

- **Burner** — culture identity after (and between) burns
- **Participant / citizen of Black Rock City** — official anti-spectator nouns (first-timer, Survival)
- **Virgin / first-timer / newbie** — newcomer (first-timer page; Hive Virgin Burner Guide named, not opened)
- **Campmate / theme-camp member** — parish identity
- **Steward** — 2026 reserved-sale noun for camp/art/mutant-vehicle/infrastructure crews (ticketing). Wikipedia: formerly Directed Group
- **Regional Contact** — year-round volunteer node (Regional Network)
- **Ranger / Green Dot / ESD / DPW / Greeter / Earth Guardian / Temple Guardian / BEDucator** — service identities (mix of official consent/Survival/media + Wikipedia)
- **Ticket Aid recipient** — means-tested inclusion identity; legal name at Will Call
- **Patron of Black Rock City** — named giving identity (program body not opened)
- **Renaissance Champion** — named connector identity (program body not opened)
- **Plug-and-play / turnkey / sparkle-pony** — external/vernacular out-group or pejorative ranks (Wikipedia/Vox; not official)

## Rank / ladder labels

- curious / JRS reader → first-timer → ticket holder (Get the Gift $550–$675 / Pay your Way $775 / Give the Gift $975–$3,000) → camp member → camp lead / Steward invitee → volunteer dept → Regional Contact → Meta Regional Committee
- **Rx-equivalent is not a Burning Man word.** Rank is **year-count, camp role, gift visibility, Steward access, volunteer dept**
- TAP / Resilience are side doors, not a lower caste in official copy; they are non-transferable
- Staff / CEO / founder-director is an org rank, not a playa caste (990)
- Official Team-equivalent is **Placement** (you are on the map) vs open camping (Wikipedia)

The missing log line is that **the ticket tier is both a price and a virtue label**: Get / Pay / Give the Gift turns dollars into a Principles performance.
