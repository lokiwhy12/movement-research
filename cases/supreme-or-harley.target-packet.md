At its core was a group of neighborhood kids, New York skaters, and local artists who became the store's staff, crew, and customers. Over 30 years it expanded from New York origins into a global community, working with generations of artists, photographers, designers, musicians, filmmakers, and writers who defied conventions and contributed to its unique identity and attitude.
