## 1. What kind of case Occupy Wall Street / NYCGA / Liberty Square is, for each lane

Three objects, labeled and not collapsed. (A) 59-day Zuccotti / Liberty Square encampment, 17 Sep–15 Nov 2011, plus NYCGA process. (B) Name-franchise: occupywallst.org About, 100-plus U.S. / 1,500-plus global (S1). (C) Later brand-use afterlife: occupy.org (Jason Samel / Occupy This Album, not NYCGA) and Occupy Democrats Election Fund (OpenSecrets; Autonomy-independent).

**Commitment (Kanter).** 59-day residential encampment is closer to her communal sample than WeWork, still not 1780–1860 utopian communities. Success cut 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Continuance is partial; cohesion is the closest of the three; control without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90). 59 days is not her duration cut.

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Mix: Adbusters poster/tweet; occupywallst.org; Tumblr 99%; livestream; walk-in; union marches; city-copy (§0, §7). Network on Stark 1996 (open network vs social implosion). Religious-seekership does not map for a modal walk-in.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** Packet §13 scan is observational, not a verdict. Overnight sleepers are not Harvey default leaving employer/housing/social orbit for the modal walk-in. Lifton milieu control is 1950s thought-reform (Ch.22). Do not score Lifton or Harvey. Cult language stays the packet's paraphrase.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Mismatch or very partial. Autonomy: not a brand, not for sale (S7). Brand community is "a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand" (Muniz and O'Guinn 2001). 99% + GA is a movement identity, not admirers of a brand. Later PAC / occupy.org is brand-use afterlife, not NYCGA. Harley 1995 stays closed.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Closer than WeWork (2011 encampment + meme that spread and decayed). Still a mismatch. A cycle of contention is "a phase of heightened conflict across the social system" (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). The rest of that sentence lists sector diffusion, innovation in forms, new or transformed frames, mixed organized/unorganized participation, intensified challenger-authority flow (p.195). A cycle is "not a single movement studied en bloc" (failure note). City-copy is franchise spread, not automatically sector diffusion. Later offshoots do not by themselves license the fork. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: $0 at the door; in-kind; later cash pile — Observer Oct. 18 $454,437.47 in / $55,131.85 out; CNN / Marketplace ~$800,000; Graeber >$0.5m; not reconciled (§8, S11–S13, S17). Cohesion: nightly GA; kitchen; People's Library; 100–200 overnight sleepers (S10); welcome / info tables (S8). Control: modified consensus; progressive stack; working groups; Autonomy; Spokes Council (S4, S7). Closest of the three is cohesion.

**Q2.** Investment is partial: buckets / online; leftover cash; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); no dues clawback on pages opened (§7, §10). Sacrifice is mostly unavailable: not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy (Table 1, p.95). Weather, time, arrest risk are costs, not her coded pair. Communion is partial: shared kitchen / library / camp — not communal property as coded (pp.88, 110–116). Mortification is partial at most: GA stack; Safer Spaces (Feb. 2012, after the camp) — not mutual criticism or probationary socialization (pp.122–125). Renunciation is partial for sleepers, unavailable for walk-ins (pp.99–102). Transcendence (pp.128–138) is not codeable.

**Q3.** Something expensive is present as time, weather, and arrest risk. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense, not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices sit at the GA stack and later Safer Spaces (Feb. 2012; S7). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing park arrangements.

**Q7.** Meaning claims stay labeled. NYCGA / Declaration: occupy space, create a process, generate solutions accessible to everyone (§1, S2). About: people-powered movement fighting banks and the 1% (S1). Adbusters: one demand; presidential-commission candidate (S5, paraphrased label). Autonomy: party-less, leaderless, not a brand, not for sale (S7). Later voice: crushed in 2012 (S1). Voices stay apart.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogue: Zuccotti 59 days. Not her duration cut.

**Q10.** Stated in advance: strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if cohesion is the only close fit; if sacrifice is weather / arrest rather than Table 1; if communion never leaves kitchen / library / camp; if 59 days is treated as her duration.

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix: Adbusters poster / tweet; occupywallst.org; Tumblr 99%; livestream; walk-in; union marches (Oct. 5 Foley Square); city-copy (§0, §7, S1, S5, S10). Proportion not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: walk-in / kitchen sells the room; first night / first GA installs occupier identity; working group deepens by role; after eviction the container is gone (§3, §7). Consistent with Stark 1996's belief-follows-attachment restatement.

**Q4 (partial).** Affective bond is present as kitchen, welcome table, people's mic, working-group warmth. Intensive interaction is present for overnight sleepers — not daily deployable-agent contact as 1965 defined it for the modal day visitor. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured "acutely felt, enduring discrepancy." Religious problem-solving, seekership, and turning point are mismatched or un-evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted (no live-in as default for walk-ins; no shunning doctrine; city-copy keeps the network open; §13 items 3–4, 10). *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips' partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three, and only inside the park for sleepers.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Walk-in, livestream, Tumblr, union marches, and city-copy require outward ties. Autonomy's "not part of Occupy" clause is a name boundary, not network closure (S7).

**Q7.** Mix as in Q1. The closer is the first walk-in or first night (§7). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** Walk-in / slogan / livestream is entry. Overnight, working group, facilitation, arrest / bail are retention rungs. After Nov. 15, leaving was the default (§7). Official later voice crushed in 2012 (S1). Do not reconcile city counts.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey's opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost. Overnight sleepers are not Harvey default leaving employer, housing, and social orbit for the modal walk-in (§13 items 4, 10). Exit: no shunning doctrine; after eviction, exit was the default. Separable from co-residence for walk-ins.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show livestream, open GA, and abundant critical press (S10–S18) without a closed-media system. That does not test the dependency claim. Do not score Lifton.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey's two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet's split is 24-hour park as environment-creating for sleepers versus $0 walk-in as a day visit (§5, §13 items 2, 4, 7). The two senses look separable.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming "blurred boundaries → high exit cost" would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records no shunning doctrine and post-eviction default leave (§13 item 10).

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing consumer brand. 99% vs 1% is the identity mark, not admirers of a brand (§0b, §2, §13 item 3). Named movement other: Wall Street / the 1% (Declaration; About). Autonomy's other: symbol-appropriators (S7). Muniz and O'Guinn's definition is consumer-brand. Mismatch or very partial.

**Q2.** Partial at most. Inside the park: nightly GA, kitchen, working groups — interacting, not a brand public only. Autonomy: not a brand, not for sale. After eviction: slogan without a room. Later occupy.org and Occupy Democrats PAC are brand-use afterlife, not NYCGA (S9, S19).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Welcome / info tables, kitchen, Community Agreement, bail criteria for nonviolent OWS-connected arrests (S8, S14).

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official movement talk is 99% / process / mortgaged future — not Kozinets' religion/myth as a finding (§1, S1–S2, S6). Cult noun: official silence. Packet §13 does not decide the noun. This note does not either.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: occupier; 99%; working-group role (§2, §6). Recruiting: Adbusters call (label, not a script); occupywallst.org; Tumblr; livestream; walk-in; city-copy. Physical park first (§0, §7). Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** Occupy Wall Street / NYCGA / Liberty Square is closer than WeWork (2011 encampment + meme that spread and decayed). It is not automatically a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow's p.195 definition (as in §1) is not shown as a multi-sector phase from opened Ch.8 plus this packet. City-copy (100-plus U.S. / 1,500-plus global, S1) is franchise spread, not automatically sector diffusion. Later offshoots (Occupy Sandy, Strike Debt, Occupy the SEC; §0, S10) do not by themselves license the fork. Mismatch. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline of a periphery, and early-riser pattern (pp.196, 201–203) are contention-cycle terms and cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Ended-encampment / decayed-control for NYCGA / Zuccotti. The 2011 encampment is not a living continuing-practice. Zuccotti was 59 days (17 Sep–15 Nov 2011). Official later voice: crushed in 2012 (S1). Brand-use afterlife labeled separately: occupy.org is Jason Samel / Occupy This Album, not NYCGA (S9); Occupy Democrats Election Fund OpenSecrets 2023–24 $3,422,620 raised / $3,177,486 spent / $106,600 to Democratic candidates — Autonomy-independent (S19). Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley): Philadelphia rave/EDM. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case. Do not import onto Zuccotti.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule's plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Individual walk-away is askable: after eviction, leaving was the default; no shunning doctrine (§7, §13 item 10). Eviction-as-container-loss is in the packet: the ritual room was the product; CNN: loss of park = loss of momentum, media, in-kind food (S11). Field collapse is not automatic: later offshoots exist (S10). Eviction of one park is not field collapse.
