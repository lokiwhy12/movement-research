## Executive summary

The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is a restorationist Christian church whose official pages present a complete life structure: restored priesthood authority, additional scripture (the Book of Mormon as another testament of Jesus Christ alongside the Bible), a local ward as the weekly social unit, a covenant-path ladder from baptism through temple ordinances, unpaid local callings, a full-time missionary force, and a household study loop (Come, Follow Me). The movement-DNA read is not a verdict on restoration claims. It is a persuasion and retention system that converts a free first yes (meet missionaries; visit sacrament meeting; take a free Book of Mormon) into ordinances, then keeps the convert inside a role-rich congregation whose next visible step is always a recommend, a calling, a mission, or a sealing.

Official pages opened this pass still run the same grammar in 2026: all are welcome at Sunday meetings; missionary visits are free; tithing is framed as one-tenth of income (For the Strength of Youth, Dec. 2023, citing Doctrine and Covenants 119:3 — paraphrase, not a transcript); fast offerings are the value of two missed meals, used locally for food, rent, medical bills; a current temple recommend is required to enter a dedicated temple; the recommend interview asks whether the member is a full-tithe payer (new members seeking a youth/proxy recommend are asked whether they are willing to obey the commandment to pay tithing); young teaching missionaries are called by the President of the Church, set apart by the stake president, and live away from home (men 18–25 usually 24 months; women 18–29 usually 18 months); the family and the missionary have primary financial responsibility for a monthly contribution commitment whose equalized dollar is identified at Church headquarters and is **not published** as a current figure on the handbook or missionary-finances help pages opened this pass. A 2019 First Presidency letter, as printed by Church News, named a then-current equalized amount of 400 USD / month and a planned 500 USD / month from 1 July 2020 for listed countries. Whether that 500 USD figure is live in 2026 is **not recovered** from official pages opened this pass. Do not invent it. Ward missionary contributions, including prepaid amounts, cannot be refunded (official missionary-finances help, last updated 2 Apr 2024). Temple clothing and garments may be purchased at store.ChurchofJesusChrist.org; unit prices were **not recovered** this pass (store login / catalog not opened as a price table). BYU (CES affiliate) 2026-27 full-time undergraduate tuition, opened: 3,548 USD / semester for Latter-day Saint students; 7,096 USD / semester for non–Latter-day Saint students. Official: significant operating costs are paid with the tithes of Church members.

External pages opened this pass (Wikipedia The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints as pointer-then-open; Pew Research Center 2023-24 Religious Landscape Study religious-identity chapter; SEC press release 2023-35 and AP report on the Ensign Peak settlement) document the other layer: official year-end 2025 membership 17,887,212 and 385,490 convert baptisms (Newsroom statistical report, 4 Apr 2026); Pew: Latter-day Saints about 2 percent of U.S. adults across 2007 / 2014 / 2023-24 landscape studies; Wikipedia: researchers estimate weekly attendance around 4 million globally and record a 2016 survey finding a majority of millennials raised in the church had disaffiliated; SEC order (settled, not a trial verdict): from 1997 through 2019 Ensign Peak used thirteen shell LLCs to file Forms 13F and obscure a portfolio that by 2018 was approximately 32 billion USD; penalties 4 million USD (Ensign Peak) + 1 million USD (the Church); First Presidency 17 Dec 2019: a portion of donations is safeguarded as a prudent reserve; the Church complies with applicable law. Those are externally documented or official-response claims. This packet does not collapse the layers and does not write a legal or theological finding.

The prior first-pass named a restoration + missionary + ward + covenant-path + temple + tithing + calling stack and left scores, the 8-type ritual table, the 1-to-9 map, objections-as-the-system-answers-them, section 13, sourced prices, terms/privacy, and this-pass numbered sources missing or in the old shape. Those holes are filled below from opened pages, or marked honest remaining unknown. Restoration / missionary / ward / covenant-path / temple / tithing / calling beats are in this packet because those official pages were opened this pass. Temple ceremony interiors, garment unit prices, the current equalized missionary dollar, and Handbook 31 (youth-interview body) were not opened or not recovered.

Movement-DNA read: the Church is a high-adhesion living-religion system whose flagship weekly ritual is sacrament meeting, whose daily container is prayer / scripture / Come, Follow Me, whose temple is a recommend-gated house of ordinances, and whose escalation ladder is contact -> lessons -> baptism/confirmation -> ministering and a calling -> temple recommend -> endowment / sealing -> mission or lifelong temple and family-history work. There is no paid local clergy ladder. The durable mechanics are (1) a restoration claim that recodes other churches as incomplete rather than merely different, (2) a free, relational front door staffed by a rotating unpaid missionary army, (3) rapid embedding into a geographic ward, (4) a visible covenant-path scoreboard (My Covenant Path; Covenant Path Progress in Member Tools / LCR), (5) unpaid callings that convert attendees into operators, and (6) a temple-recommend interview that binds testimony, chastity, Word of Wisdom, honesty, Sabbath, loyalty to living prophets, and full tithing to access to the highest ordinances.

## 3) Transformation sequence (the protocol)

Numbered sequence from pages opened this pass. Goal / mechanism / expected emotional state.

1. **Finding / first touch**
   - Goal: a person willing to hear.
   - Mechanism: member invitation (love, share, invite); ComeUntoChrist form; street/online missionary contact; ward activity; free Book of Mormon; find-a-church. Missionary visits always free.
   - Emotion: curiosity, low threat, being seen.

2. **Lesson sequence + spiritual experiment**
   - Goal: Restoration seriousness plus a personal confirmation.
   - Mechanism: missionary lessons (Preach My Gospel); Book of Mormon reading and prayer with real intent (ch. 1); invitation to attend sacrament meeting.
   - Emotion: testing, hope, cognitive load.

3. **Congregational embedding before the ordinance**
   - Goal: the person meets a living ward, not only missionaries.
   - Mechanism: Handbook 23 invitations — sacrament meeting, baptisms, ward activities, homes, seminary/institute/family history/self-reliance/JustServe, lessons with missionaries. Ministering brothers/sisters may be assigned to people being taught.
   - Emotion: belonging rehearsal, visibility.

### Top 10 objections as the SYSTEM answers them

From homepage FAQ, ComeUntoChrist beliefs/common-questions list, Handbook 23/24/26/32/38, donations pages, Preach My Gospel ch. 1, style guide, First Presidency 2019 finances statement. Paraphrase.

1. **“Are you Christian? / Do you believe in Jesus?”** — Homepage: yes; Jesus is the Son of God and Savior; members are devoted followers; some beliefs are distinct; salvation through His life, ministry, sacrifice, and resurrection.
2. **“Do you believe in the Bible?”** — Homepage: yes; the Bible is the word of God; additional Church scripture works together with it. Book of Mormon is another testament, not a replacement.
3. **“Why extra scripture / Joseph Smith / Restoration?”** — Preach My Gospel ch. 1: after the apostles died, authority was lost and doctrine changed; the Father and the Son appeared to Joseph Smith in 1820; priesthood and the Church were restored; the Book of Mormon witnesses that restoration. People should pray about it with real intent.
4. **“Is this a cult / high-pressure group?”** — Official pages opened this pass do not use the cult noun and do not run a cult-denial FAQ. Official counter-frame: visits are free; agency; success is not baptism count; Sunday meetings are open; love / share / invite is “normal and natural.” The cult / high-control frame lives on opened Wikipedia and in external controversy lists. Not collapsed.
5. **“Why do you send missionaries? Are they paid?”** — Handbook 24: sacred privilege; young men: priesthood responsibility; young women: optional; missionaries represent Jesus Christ; family has primary financial responsibility; Church pays young-teaching travel; missionaries receive adequate field funds for food and transport and must not send those funds home.
6. **“Do I have to pay to join? What about tithing?”** — ComeUntoChrist: visits always free. FTSOY + donations page: members donate 10 percent of income as tithing; fast offering = two meals. Handbook 26: a recommend requires being a full-tithe payer (or, for a new member’s proxy recommend, willingness to obey the commandment). Joining Sunday meetings is not ticketed.
7. **“What happens in temples? Why can’t I go?”** — Homepage: temples differ from meetinghouses; members make eternal covenants; marriages meant to last beyond death; work for ancestors. Handbook 26/38: only members with a current recommend; worthiness interview. Ceremony interiors **unpublished** on pages opened; not reconstructed.
8. **“What if I am LGBTQ / trans / same-sex married?”** — Handbook 38 (opened, paraphrased): feeling same-sex attraction is not a sin; acting on it is treated as a chastity issue; same-sex marriage is outside the law of chastity and may be cause for a membership council. ComeUntoChrist front door does not lead with this. Needs counsel review as policy description, not a finding.
9. **“What if I want to leave?”** — Handbook 32: resignation by signed written request (or notarized letter to headquarters); bishop should reach out first; resignation revokes ordinances and privileges; readmission usually after at least one year and an interview, sometimes a council; previously endowed need restoration of blessings. Leaders continue to minister unless asked not to. Inactivity is not, by itself, apostasy.
10. **“Where does the money go? Why the investment fund?”** — Donations page: temples, meetinghouses, missionary work, education, welfare, humanitarian aid; First Presidency + Twelve + Presiding Bishopric decide (D&C 120 paraphrase). First Presidency 17 Dec 2019: vast majority used immediately; a portion reserved; all funds exist to support the Church’s mission; complies with applicable law. External SEC/AP layer: 13F shells, ~32 billion USD by 2018, 5 million USD combined penalty; Church statement of regret and that the matter is closed. Both layers stay on the table.

### Counsel questions (numbered). Needs counsel review.

1. Tithing as a condition of temple access: Handbook 26 asks endowed members whether they are full-tithe payers and asks new members seeking a proxy recommend whether they are willing to obey the commandment to pay tithing. FTSOY states 10 percent of income. What must be disclosed to an investigator or a fiancé before a temple date is set, in each jurisdiction, so the percentage-plus-recommend link is not heard as an undisclosed condition?
2. Missionary labor and funding: families have primary responsibility; contributions are not refundable even on early return; current equalized dollar is not on the public handbook page; 18–24 months of unpaid representative labor; young men: priesthood responsibility. What labor, child, consumer-protection, and charitable-solicitation rules apply to the monthly sweep and to recruiting 18-year-olds?
3. Worthiness interviews (adults): private interview covering sexuality, honesty, tithe, substances, loyalty to living prophets, and self-assessed worthiness. The member may invite a spouse, parent, or other adult. What consent, recording, and clergy-penitent rules apply, and what is the live complaint path if an interviewer adds questions the handbook forbids adding?
4. Youth interviews: Handbook 26 allows a parent or other adult in the room; Handbook 31 body **not opened** this pass. Wikipedia and external debates on one-on-one youth interviews are not reconstructed here. What is the live minimum-age instrument, parent-presence default, and sexual-question scope for minors?
5. LGBTQ / gender-identity policy: Handbook 38 distinguishes attraction (not a sin) from same-sex sexual behavior and from same-sex marriage (chastity / possible council). Transgender ordinances follow biological sex at birth; transition can block priesthood, recommend, and some callings; First Presidency exceptions exist. How should this be described to a ComeUntoChrist inquirer so the front-door “all are welcome” and the handbook restrictions are on the same table?
6. Excommunication / withdrawal / resignation: Handbook 32 names purposes (protect others; help repentance; protect Church integrity), required-council sins, apostasy, annotations that bar callings with children, and resignation that revokes ordinances. What notice, appeal, defamation, and data-retention rules apply, and when does a public ward announcement (allowed in predatory / apostasy / flagrant cases) create risk?
7. Abuse reporting vs confidentiality: Handbook 32/38 — duty of confidentiality; abuse help line; policy is to obey the law; one exception for imminent life-threatening harm. External criticism (Wikipedia) includes alleged failures to report. What is the live rule in each jurisdiction for bishops who hear child-abuse confessions?
8. Ensign Peak / reserves / donation representation: SEC settled order (13F shells; ~32 billion USD by 2018; 5 million USD combined penalty); First Presidency 2019 reserve statement; no churchwide financials published since 1959 (Wikipedia). How should tithing solicitation and “windows of heaven” language sit beside a large investment reserve in each jurisdiction’s charitable-solicitation and securities-adjacent rules?
9. Data / Member Tools / eligibility scoring: Privacy Notice uses data to evaluate temple, missionary, and leadership eligibility; ward-level sharing of membership info; permanent historical/membership retention. What happens when a recommend cancellation, a membership annotation, or a missionary-account balance is visible to clerks, and what is the erasure story for a resigned member?
10. CES / BYU tuition split and Honor Code adjacency: 2026-27 member tuition is half the nonmember rate because tithes subsidize operations. Honor Code body **not opened**. What membership-verification, Title-IX, and consumer-tuition rules apply when ecclesiastical status sets the price?
11. Temple clothing / garments: required after endowment; day and night; not to be displayed or altered; prices not public on pages opened; bishop may use fast offerings to buy them. What consumer, import, and bodily-autonomy disclosures apply, and what is the rule when a workplace or military uniform conflicts (Handbook 38 already names a temporary-lay-aside)?
12. Minors as members of record and as baptizands: children of record created at blessing; baptism at 8 with parental permission (Handbook 38); missionaries do not teach people in prison. What parental-consent and data-privacy instrument covers a child of record whose parents later resign?
