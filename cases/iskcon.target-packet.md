## Executive summary

ISKCON sold a portable **devotional-identity product**: the **Hare Krishna maha-mantra** as sonic theology anyone can chant, a **daily japa quota** (16 rounds / 1,728 mantra repetitions), **four regulative principles** as a purity vow-kit, **prasadam hospitality** (Sunday Feast) as the soft front door, and a **founder-acharya canon** (Prabhupada's English commentaries, especially *Bhagavad-gita As It Is*) as the closed interpretive frame. The adhesion engine was not "exotic Hinduism" alone. It was a stack: public sankirtan spectacle → free feast belonging → book as portable guru → initiation rename + beads → ashram/temple service immersion → (historically) totalizing commune and sankirtan finance → (after founder death) lieutenant-guru zones that fractured → congregational/diaspora patronage that restabilized the brand.

**Movement-DNA read (this pass):** ISKCON fused **sonic ritual tech** (loud congregational kirtan + private bead japa), **vow-locked initiation**, **book-as-mission/cash dual engine**, **founder fossilization** (Founder-Acharya title exclusivity; GBC as ultimate managing authority), and — in the post-1977 crisis era — **zonal charismatic succession** that the institution later partially dismantled. The contemporary face keeps mantra, feast, deity worship, Ratha-yatra, Food for Life, and Life Membership while officially centering Prabhupada's books and a collegial GBC rather than a single living godman. That split — **practice continuity + founder-as-permanent siksa + institutional amnesia/reform narratives around zonal-acharya and gurukula eras** — is itself a durable mechanic.

**Public record / scholarly reporting (paraphrase, not our finding):** Post-1977 succession produced repeated guru crises; Kirtanananda Swami was expelled (1987) and later pleaded guilty to racketeering (public record / Wikipedia paraphrase); an internal 1998 exposé and Rochford's scholarship describe widespread physical, emotional, and sexual abuse in ashram-gurukulas (1970s–mid-1980s); a Texas suit seeking ~$900M led to Chapter 11 and a settlement reported around $15M covering hundreds of claimants (Wikipedia / press paraphrase). **Needs counsel review. Not a legal conclusion of this packet.**

## 1) Promise + positioning (the offer)

**Explicit promise (1 sentence, official register):** through chanting the Hare Krishna maha-mantra, following regulative principles, studying Prabhupada's presentation of Bhagavad-gita / Bhagavatam, and engaging in devotional service, reawaken dormant love for Krishna and return to your eternal spiritual identity.

**Implicit promise:** you can become pure, protected, and purposeful while outsiders remain entangled in maya — and the temple/guru/sanga will hold the form while you cross from curiosity to vow.

**Pain named:** material suffering; rebirth; ignorance; temporary body; sense gratification; Kali-yuga contamination.

**Time-to-win:** immediate sensory win via prasadam + kirtan high; sadhana steadiness measured in months before initiation eligibility; "back to Godhead" as ultimate, not weekend, horizon.

**Before / after identity:** John/Jane / karmic conditioned soul → bhakta / initiated das/dasi with spiritual name → (optional) brahmacari/brahmacarini or grihastha devotee → (historical peak) temple resident sankirtan warrior.

**Positioning markers (official):** bhakti-yoga; Gaudiya Vaishnava / Vedic; Founder-Acharya Prabhupada; not ritvik; Global Membership open without discrimination (Constitution 8.1); science of self-realization rhetoric in older preaching; contemporary "Hindu denomination" legitimacy turn (scholars: Rochford/Neubert Hinduization).

- **Clarity:** 5/5 — mantra quota, four regs, and feast entry are unusually clear.
- **Believability:** 4/5 inside sanga / Indian cultural familiarity; 2–3/5 for uninitiated Western street public after 1980s cult scare (varies by country).
- **Urgency:** 4/5 historically (Kali-yuga / preach before death); 3/5 contemporary congregational (festival seasons, Life Membership campaigns).
- **Distinctiveness:** 5/5 — public orange-robed sankirtan + maha-mantra brand is one of the most recognizable NRM→Hindu mission stacks of the late 20th century.

- Optional practice quotas with health/sleep contraindications

- Child safety as non-negotiable institutional priority, not appendix after catastrophe
- Treat investigative scholarship as data, not persecution proof
