## 1. What kind of case A.A. is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** Living decentralized peer fellowship: no-dues meetings, desire-only membership, home group, sponsor dyad, service loop (§0, §5, §8). Kanter’s opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). A.A. is not that sample. Continuance (time and honesty; unpublished basket; priced literature; no paid ladder) is the closest public fit. Cohesion and control run through the room without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Acquisition is a mix: self-decision; family / friend; treatment; court / employer; other members; PSA; Find A.A. (§0, §7). Official: welcomes, does not recruit (S1). Network entry on Stark’s 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian series. Religious-seekership as written does not map for a modal attendee (official: spiritual, not a religion; S11).

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet’s high-control scan is observational and contested, not a verdict (§13). No communal living requirement is found (§13 item 4). Harvey’s blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default meeting condition. Lifton’s milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). Cult / religion language stays official denial or silence versus opened external charge (S11, S20).

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Mismatch as a consumer-brand community. Brand community is “a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand” (Muniz and O’Guinn 2001). Official pages present a fellowship, not a branded good; no single consumer brand; no paid spiritual ladder (§0, §8). Meeting and home group are interacting, not a brand public. Official talk is spiritual-not-religion (S11), not Kozinets’ fan-side religion/myth.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A living fellowship is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule’s individual disengagement can be asked of people who slip or walk away; field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: meetings 0 USD; basket amount unpublished; literature priced; GSO individual cap 7,500 USD / year; no paid ladder (§8, S9). Cohesion: home group; testimony triad; 81 percent of surveyed members have a sponsor (S17). Control: group conscience; trusted servants do not govern; inventory / telling another human being / amends as named Step handles, not transcribed lists (S3–S4). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is partly codeable: time and honesty put a stake in return; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); no tuition to claw back (S9). Sacrifice is partly codeable: hours, vulnerability, service — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Not-drinking-today is the official purpose, not her sacrifice-to-justify-joining. Communion is partial: frequent contact and home group (§5), not communal property. Mortification is partial: meeting share; Step Five-style admission (§5); do not upgrade every share to her mortification. Renunciation is thin as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical, pp.99–102). Official: families welcome at open meetings; no communal housing; A.A. does not operate hospitals (S12; §13 item 4). Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is partly codeable as dead-founder literature authority, and contradicted as living insulated hierarchy: leaders do not govern (S4).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present (time, honesty, disclosure). Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost-shape plus later defense (slip causes in P-2), not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (share; inventory; telling another human being). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing Conference arrangements. Do not upgrade a share to her mortification.

**Q7.** “Not an empty promise” and Steps as spiritual principles that can expel the obsession are official meaning claims (S1–S2, S12). Slogan and literature authority, not her transcendence pages. Official religion FAQ: no; not allied (S11).

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: 1935 Akron; 1951 Conference; 2026 pages still listing meetings (S8, S2) — organizational span, not member-commitment duration. There is no graduation (§0).

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if a free first meeting alone produces belonging; if walking out is common and unpenalized beyond time already spent and identity / network cost (§13 item 10).

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix, proportion not quantified. 2022 survey page names self-decision; family / friend; treatment; court / employer; other members; PSA (S17). Cold-door: Find A.A.; sit and listen (S2, S5). Official: no recruiting (S1).

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: first meeting sells recognition; return / home group / sponsor / Step work reveal the deeper identity (§3, §7). Official: keep an open mind if the God-word is a problem (S12) — consistent with Stark 1996’s belief-follows-attachment restatement.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions: affective bond is present as first-meeting warmth / after-meeting / sponsor ask (their “cult” names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as frequent cheap meetings and optional sponsor contact — not daily contact that makes every verbal convert a deployable agent. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured “acutely felt, enduring discrepancy.” Religious problem-solving sits next to the official not-a-religion line (S11, S12). Religious seekership, turning point, and extra-cult neutralization are not systematically evidenced for the modal attendee; extra-cult is contradicted by walk-away and open meetings (S5). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips’ partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Find A.A., open meetings, professional outreach, and attraction-not-promotion require outward ties (S1, S5). Closed meetings are the mild exclusive form. New groups listed with GSO still receive a starter pack (S6).

**Q7.** Mix: cultural / media presence (Alexander flood on the official history page; PSA) plus organized-group invitation (Twelfth Step / institutions) plus self-decision (S8, S17). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. Find A.A. / first meeting / self-diagnosis are entry. Home group, sponsor, and service are retention (S17). Kelly 2012 is an external mechanism paper (S19), not an entry study. Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Members do not co-reside on pages opened (§13 item 4). What remains: compact lexicon; alcoholic-for-life identity; home group; sponsor. Exit cost: official walk away; no dues to claw back; no sourced shunning doctrine (§13 item 10; S12). Practical cost is social and identity (S20, external). Blurred boundaries are separable from co-residence for the modal attendee.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show Conference literature as an integrity lock, Terms against dumping the canon, and an official “read anything” line (§13 item 5; S13). That does not test his dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey’s two senses: environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet’s split is time-bounded first meeting / sit-and-listen vs home-group / sponsor / 24-hour program (§5, §13 items 4, 7). People can walk out. The two senses look separable here.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost” would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records a free door and official walk-away, with higher practical penalties once home group and identity attach (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal first-meeting attendee, not necessarily for a long-attached member.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing brand. Alcoholic vs people who can drink, and member-if-you-say-you-are, are the membership marks (S6, S12). Named official other: alcohol as unmanageable power; isolation; personalities; outside money — not society or moderate drinkers (official: not a temperance movement) (§0b). External pages add cult rhetoric (S20). Muniz and O’Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement. Brand-community remains a mismatch.

**Q2.** Interacting community at meeting / home group / sponsor / service, not a non-interacting brand public. Public-only (homepage or PSA without room ties) is top-of-funnel (§0, §6).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Twelfth Step work and carrying the message (S1, S10) thicken as the ladder is climbed. Official P-2: Twelfth Step work is enlightened self-interest. Not measured; not obviously “limited liability” for sponsors.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official A.A. talk is institutional self-description — spiritual, not a religion, not allied (S11, S12) — not his fan articulation, and not this note’s religion finding. Cult is official silence plus no-recruiting / walk-away (S1) versus Wikipedia’s Rudy/Greil / Vaillant / Peele layer (S20). Not collapsed.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: compact lexicon; first-name + last initial; home group; chips local and unpublished (§2, §6). Recruiting: official no recruiting; attraction not promotion; interaction-primary at the meeting. Room-first. Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** A.A. is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow’s definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. A living fellowship is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization and institutionalization (pp.201–202) are contention terms. Differential decline of a periphery (pp.202–203) is a cycle-demobilization claim. Early-riser pattern with opportunity held constant (p.196) cannot be posed from this packet.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. Official 2026 pages still publish meetings, literature, the 2022 survey, and the 2025 Vancouver Convention (S1–S17). The packet can stand as a continuing-practice study of a living fellowship — not an ended scene, not Anderson’s scene, not Tarrow’s cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule’s plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Official slip (P-2: return to drinking) and walk-away are individual (S12; §13 item 10). Field collapse of the Fellowship is not observed (still delivering; §9; S2).

## 3. Questions left unanswered

**Commitment.** Q4: substitution and screening — later Iannaccone-channel labels; 1992 body closed. Q5: club good with participatory crowding vs private good merely co-consumed — Iannaccone body closed; official product is individual sobriety today (S12) in a rented room. Co-consumption is visible; his crowding-quality claim is not licensed. Q9: Hall 1988 closed.

**Conversion / network.** Q2: overbalance cannot be operationalized — secondary wording, not an opened 1980 AJS body; no member-tie / nonmember-tie ratio at first meeting. Q4 remainder: religious three as measured, turning point, extra-cult neutralization, funnel order. *Doomsday Cult* closed. Q5 necessity: no comparison sample.

**NRM interiors.** Q3: stayers and leavers exist, but not two populations under roughly constant intensity from the same period. Q4: cannot sample retained and departed from the same period as a design. Q5: mode of exit vs age of socialisation — neither systematically evidenced. Q6: meeting count and sobriety time are survey marks, not Ransom et al.’s self-measurement. Lalich and Introvigne 2022 stay closed.

**Brand community / fandom.** Q5: internal heterogeneity vs continuity — unanswered. Thomas et al. 2013 and Schouten and McAlexander 1995 stay closed. Brand-community as defined remains a mismatch even where Q1–Q4, Q6 can be asked of fellowship marks.

**Movement failure.** Q1–Q4 as Tarrow case tests: mismatch, not pending more A.A. facts. Taylor 1989, Tarrow 2011 Part III, and Tarrow 2022 Chs 9–11 remain unopened. Anderson 2009 is opened and not imported.
