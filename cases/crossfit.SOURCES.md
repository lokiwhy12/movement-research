## Access notes
- HQ does not publish member-dues. Membership band is from an affiliate explainer (Aerial, 2026-03-06) and a gym-software vendor survey (Gymdesk). Both opened. Region variance is explicit.
- Affiliate annual fee conflicts on official pages: $2,500–$4,500 (open-gym / application overview) vs US$4,500 standard (FAQ + Support article). Both recorded.
- Games ticket dollars and Open shirt-bundle dollars were visible in search snippets of official URLs that timed out on fetch. **Not used.**
- CCFT exam fee is behind the certifications portal. Application $150 is on the opened overview.
- Full affiliate license agreement is e-signed, not published.
- Wikipedia is a map for origin counts and 2024 Games death. Injury/rhabdo and 2020 leadership critiques that meet the bar are Outside + AP + CNN.
