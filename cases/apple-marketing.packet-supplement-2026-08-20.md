## 1. What kind of case Apple is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** Public company selling devices and services: no communal residence; daily container is the device (packet §0, §5, §6). Kanter’s opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Apple is not that sample. Continuance (premium bands; 14-day-then-zero; lease; Apple One / AppleCare One; Developer dues) is the closest fit. Cohesion and control run through Store, account, and calendar without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Acquisition is a mix: cultural presence and other people’s devices; Store + Specialist; Switch / trade-in; keynote; Developer funnel (§0, §7). Network entry on Stark’s 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian convert series. Religious-seekership does not map as written for a modal first device.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet’s high-control scan is observational, not a verdict (§13). No communal living requirement is found (§13 item 4). Harvey’s blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default customer condition. Lifton’s milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). Cult-of-Apple / temple / religion language stays the packet’s opened-press paraphrase (S20), not this note’s verdict. Official silence stays recorded, not filled (§7 objection 10).

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Closer here than Landmark or NXIVM. Muniz and O’Guinn used Macintosh as a named sample. Membership mark: “Apple person,” visible device, Developer. Brand community is “a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand” (Muniz and O’Guinn 2001). Store, Family plan, and Developer rooms are interacting, not a brand public. Official talk is privacy / just works, not Kozinets’ religion/myth as a finding. Harley 1995 stays closed.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A living public company selling devices and services is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule’s individual disengagement can be asked of platform-leavers; field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: 599–2499 USD bands; 14-day then none; Upgrade terms; Apple One / AppleCare One; Developer 99 / 299 USD (§8, S4–S9, S16). Cohesion: Store / Family / keynote witness; visible device (§5, §6, S3). Control: Activation Lock vs returns; jail-break voids warranty repair; App Store review; privacy / just-works as doctrine (§13 items 5, 9; S9, S11). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: money and an Apple Account put a stake in the next device; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); 14-day-then-zero is the nearest analogue (S9). Sacrifice is partly codeable: premium price, launch-day obtain — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is partial: Store or Family contact (§5, §6), not communal property. Mortification is partial at most: Genius / trade-in sit in the packet’s confession/closure row (§5); do not upgrade service to her mortification. Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical services, pp.99–102). Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is not codeable. RDF is an opened observer term (S18–S19), not a 2026 living guru (§13 item 1).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense (complain but do not leave; S20), not that sequence. Iannaccone’s price on outside activity: 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (Genius; trade-in). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing customer arrangements. Do not upgrade a repair visit to mortification.

**Q7.** Think Different, here is to you, and privacy as a fundamental human right are official meaning claims (§1, §2, S1–S2). Slogan and brand authority, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: founded 1976; still selling on 2026 pages (§0, §12, S1–S2) — corporate/product span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable and communion never leaves Store / account / Family; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if cultural presence plus first-device alone produces belonging; if 14-day leave is common and unpenalized beyond money already spent (S9).

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry runs through a mix: seeing others’ devices and ads; unknown Specialists and free sessions; Switch / trade-in; keynote (§0, §7, S13–S14). Proportion is not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: Cook letter sells identity; Personal Setup installs daily proof; Apple One / Developer / WWDC reveal inner-circle rank (§3, §7). Store-warmed attachment, then distinctions — consistent with Stark 1996’s belief-follows-attachment restatement, extended to a secular brand.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions, affective bond is present as Specialist / session / device-as-identity warmth (their “cult” names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as daily device use — not daily contact that makes every verbal convert a deployable agent. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured “acutely felt, enduring discrepancy.” Religious problem-solving perspective and religious seekership are mismatched (consumer tech, not church; §0, §1). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted for modal customers (no live-in; no published shunning rule; §13 items 3–4, 10). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips’ partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Switch corridor, Store, ads, and seeing devices require outward ties. Lexicon and ecosystem can raise a boundary; they do not close the network.

**Q7.** Mix, weighted to cultural / media presence plus Store invitation. The closer is the Store and the first object (§7). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. Ads / Store / Switch is entry. One / Care / second device / Developer / WWDC are retention. Employee-count and campaign-award lines are stock / prestige (§11). Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Customers do not co-reside (§13 item 4). What remains: lexicon, Apple-person / Developer identity, account-plus-ecosystem graph. Exit cost: 14-day-then-zero; Activation Lock; lease early-termination; leaving photos / messages / Watch / Mac; no sourced shunning doctrine (§13 item 10; S9, S16). Lock-in stays the packet’s paraphrase, not this note’s finding.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show App Store review without a closed-media system for customers; internal secrecy policy was not opened (§13 item 5). That does not test his dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey’s two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet’s split is time-bounded purchase / Store vs lifestyle / ecosystem (§13 items 4, 7). People can buy and return inside 14 days. The two senses look separable here.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost” would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records 14-day leave paths, with exit penalties low-to-partial for the modal customer (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal buyer.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing brand. Apple person / Think Different vs conventional / Android switcher is the membership mark (§0b, §2, §6). Named other on 2026 official pages: convention, tracking, clunky stacks — not Microsoft as the enemy noun (§0b, §12). Mac-vs-PC lives in opened critiques (S20). Muniz and O’Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement; oppositional brand loyalty (Mac vs PC/Microsoft) is their 2001 sample claim, not a 2026 official enemy.

**Q2.** Interacting brand community at Store / Family / Developer, not a non-interacting brand public. Public-only (keynote clip without room ties) is top-of-funnel (§0, §6). Do not treat Muniz and O’Guinn as a how-to.

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Developer / unofficial evangelist / Specialist path (§4b; §7) thickens as the ladder is climbed. Not measured; not obviously “limited liability” for those who staff or ship.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official Apple talk does the reverse: privacy, just works; official pages do not answer is-Apple-a-cult (§1, S1–S2; §7 objection 10). Opened critiques (Al Jazeera; Atlantic; Belk via both; S20) supply cult / temple / religion language — the packet’s paraphrase, not this note’s verdict.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: visible device, compact lexicon, Developer rank (§2, §6). Recruiting: mediated (ads, keynote) plus interaction-primary at the Store. Device-and-Store first, not a largely-online public (§0, §5). Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** Apple is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow’s definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. A living public company selling devices and services is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization and institutionalization (pp.201–202) are contention terms. Differential decline of a periphery (pp.202–203) is a cycle-demobilization claim. Early-riser pattern with opportunity held constant (p.196) cannot be posed from a marketing packet.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. Apple is a living company (2026 pages still sell hardware, services, Developer, WWDC; S1–S16) and has not ended. Not “one ended scene.” Not Anderson’s scene. Not Tarrow’s cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule’s plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). 14-day return and platform-exit are individual (§7, §13 item 10). Field collapse is not observed (still delivering; §9).

## 3. Questions left unanswered

**Commitment.** Q4: substitution and screening — later Iannaccone-channel labels; 1992 body closed. Q5: club good with participatory crowding vs private good merely co-consumed — Iannaccone body closed; official product is individual tools in the hand (§1) plus Family / Store co-presence. Co-consumption is visible; his crowding-quality claim is not licensed. Q9: Hall 1988 still closed. Seeing-a-device could account for first belonging without the six-mechanism story.

**Conversion / network.** Q2: overbalance cannot be operationalized — secondary wording, not an opened 1980 AJS body; no member-tie / nonmember-tie ratio at first purchase. Q4 remainder: religious three, turning point, extra-cult neutralization, funnel order. *Doomsday Cult* closed. Q5 necessity: no comparison sample.

**NRM interiors.** Q3: stayers and leavers exist, but not two populations under roughly constant intensity from the same period. Q4: cannot sample retained and departed from the same period as a design. Q5: mode of exit vs age of socialisation — neither systematically evidenced. Q6: units, services attach, and Developer membership are organizational metrics (§11), not Ransom et al.’s self-measurement of the self. Lalich and Introvigne 2022 stay closed.

**Brand community / fandom.** Q5: internal heterogeneity vs continuity — unanswered. Thomas, Price and Schau 2013 stays closed; iPhone / Mac / Developer are product lanes, not Kozinets’ elective affinities. Schouten and McAlexander 1995 stays closed.

**Movement failure.** Q1–Q4 as Tarrow case tests: mismatch, not pending more Apple facts. Taylor 1989, Tarrow 2011 Part III, and Tarrow 2022 Chs 9–11 remain unopened. Anderson 2009 is opened and not imported.
