## 1. What kind of case Peloton is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** Public company selling hardware and subscriptions: no communal residence; daily container is the home room plus touchscreen or App (§0, §5, §6). Kanter's opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Not that sample. Continuance (hardware bands; All-Access auto-renew; home-trial return fee; used $95; no prorated month) is the closest fit. Cohesion and control run through Leaderboard, Teams, and instructors without commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Acquisition is a mix: ads; showroom; 30-day App trial; friend Leaderboard name; Facebook; used marketplace (§0, §7). Network entry on Stark's 1996 restatement, not a millenarian series. Religious-seekership does not map for a modal first class.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet's high-control scan is observational, not a verdict (§13). No communal living (§13 item 4). Harvey's blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default member condition. Lifton's milieu control is a 1950s thought-reform claim (Ch.22). Cult / tribe / religion language stays the packet's paraphrase (S23) or working label, not this note's verdict. Official silence stays recorded (§7 objection 10).

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Closer here than for A.A. or LDS. Brand community is "a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand" (Muniz and O'Guinn 2001). Official pages sell classes, instructors, and community as one offer around a branded good (§0, §1, S13–S14). Teams, high fives, and the official Facebook member page are interacting, not a brand public only. Official talk is boutique energy at home, not Kozinets' religion/myth as a finding.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is "a phase of heightened conflict across the social system" (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A living public company selling hardware and subscriptions is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule's individual disengagement can be asked of cancel / unused-bike; field collapse is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: Bike $1,695 (S3); All-Access $49.99/mo (S18–S19); App One $12.99 / App+ $28.99 (S19–S20); home-trial return $150 / $250 (S8); used $95 (S9); no prorated refund except listed Canadian / NY notes (S10). Cohesion: Leaderboard; Here Now; Teams; tags; high fives (§5, §6, S14–S17). Control: auto-renew; TOS / Membership Terms; commercial-use conversion; public-by-default metrics (§13 items 5, 9; S10–S12). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: hardware and All-Access put a stake in the next class; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); return fee plus no-prorate is the nearest analogue (S8, S10). Sacrifice is partly codeable: premium price, public rank, time — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Communion is partial: Teams / Leaderboard / Here Now (§5, §6), not communal property. Mortification is partial at most: high five / shoutout sit in the confession/closure row (§5). Renunciation is structurally unavailable as she defined it (competing ties dropped; couple/family weakened; institutional completeness including medical services, pp.99–102). Transcendence as "institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday" (pp.128–138) is not codeable. Charisma is distributed across named instructors, not a living founder-guru (§13 item 1; S13).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost plus later defense (bike as furniture; canceling wastes the object and the history; §3, §4b), not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (high five; shoutout; Team feed). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are not standing customer arrangements.

**Q7.** "Magic of classes, instructors, and community" and "habits beat motivation" are official meaning claims (§1, S13–S15). Slogan and brand authority, not her transcendence pages.

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: founded 2012; still selling on 2026 pages (§0, S1–S21) — corporate/product span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if renunciation remains unavailable; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if first class alone produces belonging; if cancel / pause is common and unpenalized beyond money already spent and unused hardware (S10; §13 item 10).

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix: ads and others' hardware; unknown showroom staff and a 30-day App trial; friend Leaderboard name; Facebook; used marketplace (§0, §7, S1–S2, S9). Proportion not quantified.

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: first class sells live-feeling home fitness; Leaderboard name / favorite instructor / hardware / Teams / milestones reveal inner-circle rank (§3, §7). Consistent with Stark 1996's belief-follows-attachment restatement.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions, affective bond is present as first-class / instructor / high-five warmth (their "cult" names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as recurring class use — not daily contact that makes every verbal convert a deployable agent. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured "acutely felt, enduring discrepancy." Religious problem-solving and seekership are mismatched (connected fitness, not church). Turning point is not systematically evidenced. Extra-cult neutralization is contradicted for modal customers (no live-in; no published shunning rule; cancel / pause exists; §13 items 3–4, 10). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips' partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Ads, showroom, App trial, and used-bike entry require outward ties. Tags and Teams raise a boundary; they do not close the network.

**Q7.** Mix, weighted to cultural / media presence plus showroom and App invitation. The closer is the first class and the first object (§7). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. Ads / App trial / showroom / used $95 are entry. All-Access, second machine, Teams, streaks, and Homecoming (external) are retention. Teams 395K+ (internal, 1 Feb 2026; S16) and the 2022 Foley 6.6M quote (S24) are stock / prestige, not a 2026 census. Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey's opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Customers do not co-reside (§13 item 4). What remains: lexicon, Leaderboard name, favorite instructor, Teams. Exit cost: pause; cancel at period end; no prorate; hardware without the library; $95 for the next new member; deletion wipes history; no sourced shunning doctrine (§13 item 10; S10, S12, S18). Lock-in stays the packet's paraphrase.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show TOS limits on scraping and third-party apps, and Privacy's public-by-default Leaderboard fields, without a closed-media system for customers (§13 item 5; S11–S12). That does not test his dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey's two senses, environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet's split is time-bounded App trial / first class vs hardware-in-the-room / instructor / Teams (§13 items 4, 7). App cancel-before-convert and 30-day hardware return minus a fee (S2, S8) keep the two senses separable.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming "blurred boundaries → high exit cost" would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records 30-day leave paths, with exit penalties low-to-partial for the modal first-yes and higher once hardware and history attach (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal buyer.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing brand. Member / Leaderboard name / Peloton-person vs isolated or inconsistent exerciser is the membership mark (§0b, §2, §6). Named official other: friction, isolation, inconsistency, one-size gyms — not a competing bike as the enemy noun (§0b). Atlantic supplies tribe / religion-shaped services and Homecoming-as-pilgrimage (S23). Muniz and O'Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement.

**Q2.** Interacting brand community at Teams / high fives / official Facebook member page, not a non-interacting brand public. Public-only (homepage clip without Team ties) is top-of-funnel (§0, §6, S14, S16).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. High fives, Team encourage, and Official Teams (§4b, S16–S17) thicken as the ladder is climbed. Not measured.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official talk does the reverse: classes, instructors, community; official pages opened do not use "cult" (§1, S13–S14; §7 objection 10). Atlantic (S23) supplies almost-religious zeal, Homecoming as pilgrimage, tattoos — the packet's paraphrase, not this note's verdict.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: Leaderboard name, tags, badges, visible hardware (§2, §6). Recruiting: mediated (ads, homepage) plus interaction-primary at Teams / Facebook / showroom. Hardware-and-class first, not a largely-online public only (§0, §5). Harley 1995 stays closed.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** Peloton is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow's definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline of a periphery, and early-riser pattern (pp.196, 201–203) are contention terms and cannot be posed from this packet.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. Peloton is a living company (2026 pages still sell hardware, All-Access, App, Teams; S1–S21) and has not ended. Not "one ended scene." Not Anderson's scene. Not Tarrow's cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule's plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Cancel, pause, and unused-bike-in-the-room are individual (§7, §13 item 10; S10, S18). Field collapse is not observed (still delivering; §9). NBC 2022 boom/bust (S24) stays external demand/overbuild reporting, not field collapse.
