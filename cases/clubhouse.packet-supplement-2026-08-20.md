## 1. What kind of case this is, for each lane

Two objects, labeled and not collapsed. (A) Invite-era status spike (2020–21): invite-as-status, stage/audience, celebrity rooms, waitlist, Opening Day 21 July 2021, then decay after open-invite / copycats / end of lockdown. (B) 2026 living chat app: official 2026 still ships (homepage / App Store / Terms July 22 2026; Live Rooms + Chats + Voice Messages + Houses + Plus). Do not treat (A) as still the living product. Do not treat (B) as the 2021 spike.

**Commitment (Kanter).** For-profit app, non-residential. Not her 1780–1860 utopian sample. Continuance is thin: drop-in / leave-quietly is low ordeal and low sunk cost; Plus / coin IAPs exist but SKUs unreconciled and which Plus is live US unpublished. Cohesion (rooms / clubs / Houses) is session-bound, not commune. Control (Guidelines / recording-for-safety / stage caste) without commune-ordering of existence. Sacrifice/renunciation/transcendence mostly unavailable. Object (A) invite-as-status is scarcity, not her sacrifice table. 2020–2026 is institutional span, not her 25-year member-commitment cut. Object (A) failed that cut as a spike; object (B) is the remainder.

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Mix — invite (A) was network-weighted; Opening Day / 2026 guest-by-link / App Store is open-door. Celebrity rooms as borrowed destiny, not 1965 millenarian seekership. Extra-cult neutralization contradicted (leave quietly; guest users). *Doomsday Cult* closed. Stark 1996: (A) closed network then opened; social implosion is not the packet's story — the door opened and friends left.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** Packet §13 observational, not a verdict. No communal living. Harvey blurred boundaries not default for a drop-in app. Lifton milieu control is 1950s thought-reform (Ch.22). Do not score. Critical press exists (Verge/SIO/Agora). Do not reconstruct SIO-primary (timed out).

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O'Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Partial. Closer than Occupy for (A) (invite identity, stage caste, clubs). Farther than Apple (status spike + chat app, not a durable consumer-brand community). Muniz and O'Guinn 2001 is consumer-brand. Harley 1995 stays closed. Object (A) had consciousness of kind (I got in). Object (B) is a free chat app plus Plus.

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Tarrow mismatch. A cycle of contention is "a phase of heightened conflict across the social system" (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). An invite-era status spike and a living 2026 chat app are not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings; do not run it.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Continuance, cohesion, and control appear (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as commune solutions. Continuance thin: drop-in / leave-quietly is low ordeal and low sunk cost. Plus / coins exist (App Store lists $4.99 / $5.99 / $6.99 monthly and $49.99 / $59.99 annual); which Plus is live US unpublished. Cohesion: rooms / clubs / Houses Open or Closed (2026 Terms) — session-bound. Control: Guidelines (August 2025); recording-for-safety (2026 Terms/Privacy); stage caste (official 2020: enter as audience; raise a hand; speakers invite you up). Not her commune-ordering of existence (Ch.4 p.90).

**Q2.** Sacrifice mostly unavailable (Table 1 p.95). (A) invite-as-status is scarcity, not her table; Wikipedia eBay up to $400 is secondary-market, not official. Investment partial: optional Plus/coins; Payments take-rate zero; leave-quietly weakens the seceder-nonreimbursement analogue (Ch.4 p.96). Renunciation unavailable (pp.99–102). Communion partial: hot-room contact, not communal property (pp.88, 110–116). Mortification partial: in-app report / T&S, not confession/criticism/probation (pp.122–125). Transcendence (pp.128–138) not codeable. "More human Internet" (Opening Day) is slogan.

**Q3.** Expense as time (Opening Day hour-plus — 2021, not restated 2026) and Wikipedia eBay listings. Her path: sacrifice raises psychic expense so members justify staying by belief (p.91). Packet shows scarcity plus decay, not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** In-app report, one appeal, Guidelines speak-up. Official 2026 records Live Rooms; official 2020 still asking whether to store — era split, not a finding. Not her confession/criticism/probation (pp.122–125). Recording / Agora / leak stay packet paraphrase.

**Q7.** Official 2020: leave feeling better (Check 1, 2, 3). Official 2021: more human place on the Internet. Official 2026 App Store: find your community; real connections. External: next great social network (CNBC paraphrase). Cult noun: official silence; CNBC fad / posterchild.

**Q8.** Cut is 25 years; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). 2020–2026 is institutional span, not member-commitment duration. (A) failed that cut as a spike. (B) is the remainder (Terms July 22 2026; app 26.08.13). ~100 vs ~200 headcount not reconciled.

**Q10.** Strained if drop-in / leave-quietly = continuance; if invite-as-status = her sacrifice; if rooms/Houses = communion; if 2020–2026 = 25-year member duration; if Plus SKUs are reconciled; if recording is upgraded to a legal finding.

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Mix. (A): leftover invite / waitlist (official 2020; waitlist gone Opening Day 21 July 2021). Celebrity rooms as borrowed destiny (Wikipedia / CNBC / Verge — not an official tile). (B): 2026 App Store / homepage; Guest Users by link (2026 Terms). Proportion not quantified.

**Q3.** Belief-follows-attachment at public-copy layer only (Stark 1996). Official 2020: voice builds empathy. Invite-era implicit: closed door means quality. Opening Day: always wanted it open. Official 2026: drop into live voice chats.

**Q4 (partial).** Affective bond: first hot room, friend-is-speaking, celebrity proximity. Intensive interaction: hours-long hangs — session-bound, not deployable-agent. Tension is §0b demand, not their measured discrepancy. Religious three and turning point not shown. Extra-cult neutralization contradicted (leave quietly; guest users; no shunning). *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction more visible in invite-era hot rooms than the predisposing three. Necessity not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: open network vs implosion. (A) closed then opened. Implosion is not the packet's story — the door opened and friends left. (B) App Store and guest-by-link require outward ties. CNBC/TechCrunch quotation of 2023 founders: harder to find friends and to fit long conversations.

**Q7.** Closer first contact: leftover invite / waitlist in (A); App Store / guest link in (B). Celebrity press is borrowed destiny, not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** Entry: invite / waitlist / celebrity / Opening Day / 2026 App Store / guest link. Retention: Club / House, Payments, Plus, Chat. Census unreconciled: Wikipedia 600k / 2m WAU / 10m WAU / minus 21%; Opening Day plus 10m since Android; CNBC 14.2m downloads.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey is whole-life entanglement and high exit cost. No communal living. Blurred boundaries not default for a drop-in app. Exit: leave quietly; delete account (Terms); Plus cancel via store; UK/EEA 14-day (Terms). No shunning doctrine. Separable from co-residence.

**Q2.** Lifton: milieu control is the base condition (Ch.22); 1950s thought-reform, not a chat app. Packet shows Guidelines, recording-for-safety, contact hashes, Plus who-viewed without a closed-media church. Critical press exists (Verge SIO/Agora). SIO primary timed out — do not reconstruct. Do not score Lifton.

**Q7 (partial).** Environment-creating vs life-claiming look separable: (A) invite identity / hours-long rooms without housing or employer capture; (B) rooms / Chats / Houses without a life-claim. Packet §13 observational.

**Q8 (partial).** Intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement. Leave-quietly, account delete, store-cancel. Exit penalties contractual (Plus generally non-refundable after the window; doxxing permanent ban), not whole-life loss.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind: (A) I got in — invite-holder vs still waiting; stage vs mute. (B) free chat app plus Plus. No required competing consumer brand. Official foil: typed social media (2020–21); Zoom / empty scrolling (2023 via TechCrunch quotation).

**Q2.** Partial. (A) rooms, clubs, Wednesday Orientations / Sunday Town Hall — interacting. (B) Live Rooms + Chats + Houses interacting if anyone is there; brand-public risk when friends leave. Muniz and O'Guinn 2001 is consumer-brand. Harley 1995 closed.

**Q3 (partial).** Moral responsibility limited: leftover invites; Opening Day post-your-link; moderate; Guidelines (no selling access); Payments 100% to creator.

**Q4.** Official talk is voice chats / drop-in. Do not upgrade to Kozinets' religion/myth. Cult noun: official silence; CNBC fad / posterchild stay reporting.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: invite-holder / speaker / House founder / Plus. Recruiting: leftover invites (A); Opening Day open download; 2026 App Store / guest link.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** Not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow 2022 p.195 does not apply. Invite-era spike and living 2026 chat app are not automatically that. Mismatch. STOP. Closed vs transformative not run. Do not reconstruct Tarrow 2011 Part III or 2022 Chs 9–11.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization, institutionalization, differential decline, early-riser (pp.196, 201–203) cannot be posed here.

**Q5.** Both as labeled layers. Continuing-practice: (B) 2026 app still ships (homepage "join live voice chats"; App Store free; Terms July 22 2026; Guidelines August 2025). Ended-spike / decayed-control: (A) did not survive open-invite / copycats / end of lockdown. Wikipedia lists incumbent copycats. CNBC/TechCrunch quotation: harder to find friends; half-staff April 27 2023; Clubhouse 2.0; years of runway. Neither Anderson's Philadelphia rave/EDM nor Tarrow's cycle. Anderson 2009 (Wiley, 2026-08-20): her five forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims about her scene, not imported.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule: individual leave-quietly / uninstall is askable; field collapse is not recorded. Copycat live-audio rooms on larger networks are not automatically field collapse of Clubhouse-the-app. (A) decayed; (B) still ships.
