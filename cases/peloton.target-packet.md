## Executive summary

Peloton is a public connected-fitness company (Peloton Interactive, Inc.; Nasdaq PTON) that sells a premium at-home class as a tribe, not just a bike. Official voice (instructors page, community-features article, milestones article, Teams pages, shop/membership support) bundles three things as one offer: classes, instructors, and community. Hardware is the altar. All-Access is the ticket. The Leaderboard name is the public self. Instructors are the parasocial clergy. Milestones, badges, streaks, Teams, tags, high fives, and shoutouts are the recognition liturgy.

Movement-DNA read from pages opened this pass: Peloton resolves a structural tension most fitness systems cannot — convenience and community arrive together. You do not commute to find a tribe. The tribe is already on the screen. Recurrence is cheap in time and expensive in identity: every class adds a countable proof (output, rank, badge, streak, shoutout). Exit costs are hardware sunk cost, unused equipment in the room, lost Leaderboard history, lost Teams, and a broken instructor attachment. The subscription is month-to-month on All-Access (opened Membership Terms + support), but the bike does not become a dumb object when you cancel; content on the touchscreen requires All-Access (opened shop footnotes and support).

Layer split (do not collapse):

- **Official self-description (opened):** boutique energy at home; “magic of classes, instructors, and community”; convenience and community are compatible; milestones are “more than just a number”; Teams as accountability; 30-day home trial; household All-Access; health content framed as educational/entertainment only (TOS §17).
- **External reporting (opened):** pandemic demand surge then bust; Foley resignation and layoffs (NBC 2022); Tread+ child-death recall and later CPSC civil-penalty reporting (CPSC recall opened; $19M penalty page timed out this pass); instructor-as-celebrity and Homecoming-as-pilgrimage (Atlantic 2019); 2024 instructor contract exits (NY Mag); Wikipedia as pointer-then-open for corporate timeline, valuation collapse, and product history.

This is a high-adhesion commercial tribe, not a doctrine-heavy movement. The mechanics still extract: charisma at scale, visible effort, recurring ritual, status accumulation, and hardware lock-in.

## 0b) Environmental conditions (“why now / why it scaled”)

3–7 condition → demand → promise:

1. **Time scarcity + commute friction** → demand for boutique-class feeling without travel/parking/studio slots → official: the class is in the room; “class is always in session” (homepage).
2. **Social isolation / lonely workouts** → demand to be seen while sweating alone → Leaderboard, Here Now, high fives, Teams, tags, official Facebook member page (community-features article).
3. **Boutique-fitness aspiration + income stratification** → demand for SoulCycle-grade coaching without a $40 single-class meter (Atlantic comparison is historical; live studio prices **not opened** this pass) → hardware prestige + All-Access household.
4. **Quantified-self culture** → demand for effort that produces a number other people can see → output, cadence, resistance, heart-rate, rank, PRs, weekly Team stats.
5. **Pandemic home-fitness spike (external)** → demand for structure and company when gyms closed → hardware boom, then overbuild (NBC 2022; Wikipedia). Official pages opened this pass do not narrate the bust.
6. **Instructor-as-media / parasocial era** → demand for a person to return to, not a playlist → instructors page “magic of classes, instructors, and community”; Atlantic Homecoming as pilgrimage.
7. **Subscription-software normal** → demand to keep paying for a living library rather than own a dead bike → All-Access required on hardware (shop + support); used-bike $95 activation as a second-hand toll.

**Meaning crisis named (official, implicit):** generic fitness is inconvenient, lonely, unmotivating, and easy to quit; you will not become “the best version of yourself” (Privacy mission line) without a coach, a metric, and a tribe.

**Enemy / chaos named:**
- Official implicit: friction, isolation, inconsistency, one-size gyms, working out without being seen.
- External: pandemic loneliness; later, the company’s own overbuild, recall, and instructor churn (NBC, CPSC, NY Mag). Official shop/membership pages opened this pass do not name those as enemies.

**Certainty / belonging / status offered:** a named screen identity, a favorite instructor, a countable history, a Team, a badge cabinet, and a household membership that makes the living room a studio.

## 1) Promise + positioning (the offer)

**Explicit promise (1 sentence, official):** world-class classes, instructors, and community — boutique coaching and motivation at home or on the App, with measurable progress.

**Implicit promise:** you do not have to choose between convenience and belonging; showing up becomes who you are; a charismatic stranger on a screen will see you (or someone like you will).

**Pain named:** gym friction, boredom, inconsistency, schedule mismatch, lonely workouts, lack of motivation. Homepage Tiffany quote: more than a fitness platform — survival, healing, growth (testimonial layer).

**Time-to-win:** the next class can start now. App trial 30 days (membership page). Hardware 30-day home trial (home-trial). Milestones start at class 1 (milestones article). Official does not sell a 30-day body transformation as a clinical claim.

**Before / after identity:** isolated or inconsistent exerciser → Member with a Leaderboard name, a favorite instructor, a badge cabinet, and a Team.

**Positioning markers (opened):** “Experience the magic of Peloton’s classes, instructors, and community” (instructors); convenience and community as compatible (community-features); “It’s so much more than just a number” (milestones); “Crush goals, cheer friends, and connect” (Teams).

Representative arc from pages opened this pass. Not a universal member biography.

2. **First-class activation** — instructor energy, music, metrics, Leaderboard. Goal: immediate felt proof that home fitness can feel live. Expected state: surprise, endorphins (Atlantic first-person; official testimonials).
3. **Identity hook** — pick a Leaderboard name, a favorite instructor, maybe a Tag. Goal: the screen now has a self on it.
4. **Hardware yes (or rental / refurbished / used)** — Cross Training Bike from $1,695 or Affirm $141.25/mo at 0% APR / 12 mo; All-Access separate. Goal: put the altar in the room. Commitment device: sunk cost + 30-day trial with a return fee.

7. **Discipline expansion** — strength / yoga / tread / row / bootcamp / Artist Series so the membership covers “whole fitness life.”

9. **Advocacy / pilgrimage** — tags, Team recruit, Homecoming / On Tour, tattoos in the Atlantic 2019 crowd (external; not official).

### Working score
