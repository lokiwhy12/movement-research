## Identity labels
- crazy ones / misfits / rebels / troublemakers / round pegs — hero class in the 1997 ad and the 2026 Cook letter
- the ones who do — Cook letter close (people crazy enough to think they can change the world)
- someone who thinks different — the after-state the letter enrolls
- Apple person / Mac person — unofficial everyday identity; implied by shop-Mac complementarity copy
- switcher — official conversion identity on Switch to iPhone
- Developer — paid inner-circle identity (99 USD Program)
- Specialist / Genius — staff identities that the customer meets as priests of the temple
- Here is to you — the letter's move that makes the customer the anniversary subject

## Rank / ladder labels
- passerby / session-goer -> first-device customer -> multi-device / Apple One / AppleCare One member -> Developer (99 USD/year) -> Enterprise (299 USD/year) -> WWDC / Design Awards -> unofficial evangelist
- Specialist / Genius / Today at Apple presenter are employment ranks, not a published customer sash
- Upgrade lessee is a term-rank (12/24 Watch+iPhone; 24/36 Mac+iPad), not a title
- Family / Premier on Apple One is a household rank (up to five others)
- Neo / Air / Pro / Studio and 17e / 17 / Air / Pro are product ranks that read as taste ranks

The missing log line is that rank is mostly device-plus-account fluency, not a belt. Developer and Genius are the everyday visible ranks.

## Phrase shapes
- Think Different — different as a noun, not an adverb (Wikipedia: Jobs wanted it like think big)
- Here's to the crazy ones — 1997 close still running as here is to you in 2026
- If you love iPhone, you will love Mac
- Privacy. That's Apple.
- Switching from Android to iPhone is super simple
- We believe you will be thrilled (Sales Policy)
- Technology alone is not enough
- Come see the best of Apple
- Reality Distortion Field — observer/staff term (Hertzfeld / Nash / Wikipedia), not a 2026 marketing slogan

The addition for the log: Apple's public nouns are taste and virtue (privacy, creativity, personal) while the verbs are calendar and keep (upgrade, switch, ship, just works). The phrase that travels is Think Different plus here is to you.
