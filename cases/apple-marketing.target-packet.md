## Executive summary

Apple is a public company that sells hardware, software, and services. The movement-DNA read is not the chip table. It is a persuasion system that turns a device purchase into an identity declaration, then keeps that identity alive with ritualized launches, retail temples, a small portable lexicon, and an ecosystem that rewards staying.

Official pages opened this pass still run the 1997 Think Different grammar in 2026. The 50th-anniversary newsroom piece and the Cook letter on apple.com recast the customer as the person who thinks different, and close by toasting the crazy ones, misfits, and rebels. Store pages sell the first yes as a Specialist conversation, a free Today at Apple session, a trade-in, or a lease (Apple Upgrade). Services pages (Apple One, AppleCare One, Developer Program) convert the identity into a monthly or annual keep-paying loop. Retail and the 50th-anniversary store concerts treat the Apple Store as a congregation space, not only a checkout.

The prior stub named six portable primitives and left prices, terms, the 8-type ritual table, the 1-to-9 map, objections, scores, section 13, and numbered sources thin or missing. Those holes are filled below from opened pages, or marked honest remaining unknown. Founder-origin, Think Different, keynote / Reality Distortion Field, and retail-temple beats are in this packet because those pages were opened this pass.

Movement-DNA read: Apple is a myth-first identity system whose flagship ritual is the synchronized launch, whose daily container is the device in the hand, whose temple is the Store / Genius Bar / Today at Apple session, and whose escalation ladder is device to devices to Apple One / AppleCare One to Developer Program. The durable mechanics are (1) a tiny lexicon that casts the buyer as a creator/rebel, (2) staged keynotes that create shared attention, (3) premium price as a seriousness filter, (4) ecosystem complementarity as sunk-cost and convenience, (5) retail as pilgrimage and aftercare, and (6) a privacy / just-works doctrine that answers the lock-in objection in the company voice.

## 0b) Environmental conditions (why now / why it scaled)

3-7 conditions to demand to promise:

1. Late-90s PC commodity chaos and a wounded Apple brand -> demand for a coherent identity, not another spec sheet -> Think Different casts buyers as the crazy ones (Wikipedia Think Different, opened; 2026 Cook letter still runs the line).
2. Always-on pocket computing + status visibility of the device -> demand for a public taste badge -> iPhone as the everyday identity object; Switch page: make it you, through and through.
3. Subscription and cloud lock-in as the default consumer pattern -> demand for one bundle that feels like membership, not six invoices -> Apple One (19.95 / 27.95 / 39.95 USD) and AppleCare One (19.99 / 5.99 USD).
4. Distrust of tracking-based consumer tech -> demand for a moral reason to stay -> Privacy. That is Apple. / privacy as a fundamental human right (privacy marketing page).
5. Launch-culture media and social video -> demand for a shared holy day -> keynote / WWDC / 50th store concerts as synchronized witness.
6. Creator-class and student markets that want tools-plus-belonging -> demand for a pilgrimage site and a cheap-feeling on-ramp -> retail temples, Today at Apple, education gift-card offer, Developer Program 99 USD.
7. Fatigue with clunky multi-vendor stacks -> demand for just-works as a worldview, not a slogan -> Switch page ease-of-use / seamless block; ecosystem complementarity on shop Mac (If you love iPhone, you will love Mac).

Meaning crisis named (implicit, official language): technology that is impersonal, conventional, not enough by itself; a world that is not moved forward unless someone imagines a different path. Cook letter: progress begins with someone who imagines a better way.

Enemy / chaos named: on 2026 official pages opened this pass — convention, tracking (Safari vs Chrome table), clunky other-phone stacks (Switch page implies Android as the before-state), accidents / loss (AppleCare). Historical satanic myth (Gates / PC) is in opened critiques and Wikipedia RDF/Think Different context, not in the 2026 Cook letter. Official 2026 copy does not print cult or Microsoft as the enemy noun.

Certainty / belonging / status offered: a tribe of people who think different; a visible device; Specialist / Genius / Developer ranks; WWDC / store concert witness; privacy as virtue; Here is to you.

## 1) Promise + positioning (the offer)

Explicit promise (1 sentence): put powerful, personal tools in the hands of people who think different — so they can express themselves, connect, and create something wonderful (50th newsroom + Cook letter).

Implicit promise: buying Apple is a declaration that you are one of them — creative, not conventional — and that the object will keep proving it every day.

Pain named: impersonal / conventional tech; clunky smartphones (Jobs-era keynote problem-setup, Nash); tracking; accidents and loss; the friction of switching and of many subscriptions.

Time-to-win: immediate (unbox / Personal Setup / just works); launch day (shared awe); 14-day return window as a low-regret first yes; then years of ecosystem keep.

Before / after identity: from generic-tech user / Android switcher / conventional thinker -> someone who thinks different; an Apple person; optionally a Developer.

Positioning markers: myth over spec (Think Different showed no product in the brand portraits); privacy as doctrine; retail as experience; premium price; technology alone is not enough.

### Working score

- Believability: 4/5 insiders / 3/5 outsiders who hear cult-of-Apple (opened critiques). Product quality is the proof the myth leans on; official pages do not argue the cult charge.
- Urgency: 4/5 around launch / Upgrade / trade-in windows / education gift-card; 2/5 as everyday brand (the urgency is the calendar, not a countdown clock on the homepage).
- Distinctiveness: 5/5 in consumer tech as an identity church; 3/5 if scored only as another premium-hardware funnel.

## 3) Transformation sequence (the protocol)

Staged from pages opened. Do not invent unboxing liturgy or keynote run-of-show beyond what Nash / newsroom / shop pages state.

A. Pre-encounter — cultural presence, other people devices, Think Different residue, newsroom / social clips of launches and store concerts.
B. Myth recognition — Cook letter / campaign grammar: you are (or could be) one of the people who think different.
C. Low-threat first yes — visit a Store; book a Vision Pro demo; sit a free Today at Apple session; talk to a Specialist; open Switch-to-iPhone; run a trade-in estimate. 0 USD on pages opened.
D. Commitment moment — buy or lease. 14-day clock starts on receipt (Sales Policy). Activation Lock and original-condition rules bind the exit.
E. Initiation — pickup or delivery; Personal Setup; first use. Shop copy treats this as helped, not alone.
F. Proof window — daily use + just works + optional Genius Bar / AppleCare. The object is the argument.
G. Social reorientation — visible device; Messages / FaceTime / iCloud; family Apple One; being the person who has an iPhone in the meeting.
H. Escalation — second device (you will love Mac); Apple One; AppleCare One; Developer Program; WWDC; watching or attending the next keynote.
I. Advocacy — showing the device; defending the brand; filming the store concert on iPhone (50th newsroom); shipping an app.

### Working score
- Repeatability: 5/5 — the funnel is industrial and published.
- Speed: 4/5 to first identity hit (hours to a weekend); years to full ecosystem fusion.
- Durability: 5/5 while the device + account + social proof remain; 2/5 after a clean platform exit (not described as easy on official pages).
