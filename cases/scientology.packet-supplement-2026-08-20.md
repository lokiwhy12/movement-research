## 1. What kind of case Scientology is, for each lane

**Commitment (Kanter).** Living religious organization: donated auditing/training on a named Bridge, ethics language, and — for a subset — Sea Org communal life (§0, §5, §8). Kanter’s opened pages treat commitment as an organizational problem in 1780–1860 utopian communities, success cut at 25 years (1972, Appendix p.260). Scientology is not that sample. Continuance (donation-gated ladder; AOSHEU generally no refund) is the closest public fit. Cohesion and control thicken in the course room, ethics/SP frame, and Sea Org without commune-ordering of existence for the whole Church (Ch.4 p.90).

**Conversion / network (Lofland–Stark / Stark).** Official acquisition: free OCA / personality test; locate-a-church; Sunday; missions that bring new people into contact (§0, §7; S10, S15). Network entry on Stark’s 1996 restatement (ties; open network vs social implosion), not a millenarian series. Religious-seekership and extra-cult-detachment do not map as written for a modal test-taker.

**NRM interiors (Lifton / Harvey / Melton / Ransom).** The packet’s high-control scan is observational and contested, not a verdict (§13). Public orgs are not live-in on pages opened (§5). Sea Org official: communal housing, meals, uniforms, long hours, allowance (S11). Harvey’s blurred boundaries — leaving employer, housing, and social orbit (Harvey §§1–2) — are not the default public condition; closer as an official Sea Org description. Melton 2018 takes Sea Org as his sample; positionality travels.

**Brand community / fandom (Muniz and O’Guinn / Kozinets / Arvidsson and Caliandro).** Official pages present a religion (scripture, ministry, Sunday, marks), not a consumer brand. Brand community is “a specialized, non-geographically bound community, based on a structured set of social relationships among admirers of a brand” (Muniz and O’Guinn 2001). Course room, Sunday, events, and co-audit are interacting, not a brand public. Official talk is religion, not Kozinets’ fan-side religion/myth as stigma-management. Cult / brainwashing language stays official denial versus opened external charge (S18; S21–S22).

**Movement failure (Tarrow 2022 Ch.8 / Fillieule 2015).** Mismatch for Tarrow. A cycle of contention is “a phase of heightened conflict across the social system” (Tarrow 2022, print p.195). A living religious organization is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is his fork for cycle endings (p.195); do not run it. Fillieule’s individual disengagement can be asked of people who left fellowship; field collapse of the religion is not recorded.

## 2. Answers the packet can support

### Commitment

**Q1.** Arrangements touch continuance, cohesion, and control as three problems (Kanter Ch.3 pp.86–89), not as three equally built commune solutions. Continuance: donate for auditing/training; AOSHEU generally not refundable; dual path without a published dollar table (§8, S15, S20). Cohesion: Sunday; course room; nine events; Sea Org communal life (S11, S19). Control: conditions formulas; Confessional; SP label so others know not to associate; RTC as final arbiter (S5, S8, S14). Separable as problems.

**Q2.** Investment is codeable: a first donation puts a stake on the chart; successful groups generally did not reimburse seceders (Ch.4 p.96); AOSHEU no-withdrawal is the nearest analogue (S20). Sacrifice is partly codeable: donated hours; Sea Org long hours + symbolic billion-year pledge (S11–S12) — not abstinence, austerity, or celibacy as Table 1 coded them (p.95). Official dollars unpublished. Communion is partial: course room / Sunday / Sea Org shared housing (S11). Mortification is partial: ethics Confessional (S5); do not upgrade every session to her mortification. Renunciation is partial and contested: official last-resort handle-or-disconnect and no required family-cut for mere belief difference (S13); SP as a listed Suppressive Act (S14); external pages describe practiced cuts (S21–S22). Transcendence as “institutionalized awe, insulated hierarchy, non-participative decisions, tradition as the authority of the eternal yesterday” (pp.128–138) is partly codeable: Hubbard’s words are scripture; RTC holds the marks (S7–S8).

**Q3.** Something expensive is present. Her path is that sacrifice raises the psychic expense of having joined, so members justify staying by belief (p.91). The packet shows cost-shape plus later defense (antagonism wipes gains; setback as incomplete application or SP; §4b), not that sequence. Iannaccone 1992 body closed.

**Q6.** Confession-like practices are in the ritual table (auditing processes; ethics Confessional). Mutual criticism, surveillance, and probationary socialization (pp.122–125) are only partly standing (S5). Do not upgrade every process to her mortification.

**Q7.** “Walk it and become free,” Hubbard-as-scripture, and RTC orthodoxy are official meaning claims (S3, S7–S8). Slogan and scripture authority, not her transcendence pages as coded. Official believe-FAQ: do not have to believe; apply and see (S18).

**Q8.** Success cut is 25 years of community duration; no unsuccessful coded case exceeded 16 (Appendix p.260). Analogues: Dianetics 1950; Sea Org 1967; official 2026 pages still delivering (S19) — organizational span, not member-commitment duration.

**Q10.** Stated in advance, a commitment-mechanism reading would be strained if the three problems cannot be separated; if cost exists without her belief-justification path; if the free test plus cultural presence alone produces belonging; if taking a Sunday and leaving is common and unpenalized beyond money already donated (S15).

### Conversion / network

**Q1.** Entry is a mix, proportion not quantified. Cold-door: OCA CTA; locate-a-church; free Sunday (S15, S19). Organized outreach: missions; Volunteer Ministers; FSM named, page 404 (S10). Celebrity / friend adjacency is external or unnamed (S22).

**Q3.** Progressive revelation: test/Sunday sell a map; donated auditing/training reveals the chart and next named state (§3, §7). Official: study, apply, and see if it works (S18, paraphrase) — consistent with Stark 1996’s belief-follows-attachment restatement.

**Q4 (partial).** Of the seven 1965 conditions: affective bond is present as consultation / Sunday / first-session warmth (their “cult” names D.P., not this case). Intensive interaction is present as course-room days and, for Sea Org, communal daily contact — not as the condition that turns every verbal convert into a deployable agent. Tension is an environmental demand in §0b, not their measured “acutely felt, enduring discrepancy.” Religious problem-solving sits next to the no-belief-required line (S18). Religious seekership, turning point, and extra-cult neutralization are not systematically evidenced for modal public (S13); extra-cult is contested externally (S21–S22). Funnel order cannot be tested. *Doomsday Cult* closed.

**Q5 (partial).** Affective bond and intensive interaction — the two that survived Snow and Phillips’ partial critique — are more visible than the predisposing three. Necessity is not shown.

**Q6.** Stark 1996: growth requires internal ties plus an open social network; social implosion stops recruitment. Missions, OCA CTA, Sunday, and Volunteer Ministers require outward ties (S10, S15). SP / last-resort disconnect / public renunciation as a Suppressive Act are inward boundary (S13–S14). Open-network at the free door; 2026 pages still recruit.

**Q7.** Mix: cultural presence / Dianetics residue / celebrity adjacency (external) plus organized-group invitation (missions, test, Sunday). Not the 1980-abstract media-occult boundary.

**Q8.** The packet often mixes layers. OCA / Sunday / beginning services are entry. Donated intensives, Clear, advanced org, staff/Sea Org are retention. Opened conversion bodies explain entry, not maintenance.

### NRM interiors

**Q1.** Harvey’s opened set is written around whole-life entanglement and high exit cost from leaving employer, housing, and social orbit. Public participants do not co-reside on pages opened (§5, §13 item 4). What remains: lexicon, chart identity, ethics on setbacks. Sea Org official communal life is the closer Harvey condition (S11). Exit cost: AOSHEU generally no refund; official SP = loss of fellowship until restored; official disconnection as last-resort. External: family cuts, file-leverage, labor intensity (S21–S22). Blurred boundaries are separable from co-residence for the modal public.

**Q2.** Lifton makes milieu control the base condition on which the other seven themes depend (Ch.22). The packet can show official exact-scripture / RTC-against-altered-tech / view-only ToU / confidential files, and an external OT-secrecy / critic-filter layer (§13 item 5; S17, S20–S21). That does not test his dependency claim.

**Q7 (partial).** Harvey’s two senses: environment-creating and life-claiming. The packet’s split is time-bounded public (Sunday / first session / beginning course) vs Sea Org total-life (§5, §13 item 4). The two senses look separable for modal public.

**Q8 (partial).** Disconfirming “blurred boundaries → high exit cost” would be intensity without Harvey-scale entanglement and without high exit cost. The packet records a free congregational door and last-resort disconnection on official pages, with higher exit penalties once donation, confession, and SP-class break are in play (§13 item 10). That undercuts the strong link for the modal test-taker, not for official Sea Org communal life.

### Brand community / fandom

**Q1.** Consciousness of kind does not require a competing church brand. Preclear / Clear / Scientologist / Sea Org vs outsider is the membership mark; Scientologist is a collective membership mark on the opened ToU (S20). Named official other: reactive mind; SP; inauthentic tech; psychiatry / deprogramming (S14, S18). External pages add journalists, Anonymous, “squirrels” (S21). Muniz and O’Guinn allowed Macintosh consciousness without an opponent as a universal requirement.

**Q2.** Interacting community at Sunday / course room / co-audit / events / Sea Org, not a non-interacting brand public. Public-only (bio clip without room ties) is top-of-funnel (§0, §6).

**Q3 (partial).** Their moral responsibility is limited and specialized: integrate and retain; assist in proper use. Missions, Volunteer Ministers, and dissemination-as-purpose (S10, S19) thicken as the ladder is climbed. Not measured; not obviously “limited liability” for staff or Sea Org.

**Q4.** Kozinets treats religion/myth talk as a fan legitimacy device against commercial status. Official Scientology talk is institutional self-description — scripture, minister, Sunday (S7, S19) — not his fan articulation, and not this note’s religion finding. Cult / brainwashing is official “no; the opposite” (S18) versus Wikipedia / Urban as the public charge (S21–S22). Not collapsed.

**Q6 (partial).** Self-marking: short lexicon; chart level; Clear; Sea Org uniform/rank (§2, §6). Recruiting: mediated (events, site) plus interaction-primary at mission / test / Sunday. Room-and-org first.

### Movement failure

**Q1.** Scientology is not a cycle-of-contention case. Tarrow’s definition (2022, p.195) does not apply. A living religious organization is not automatically that. Closed vs transformative is not a fork to run. Stop.

**Q2–Q4.** Not this case. Radicalization and institutionalization (pp.201–202) are contention terms. Differential decline of a periphery (pp.202–203) is a cycle-demobilization claim. Early-riser pattern with opportunity held constant (p.196) cannot be posed from this packet.

**Q5.** Continuing-practice as the default. Official 2026 pages still publish the Bridge, funding, Sea Org, Sunday, events, and the test CTA (S1–S20). The packet can stand as a continuing-practice study of a living organization — not an ended scene, not Anderson’s scene, not Tarrow’s cycle. Do not collapse the three. Anderson 2009 *Sociological Forum* was opened 2026-08-20 (Wiley). Her sample is the Philadelphia rave/EDM scene. Her five named forces — generational schism, commercialization, cultural otherness/deviance and self-destruction, formal social control, genre-based scene fragmentation — are her claims, not this case.

**Q6 (limited).** Fillieule’s plurality: individual disengagement versus collective decline / field collapse (2015, opening pages). Official SP fellowship-loss and the thin Apostate Haggis resignation are individual (S14; S22). Field collapse of the religion is not observed (still delivering; §9).
