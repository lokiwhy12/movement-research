## Identity labels
- **CrossFitter** — the base post-threshold self; you are not someone who works out
- **athlete** — official room noun for any member, including first-day scaled
- **coach / trainer** — operator noun; L1+ required to run a CrossFit class
- **affiliate owner / Licensee of Record** — local sovereign
- **affiliate manager** — Open-season validator (one per location)
- **judge / registered judge** — seasonal ritual official
- **Seminar Staff** — HQ itinerant authority
- **Fittest on Earth** — apex identity, almost nobody’s, everyone’s myth
- **Games athlete** — pilgrimage caste
- **scaled / foundations athlete** — official inclusion nouns that can become local slurs
- **Rx’d** — as-prescribed identity, not just a load
- **box** — the place-name that replaces “gym”

---

## Rank / ladder labels
- drop-in → member → consistent → Open registrant → Community Cup level 1–10 → Quarterfinals → Semifinals → Games
- **Rx / scaled / foundations** is the everyday caste inside one class
- **CF-OL1 → CF-L1 → CF-L2 → CCFT / CF-L3 → CF-L4** — only L3+ may say “certified”
- **PR** as informal rank (a number you can say at dinner)
- **Open level 1–10** (percentile bands after all scores submitted)
- **National Champion** (2026 Rulebook: furthest-in-season per country, Rx-only)
- **affiliate owner** is a rank, not just a job
- Hero WOD completion (especially vest Murph) as moral-athletic rank

The existing log line (“short insider terms… score visibility”) is the headline. The addition is that **the same words are both ritual instructions and rank marks**; Rx is a noun you *are*.

## Ritual / action verbs
- **do the WOD** — the central verb-phrase
- **scale** — stay in the ritual; also a status confession
- **Rx / go Rx** — take the prestige form
- **log / post a score** — make the work social fact
- **PR** — beat yourself in public
- **judge** — hold the standard for another body
- **no-rep** — deny the rep; the confession/closure verb of competition
- **partition** — Murph pacing verb
- **kip** — the controversial power verb
- **affiliate** — become a licensed node
- **revalidate** — keep the credential alive
- **throw down** — Open marketing verb for compete
- **show up** — the membership virtue verb
- **forge** — legacy slogan verb (Forging Elite Fitness® — now restricted IP)

## Enemy / problem words
- **globo-gym / big-box** — anonymous, uncoached, unmeasured
- **specialization** — the methodological heresy vs GPP
- **couch / inactivity / chronic disease** — official mortal enemy (FAQ)
- **refined carbohydrates / sugar** — nutritional enemy
- **bored / inconsistent / unmeasured** — the pain the offer names
- **cheating** (as charged against kipping) — FAQ reframes as ignorance of power
- **soft / not hard enough** — unofficial local enemy
- **scaled** when used as insult — shadow of the inclusion word
- **the press / NSCA** — historical institutional enemies (Wikipedia lawsuit)
- **unknown and unknowable** — chaos the training claims to meet

## Phrase-structure patterns (why they travel)
- **X, but not Y** (“for anyone, but not for everyone”; “hard, but not out of reach”)
- **Every class is coached. Every workout can be scaled.** — two-sentence law
- **It’s hard. That’s the point.** — pain recoded as feature
- **What’s the WOD?** — insider greeting
- **Did you go Rx?** — status question in four syllables
- **as prescribed** — liturgical synonym for Rx
- **unknown and unknowable** — myth in a doublet
- **meat and vegetables, nuts and seeds, some fruit, little starch, no sugar** — diet as cadence
- **3, 2, 1… go** — trademarked start liturgy
- trademark theater on ordinary English (CrossFit®, WOD as house noun, Fittest on Earth™)
