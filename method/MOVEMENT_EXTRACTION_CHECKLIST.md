# Movement Extraction Checklist (v1)

Purpose: extract the **mechanics** of an existing movement/program/community and convert them into reusable analytical primitives for the movement-DNA database.

Operating premise (Loki): anything operationalized will be reviewed by **finance** and **legal** professionals; nothing ships against their direction.

Project role:
- one completed checklist = one target-analysis intake
- completed target packets feed the movement-DNA database
- extracted primitives may later inspire Monolith, but this checklist should stay descriptive and analytical first

---

## How to use
1) Pick a target (e.g., Landmark Forum).
2) Collect only **public artifacts** first (site pages, syllabus, intro videos, testimonials, pricing pages, terms/policies, press, third‑party critiques).
3) Fill this checklist section-by-section.
4) Produce a **Target Packet**:
   - 1-page summary
   - 10–20 extracted primitives
   - risks / constraints
   - what to borrow vs avoid
5) Repeat for 3–5 targets; reconcile into an integrated movement-DNA model (not a movement design unless Loki explicitly asks for that later).

## 0b) Environmental conditions (“why now?”)
Movements scale faster when they fit the ambient conditions.

**Questions**
- What macro conditions does this target ride?
  - rapid social/tech change
  - economic insecurity / inequality
  - social isolation / disconnection
  - moral uncertainty / value conflict
  - institutional distrust
  - information disruption (new channels/algorithms)
  - youth cohort dynamics
- What “meaning crisis” does it resolve?
- What enemy/chaos does it name (explicitly or implicitly)?
- What certainty/belonging/status does it offer?

**Extract**
- 3–7 bullets: conditions → demand → movement’s promise

## 1) Promise + positioning (the “offer”)
**Questions**
- What is the *explicit* promise in one sentence?
- What is the *implicit* promise people actually want?
- What pain is it naming?
- What does it claim is possible in what timeframe?
- What is the before/after identity?

**Extract**
- Tagline(s)
- Time-to-win claim (e.g., “3 days”)
- Outcome verbs (freedom, power, effectiveness, connection, etc.)

Score (1–5): clarity / believability / urgency / distinctiveness

---

## 2) Worldview primitives (the memetic atoms)
**Questions**
- What are the core nouns/verbs that rewire perception? (e.g., “context”, “story”, “racket”)
- Which terms are trademarked / special-cased?
- What distinctions do participants learn that feel like “new eyes”?

**Extract**
- Glossary of 10–30 key terms
- The “grammar”: how those terms combine into actions

Score: portability (can we reuse) / stickiness

---

## 3) Transformation sequence (the protocol)
Map the step-by-step arc.

**Questions**
- What’s the opening move? (disarm, intrigue, authority, curiosity, challenge)
- How do they create a felt gap between current self and possible self?
- What are the turning points?
- What is the commitment moment?
- What is the integration/aftercare structure?

**Deliverable**
- A numbered sequence: Step 1…N
- For each step: goal, mechanism, exercise, expected emotional state

Score: repeatability / speed / durability

---

## 4) Emotional arc (attention → catharsis → resolve)
**Questions**
- What emotions do they reliably elicit, and in what order?
- Where is relief released?
- Where do people feel pride?
- Where do people feel belonging?

**Extract**
- Arc graph: time vs emotion (high-level)
- 3–5 moments that produce the biggest “I get it now” shifts

Score: intensity control / coherence

---

## 4b) Psychological pull factors (why it feels irresistible)
Map the drivers that create sustained pull.

**Questions**
- Which needs does it hit hardest: meaning, belonging, status, agency, competence, novelty, certainty, catharsis?
- What are the “micro-rewards” (small wins) vs “macro-rewards” (identity shift)?
- What makes progress *legible* (public commitments, milestones, acknowledgements)?
- What’s the variable reward pattern (surprise insights, social recognition, breakthroughs)?
- What are the commitment devices (time, money, public declaration, role elevation)?
- What identity labels are offered (“graduate”, “leader”, etc.) and what privileges come with them?

**Extract**
- Top 5 drivers + how they’re implemented (mechanism → moment → reinforcement)
- Mechanism matrix (bias stack): map which levers are activated and where:
  - commitment/consistency
  - social proof
  - authority
  - identity fusion
  - cognitive dissonance + sunk cost
  - scarcity/exclusivity
  - emotional contagion
  - in-group/out-group
  - meaning/destiny narrative
  - (optional add-ons) reciprocity, liking, unity

Score: stickiness / dignity (pull without coercion)

---

## 5) Ritual Stack + container (environmental design)
Rituals are often the highest-leverage cohesion mechanism. Capture them explicitly.

**Questions (container)**
- What are the rules of engagement?
- How are breaks handled?
- How do they manage energy across long sessions?
- What’s the role of sharing / witness?

**Questions (ritual stack inventory)**
- What is the **initiation / threshold** ritual?
- What is the **daily micro-ritual** (2–10 min identity maintenance)?
- What is the **weekly gathering** ritual (primary cohesion engine)?
- What is the **challenge / ordeal** ritual (costly commitment)?
- What is the **confession / closure** ritual (debt release / reconciliation)?
- What is the **service / mission** ritual (outward contribution)?
- What is the **seasonal / annual** ritual (pilgrimage / holy day)?
- What is the **role elevation** ritual (member → guide → leader)?

**Questions (why it works)**
- Where is **synchrony** created (same action, same time)?
- What is the **cost** (time, effort, discomfort, vulnerability, money)?
- What is the **witnessing** mechanism (who sees you do it)?
- What does it make **predictable** (calendar = meaning)?
- What belief is reinforced **without argument** (embodied proof)?

**Extract**
- Room/stage pattern (or online equivalent)
- Cadence pattern (lecture → exercise → share → reset)
- Ritual stack table: ritual → cadence → cost → emotion produced → identity reinforced

Score: scalability / safety / cohesion power

---

## 6) Social architecture (belonging + peer force multipliers)
**Questions**
- How is community identity formed?
- What are roles (participant, graduate, volunteer, leader)?
- How do peers reinforce the worldview?
- What’s the boundary between support and pressure?

**Extract**
- Role ladder
- Community touchpoints (events, groups, rituals)

Score: retention lift / quality of relationships

---

## 7) Recruitment → Commitment progression (entry mechanics)
Map the full progression from first contact to advocacy.

**Questions (early stage)**
- What is the first touchpoint (conversation, content, invite, free event)?
- What is the low-threat “first yes”?
- What creates immediate warmth/attachment (attention, inclusion, affirmation)?

**Questions (progressive revelation)**
- What is revealed at stage 1 vs stage 5?
- What is reserved for “advanced” members?
- What language shifts happen as depth increases?

**Questions (commitment ladder)**
- What are the small commitments first (attendance, homework, volunteering, small donation)?
- What escalations appear later (time, money, relocation, major life decisions)?
- What makes leaving costly (social, identity, sunk cost, status loss)?

**Questions (advocacy loop)**
- When does a member become a recruiter/defender?
- What scripts/tools do recruiters use?

**Extract**
- Progression map (1→9): contact → attachment → beliefs → small commitments → identity → social reorientation → escalation → internalization → advocacy
- Funnel map (touchpoint → conversion)
- Top 10 objections + responses

Score: conversion efficiency / durability

## 10) Governance, finance, and professional-review hooks
(Designed to make professional audit easy.)

**Questions**
- Where does money flow? What are refund policies?
- What claims could create liability?
- What consent disclosures exist?
- What privacy/data policies exist?

**Extract**
- Claims inventory (what they promise)
- Policy inventory (refund, terms, privacy)
- Risk register (items to bring to counsel)

Score: audit-readiness

---

## 11) Measurement + proof
**Questions**
- What do they measure? (surveys, testimonials, retention)
- What is their evidence style? (stories vs stats)
- What are the real leading indicators of impact?

**Extract**
- KPI list + definitions
- Proof assets (case studies, stats, stories)

Score: truthfulness / usefulness

---

## 12) Brand narrative + myth (gravitas)
**Questions**
- What’s the origin story?
- What’s the enemy?
- What’s the hero’s journey?
- What symbols, aesthetics, language create weight?

**Extract**
- Narrative beats
- Symbol set

Score: gravitas / distinctiveness

## 13) Red flags / constraints
Capture anything that would be unacceptable for our system.

### High-control signature scan (cluster, not single-trait)
- Charismatic centralized leadership treated as uniquely authoritative?
- Totalizing ideology / only path framing?
- Strong insider–outsider boundary (language/symbols/persecution narratives)?
- Behavioral control (sleep, diet, relationships, sexuality, work, living arrangements)?
- Information control (discouraging criticism/outside media; internal sources only)?
- Gradual escalation of commitment (time/money/social ties) engineered stepwise?
- Identity fusion (new titles/names; constant group activity; dependency)?
- Ritual/emotional intensity used as reinforcement?
- Moral absolutism (good/evil; enlightened/ignorant; saved/doomed)?
- Exit penalties (shunning, threats, catastrophic predictions, social destruction)?
