# Movement Target Roster (v1)

Status: active corpus-planning roster.

Purpose: expand the corpus from a small set of movement case studies into a rounded DNA-extraction program.

Mission context: identify the characteristics, persuasion techniques, effective rituals and why they work, loyalty/charisma/zeal mechanisms, and the conditions under which movements become successful, irresistible, and interesting.

This roster is for **research coverage**, not yet for Apotheosis translation.

## 2) Why the corpus needs expansion

The current set is strong on:
- LGAT / transformation systems
- ordeal / discipline systems
- ritualized intimacy
- fitness-tribe mechanics

It is weaker on:
- religion / missionary structures
- recovery / testimony systems
- digital-native conspiracy / fandom spread
- premium but mostly non-cultic high-adhesion communities
- brand / product tribes beyond Apple
- movements that failed or decayed despite initial heat

Without those, the project risks learning only one subtype of movement DNA.

---

## 3) Expansion logic

The search should include **four kinds of targets**:

### A. High-adhesion cult-like / high-control cases
Used to study intensity, zeal, hierarchy, sacrifice, and coercive edge cases.

### B. High-adhesion but more socially legitimate cases
Used to isolate which mechanics create strong belonging without automatically producing overt abuse.

### C. Digital-native or memetic cases
Used to study how zeal and replication work without needing heavy in-person containers.

### D. Failed / fizzled / decayed cases
Used to learn what breaks movements even when early fascination exists.

## 4) Wave 1 expansion targets — highest value next passes

These are the next targets most likely to round out the spirit of the task.

### 4.1 Scientology
**Why add it:**
- one of the clearest modern systems for jargon, auditing ritual, doctrine ladder, secrecy, payment ladder, and totalizing worldview installation
- strong test case for how language + ritual + hierarchy + cosmology combine

**What it teaches best:**
- ladder architecture
- proprietary doctrine as identity software
- confession / auditing as both ritual and control
- strong founder legacy after founder absence

### 4.2 Alcoholics Anonymous (AA)
**Why add it:**
- durable, high-adhesion, non-premium, globally replicable transformation movement
- essential control case for ritual, testimony, sponsorship, confession, and repeated identity reinforcement without heavy charismatic-founder dependency in the current form

**What it teaches best:**
- testimony as identity maintenance
- sponsor structure
- repeated meeting ritual
- confession / surrender / service loops
- how belonging and accountability can be powerful without obvious glamour

### 4.3 LDS / Mormon missionary pipeline
**Why add it:**
- distributed evangelism system with strong rites, strong identity, family/social embedding, missionary discipline, and durable community reproduction

**What it teaches best:**
- missionary operator formation
- uniform identity markers
- doctrine portability
- life-stage capture
- family as retention infrastructure

### 4.4 Peloton or SoulCycle
**Why add it:**
- premium fitness tribe with charisma, ritualized class energy, instructor fandom, identity signaling, and recurring paid participation, but without the same extreme-control reputation as darker cases

**What it teaches best:**
- synchrony and emotional contagion
- parasocial charisma
- premium recurring ritual economics
- brand + instructor + tribe layering

### 4.5 Burning Man
**Why add it:**
- pilgrimage, temporary-city ritual, mythic annual calendar, identity signaling, artistic status economy, and strong participant evangelism

**What it teaches best:**
- annual holy-day architecture
- pilgrimage mechanics
- temporary liminal worlds
- symbolic capital / subcultural status

### 4.6 QAnon
**Why add it:**
- digital-native participatory mythology with decentralized zeal, lore-building, puzzle-solving, apocalyptic urgency, and social-media replication

**What it teaches best:**
- memetic zeal without formal central ritual spaces
- participatory doctrine construction
- digital conspiracy as identity and belonging engine
- self-radicalizing content loops

### 4.7 Tony Robbins
**Why add it:**
- stadium-scale transformation engine with live intensity, charisma, upsell ladders, testimonials, and highly engineered emotional pacing

**What it teaches best:**
- event theater
- charisma at scale
- emotional state manipulation through staging
- commercial ascension structure

### 4.8 The Secret / Law of Attraction
**Why add it:**
- already on backlog; strong memetic promise system, visualization ritual, product funnel, and belief reinforcement through selective proof logic

**What it teaches best:**
- low-friction belief spread
- ritualized visualization
- desire + destiny + manifestation framing
- consumer belief engine design

## 10) Operational note

The point of this roster is not to become infinite.

The point is to widen the sample enough that we can say with confidence:
- which mechanics are universal
- which are subtype-specific
- which create adhesion
- which create zeal
- which create abuse risk
- which help a movement scale
- which make it decay

---

## Bottom line

The current research set is strong but biased toward transformation / LGAT-style cases.

To round out the spirit of the task, the corpus now needs:
- religion
- recovery
- premium fitness charisma
- digital-native conspiracy / meme spread
- pilgrimage / annual-holy-day systems
- more legitimate brand / belonging controls
- and failed cases

That is how the project stops being “interesting cult notes” and becomes a real DNA map.
