## What this file does

This library keeps three things in one place:
1. the cross-case primitive name,
2. the best current source anchors for why it matters,
3. the on-disk status of any example card that makes the primitive concrete.

Use it to decide which mechanics are well-understood, which still need example cards, and which should be translated carefully later.

## Priority primitive queue (top 10)

### 1) Compact doctrine
Short branded distinctions compress a worldview into repeatable handles that are easy to remember, teach, and spread.
- Source anchors: `analysis/movement-master-synthesis-2026-03-18.md`; `landmark-forum.target-packet.md`
- Example-card status: live (`primitives/compact-doctrine.md`)

### 2) Threshold ritual
A clear entry ritual converts interest into participation and gives the prospect a felt crossing point.
- Source anchors: `analysis/movement-master-synthesis-2026-03-18.md`; `landmark-forum.target-packet.md`; `crossfit.target-packet.md`
- Example-card status: live (`primitives/threshold-ritual.md`)

### 3) Rehearsal + assignments that leave the room
The system becomes sticky when insight is pushed outward into behavior, outreach, declarations, or visible follow-through.
- Source anchors: `analysis/movement-master-synthesis-2026-03-18.md`; `landmark-forum.essence.md`; `movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md`
- Example-card status: live (`primitives/rehearsal-assignments-that-leave-the-room.md`)

### 4) Visible ladder / status economy
Belonging gets stronger when advancement has a legible carrier: rank, credential, role, stripe, day count, or public fluency.
- Source anchors: `analysis/movement-master-synthesis-2026-03-18.md`; `nxivm-esp.target-packet.md`; `scientology.target-packet.md`
- Example-card status: live (`primitives/visible-ladder-status-economy.md`)

### 5) Daily micro-rituals / measurable trace
A small recurring act plus a visible proof token keeps the practice alive between major milestones.
- Source anchors: `analysis/movement-master-synthesis-2026-03-18.md`; `75-hard.target-packet.md`; `crossfit.target-packet.md`
- Example-card status: live (`primitives/daily-micro-rituals-measurable-trace.md`)

### 6) Dyadic operator channels
Sponsors, coaches, guides, or mentors create a recurring one-to-one transmission lane that stabilizes behavior.
- Source anchors: `analysis/movement-master-synthesis-2026-03-18.md`; `alcoholics-anonymous.target-packet.md`; `onetaste.target-packet.md`
- Example-card status: live (`primitives/dyadic-operator-channels.md`)

### 7) Distributed node architecture
Replication gets stronger when the movement can spawn many local nodes while preserving a minimum shared structure.
- Source anchors: `analysis/movement-master-synthesis-2026-03-18.md`; `crossfit.target-packet.md`; `alcoholics-anonymous.target-packet.md`

### 8) Compressed timebox / flagship event
A high-intensity, time-bounded container can compress emotion, attention, and identity shift into a memorable crossing event.
- Source anchors: `landmark-forum.target-packet.md`; `nxivm-esp.target-packet.md`; `movement-unfiltered-reaudit-v1.md`

- Example-card status: live (`primitives/public-commitment-witness.md`)

## Working interpretation

The current corpus keeps rediscovering the same structural lesson: movements become durable when they make participation easy to repeat, progress easy to notice, and identity easy to witness. The most reusable primitives are not the loudest or most theatrical ones. They are the ones that:
- reduce activation energy,
- create recurring evidence,
- embed social witness without requiring spectacle,
- and give members a path from first contact to recurring return.

That is why the first live cards on disk skew toward recurring ritual and measurable trace. Those primitives sit closest to retention, behavior formation, and safe product translation.
