# Program over personality

Definition
--
Program over personality is the mechanic where a movement deliberately routes loyalty toward shared doctrine, repeatable practices, and service norms instead of letting authority pool entirely around one founder, star teacher, or local celebrity. The point is not the absence of leadership. The point is to keep the method portable and survivable when personalities change, fail, or disappear.

One-paragraph example
--
AA uses this mechanic explicitly through anonymity, group conscience, trusted-servant language, and the rule of principles before personalities: Bill W. and Dr. Bob matter historically, but the mature recovery engine is designed so sobriety belongs to the program, the room, and the sponsor/service lattice rather than to a permanent founder aura. Scientology shows the inverse design choice. It also solves the durability problem after the founder, but by fossilizing Hubbard as Source through scripture, trademarks, exact-application doctrine, and steward leadership. Same strategic problem, opposite answer. AA diffuses charisma into practice and fellowship. Scientology preserves charisma in canon and bureaucracy. The contrast makes the primitive visible: every durable movement has to decide whether authority lives in shared practice or in permanent founder centrality.

Why it matters
--
- Prevents the whole system from bottlenecking through one magnetic personality.
- Makes local replication easier because members can trust the method without needing direct founder contact.
- Lowers the risk that status theater or founder access becomes the main proof of truth.
- In healthy forms, keeps rooms more resilient when leaders leave, age, fail, or become controversial.
- Clarifies whether the movement is building a repeatable culture or a personality cult with logistics.

Examples (evidence anchors)
--
- Alcoholics Anonymous packet: anonymity, group conscience, trusted-servant framing, and principles-before-personalities are treated as core anti-charisma safeguards that let AA survive founder absence without becoming founder-dependent (`shared/docs/targets/alcoholics-anonymous.target-packet.md`).
- Alcoholics Anonymous packet: the authority section explicitly frames AA as an anti-charismatic high-adhesion system that dampens celebrity and distributes legitimacy across literature, rooms, sponsors, and service roles (`shared/docs/targets/alcoholics-anonymous.target-packet.md`).
- Scientology packet: Hubbard remains structurally central after death through scripture, exact-application doctrine, trademark control, and RTC/CSI orthodoxy enforcement, making it one of the clearest founder-preservation systems in the corpus (`shared/docs/targets/scientology.target-packet.md`).
- Scientology packet: the founder-to-institution durability section shows how charisma can be embedded into canon, procedure, and bureaucracy so the institution survives without diluting founder authority (`shared/docs/targets/scientology.target-packet.md`).

Shadow risk
--
This primitive can fail in two directions. One failure mode is founder immunity: the movement says the method matters, but in practice the founder or inner circle becomes unquestionable, so doctrine hardens into anti-correction lock-in. The opposite failure mode is hidden power: a movement loudly rejects charisma, but real authority still concentrates informally in sponsors, elders, or scene leaders without enough transparency. Anti-charisma language can reduce ego inflation, but it can also make power harder to see and therefore harder to challenge.

Safe translation
--
If a product or community borrows this mechanic, make the operating method explicit and teachable without personal mystique. Use rotating roles, shared language, and visible service criteria so authority is earned through competence and stewardship rather than aura or closeness to the founder. Keep founder narrative as origin story, not permanent epistemic veto. The useful mechanic is durable trust in the program. The dangerous mechanic is pretending the program matters while quietly building a throne behind it.
