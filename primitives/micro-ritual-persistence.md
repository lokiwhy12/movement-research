# Primitive: Micro-ritual persistence (example card)

Mechanic: Short, repeatable micro-ritual performed daily to convert ephemeral intention into observable trace (1–3 minutes). Example: 3x breath + write one concrete next action and timestamp it.

Lifecycle slot: Early onboarding / daily practice loop — used to convert curiosity into early habit signal and visible progress.

Example practice: After morning calibration, perform the 3-breath anchor, write one micro-action (e.g., "Send draft to Herald") and mark completion in the daily log. Repeat for 7 days to establish a measurable trace.

Shadow risk: Ritualization without purpose can become checkbox compliance or exploit attention for forced escalation. Avoid making the ritual the purpose; tie to explicit, measurable outcomes.

Safe translation: Present as a short operational experiment: "Do this for 7 mornings and record whether it made finishing the task 3x easier." Keep optional and reversible.
