# Portable canon / drift-control architecture

Definition
--
Portable canon / drift-control architecture is the mechanic where a movement gives local rooms, operators, or chapters a common body of language, readings, procedures, and governance cues so the experience stays recognizably "the same thing" as it spreads. The key variable is not whether drift is prevented; every movement tries to prevent drift. The real question is how. Healthier forms use a bounded canon plus local judgment, while darker forms turn canon into exact-application enforcement where deviation itself becomes a moral or spiritual threat.

One-paragraph example
--
AA solves this with a light but durable package: the Steps, Traditions, meeting formats, core readings, sponsorship norms, group-conscience language, and trusted-servant governance give local groups a recognizable operating system without erasing autonomy. Scientology solves the same replication problem through a much harder architecture: Hubbard as Source, massive scripture compressed into exact formulas, RTC trademark control, standard tech, and the idea that precise application is itself a test of legitimacy. Same strategic challenge, opposite posture. AA uses shared canon to keep rooms legible while leaving space for local stewardship. Scientology uses canon to narrow deviation, preserve orthodoxy, and make reform read like corruption.

Why it matters
--
- Lets a movement scale beyond founder proximity without dissolving into unrelated local variants.
- Preserves trust because newcomers can expect a recognizable experience from room to room.
- Makes operator training easier because there is a known baseline to inherit.
- Clarifies what is essential doctrine versus what can adapt to local conditions.
- Exposes a central tradeoff in movement design: resilience through bounded consistency versus lock-in through hard orthodoxy.

Examples (evidence anchors)
--
- Alcoholics Anonymous packet: local groups are autonomous, but the Steps, Traditions, common readings, sponsor norms, anonymity, group conscience, and trusted-servant framing create a shared operating system that keeps rooms recognizable across geographies (`shared/docs/targets/alcoholics-anonymous.target-packet.md`).
- Alcoholics Anonymous packet: the governance section explicitly shows how common literature and traditions create consistency without turning leaders into governors, preserving both portability and anti-charisma norms (`shared/docs/targets/alcoholics-anonymous.target-packet.md`).
- Scientology packet: the movement is organized around founder scripture, exact-application language, RTC orthodoxy control, and trademark-backed claims of authentic technology, creating one of the clearest high-control drift-prevention systems in the corpus (`shared/docs/targets/scientology.target-packet.md`).
- Scientology packet: the founder-to-institution durability section shows how charisma is embedded into text, ritual procedure, and bureaucracy so continuity is preserved by narrowing permissible variation (`shared/docs/targets/scientology.target-packet.md`).

Shadow risk
--
This primitive fails in two different ways. The soft failure is drift collapse: local rooms become so interpretive that the movement stops feeling coherent, quality falls, and identity fragments into subcultures. The hard failure is anti-correction lock-in: canon becomes so sacred that evidence, dissent, or adaptation are treated as betrayal. In that version, drift control stops protecting the method and starts protecting power. The more exactness is moralized, the easier it becomes to blame members, operators, or critics instead of updating a broken process.

Safe translation
--
If a product or community borrows this mechanic, publish a minimal canon: core language, core rituals, non-negotiable safety rules, and a short explanation of what locals may adapt. Version the canon openly instead of pretending it fell from the sky. Use lightweight certification or review for critical practices, but keep feedback loops real so correction is possible. The useful mechanic is recognizable quality across rooms. The dangerous mechanic is turning consistency into frozen dogma or founder veto.
